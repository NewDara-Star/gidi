# Gidi catalogue: education, sport, leisure and nightlife places

This catalogue extends `docs/look/research-buildings.md` and `docs/look/research-street.md`. It does not repeat their walls, gates, roofs, generators, umbrellas, plastic chairs or crowd tables. It reuses them by name: "compound wall (buildings §4)", "event canopy (street §6.3)".

Rule zero: every real place below is only an example of a pattern. Gidi names such as "National Bowl", "Freedom Ground" and "Odds Palace" are our own inventions. Brands are described generically.

Tags: [src] means the number comes from a listed source. [est] means it is our estimate from photos, typical Nigerian practice and building norms.

## 1. Public primary school

**Looks like:**
- A sandy, unpaved yard of 0.2 to 1 ha [est] enclosed by a compound wall (buildings §4). There is one wide metal gate painted the school colour with the school name stencilled above it.
- One to four single-storey classroom blocks in a line or an L, built in the 1960s to 80s. Each has a deep verandah corridor on one side, square concrete columns, and an asbestos or zinc low-pitch roof.
- Newer rehabilitated blocks are two or three storeys with a flat or hipped long-span roof and a staircase tower. Lagos reports rehabilitating more than 4,000 classrooms [src blueprint].
- Walls are painted in two bands: a dark dado about 1.0 to 1.2 m high and a light upper wall. The walls carry painted murals: an alphabet, a map of Nigeria, "Say No to Cultism", hand-washing, and fruit.
- At school-run time the gate is jammed with parents, okadas, kekes and hawkers. A flagpole stands in the yard.

**Kit parts:**
- `classroom_block_old`: 30-45 x 7-8 m [est], one storey, 3.3 m eaves, verandah 2.0-2.4 m deep, 4-6 rooms of 7x7 to 9x7 m. The roof is asbestos `#8F8F88` or zinc at 10-15 degrees, with the overhang on the verandah side. Windows are louvre blades or open hollow-block screens (breeze block, 0.2 m cells). Doors are timber or steel, one per room.
- `classroom_block_new`: 2-3 storeys, 36-48 x 9 m [est], RC frame, external corridor with a steel rail, end stair tower, aluminium sliding windows, flat parapet or long-span hip roof in blue `#2E5FA8` or green.
- `assembly_yard`: open sand or patchy concrete 20x30 m [est]. Flagpole 6-8 m tall, a white steel tube with a Nigerian flag (green-white-green) and sometimes a state flag. Raised concrete assembly stage 4x3 m, 0.6 m high.
- `school_signboard`: two steel posts 2.5-3 m with a 2.4 x 1.2 m painted board. It reads "[NAME] PRIMARY SCHOOL, [Area], LAGOS STATE UNIVERSAL BASIC EDUCATION BOARD" style text with a crest. White ground, blue/red lettering.
- `pit_latrine_block` or toilet block 4x3 m at the back. Borehole tank stand 4-6 m with a 2,000 L plastic tank (buildings §3.3).
- `mural_panel` decals: alphabet strips, Nigeria map, cartoon children.
- Interior (if visible): long wooden two-seater desk-benches 1.2 m, a blackboard painted black `#1E2420` on the front wall.

**Colours:**
- Dado bands: blue `#2E5FA8`, green `#2E7D46`, maroon `#7A1F2B` or brown `#6B4A2E`.
- Upper wall: cream `#EFE6C8`, white `#F1EFE6` or pale yellow `#E9D98A`.
- Sand yard `#C9AE80`, laterite patches `#A0522D`.
- Doors and window frames: the dado colour or black.

**Materials:** Sandcrete block with render, RC columns, asbestos or zinc roof, steel gate, timber furniture.

**Variants:**
- Old single-storey L or bar, 55% [est].
- Rehabilitated 2-3 storey block on an old site, 30% [est].
- Mission-origin school with a gable-front block and a church nearby (names like "St. …" or "Ansar-Ud-Deen"), 15% [est].
- Many sites host two or three schools sharing one yard: "[Name] Primary School I, II, III" on stacked boards [est, a common Lagos pattern].

**Condition:**
- New: clean paint and a sharp signboard.
- Worn (the default): dado scuffed, rain streaks below the eaves, a faded signboard.
- Decayed: missing louvres, a sagging asbestos roof with holes, sand drifted onto the verandah, a goat in the yard.
- Collapsed or abandoned block: roof gone, walls standing, children's play area. Classroom collapses do make the news [src channels].

**Props and life:**
- Day: uniformed pupils (street §4.2 colours) and a morning assembly in rows. Hawkers at the gate sell puff-puff, sweets and "pure water" sachets. A gateman sits on a stool. Teachers' cars are parked inside.
- 13:30-15:00: a pickup crush of okadas and kekes.
- Night: empty, a dark yard. Sometimes a church rents a classroom, or young men play football in the yard.

**SELECT BY:** `kind=school_primary`. Wealth low-mid. Dense mainland and sprawl most often; old island in mission variants; at the edge of the waterfront. A back street or collector road, rarely an arterial. Plot >= 0.2 ha. Condition = district condition.

**Gidi mapping:** public primary schools (sandy yard, painted walls, school-run).

## 2. Model comprehensive / public secondary school

**Looks like:**
- A bigger version of the primary site, 1-6 ha [est], with several classroom blocks set around a quadrangle. It has a sports field (sand or patchy grass), a laboratory block, an administration block with a staff car park, and sometimes a hall.
- Model colleges are boarding schools with dormitories and a dining hall, e.g. a 182-bed female hostel and 12-classroom block opened at one model college [src radionigerialagos/vanguard search].
- New prototype blocks have 18 classrooms over three storeys with special rooms (lab, ICT, library, art, home economics) and a sick bay [src radionigerialagos Elemoro]. These sit beside older decaying 1970s blocks.
- Often several schools share one site: "[X] Junior Grammar School" plus "[X] Senior Grammar School".

**Kit parts:**
- `classroom_block_old` (as §1), plus a two-storey 1970s variant with an external open stair and a balcony corridor.
- `prototype_block_18`: 3 storeys at 3.4 m, 50 x 10 m [est], with a corridor façade, central stair, white render with coloured spandrel bands (school colour) and a long-span roof.
- `admin_block`: 1-2 storeys, 20x10 m [est], with a porch and a "PRINCIPAL" sign. The Nigerian and Lagos flags on two poles in front.
- `dormitory`: 2-3 storeys, 40x10 m [est], with rows of small windows and laundry on the railings.
- `dining_hall` or assembly hall: a 30x15 m [est] shed with a steel truss, a long-span roof and open sides or high windows.
- `sports_field`: 90x55 m sand/grass football pitch with steel goalposts; a 400 m track only in big schools (usually just a worn oval). Basketball court 28x15 m concrete with rusted hoops.
- `gate_arch`: a concrete gate arch 6-8 m wide, 4-5 m high, with raised letters of the school name and motto and a crest.

**Colours:** As §1, plus prototype white `#F2F2EE` with green `#2E7D46`, blue `#2E5FA8` or maroon bands. Track and paths laterite red `#A0522D`.

**Variants:**
- Day comprehensive, 70%.
- Model college (boarding, larger, fenced high), 15%.
- Old federal/unity-college or famous-old-boys school with colonial-era blocks (arched verandahs, red tile or iron roof), 15%, old island and civic only.

**Condition:**
- Typical mix: one new prototype block among worn old blocks.
- Decayed: a broken perimeter wall, squatters' traders against the outside wall, an overgrown field, burnt-out labs.
- Alumni complaints about deplorable model colleges are reported [src nannews].

**Props and life:**
- Students in uniform: senior boys in white shirts and trousers, girls in pinafores.
- Morning assembly; a "Lateness" queue at the gate; football on the field after 14:00.
- Exam-season "WAEC" banners and church/rental use at weekends.
- Night: dark, with one security light and a guard.

**SELECT BY:** `kind=school_secondary`. Plot >= 1 ha. Any district except the elite core. Arterial or collector frontage. Boarding (model) variant in sprawl and at the city edge (Ikorodu/Badagry type). Old-colonial variant in old island and civic.

**Gidi mapping:** model comprehensive / public secondary schools.

## 3. Private and international schools

**Looks like:**
- **House-school (most common):** a converted 2-storey family house or a purpose-built 3-4 storey block on a 600-1,500 m² plot. The front wall is painted bright, with cartoon murals (sun, ABC, crayons) and a large painted name: "Heritage Nursery & Primary School" style. There is a small concrete forecourt with a plastic slide and swing and a branded school bus.
- **Mid private college:** a walled campus 0.5-3 ha with 3-4 storey blocks and a coloured façade (two-colour block bands, a school crest on the parapet). There is a car park for parents and a gatehouse.
- **International school (rare):** 2-10 ha [est] campus, low 2-3 storey modern buildings in white and glass with brise-soleil, a covered walkway, an Astroturf pitch, a 25 m pool, an auditorium and a big car park. Guarded, with boom barriers. Names in Gidi such as "Heritage International School" and "Lagoon International School".

**Kit parts:**
- `house_school`: reuse the house mesh (buildings §1.2/1.3) with a school signboard, a mural decal set, a forecourt playground (slide 2.5 m, swing frame 2 m, merry-go-round) and a roof-edge banner "ADMISSION IN PROGRESS".
- `school_bus`: a 14-18 seat minibus or a 30-seat coaster bus painted yellow `#F2C200` or the school colour with the school name on the side.
- `intl_campus`: separate blocks of 2-3 storeys, 40-60 x 12 m, white `#F4F3EF` render plus a dark grey frame `#3A3D42`, glass curtain panels, aluminium louvres, a covered walkway (steel posts, 3 m wide polycarbonate roof), an auditorium box 30x20x10 m, a pool, an Astroturf pitch (green `#3E8E41`, white lines) and a running track (rubber red `#B0413E` or blue).
- `gatehouse` (elite gate kit from buildings §4) with a boom barrier and branded signage.

**Colours:**
- House-school: walls in primary yellow `#F5C518`, sky blue `#4FA3E0`, lime `#8CC63F` or pink `#E86A9A`. Signs in white with red/blue lettering.
- International: white, grey and the school's accent colour (navy `#1F3A5F`, green, maroon).

**Variants by share [est]:**
- House-school, 60%.
- Mid private college, 30%.
- International campus, 5%.
- Faith school (Christian or Islamic name, crest with a cross or crescent), 5%.

**Condition:**
- House-schools fade fast: murals peel and the sign is repainted on top of an old name.
- International campuses stay new.
- Failed house-school: the sign remains and the shutters are closed, "TO LET".

**Props and life:**
- 07:00-08:00 and 14:00-16:00: SUVs queue at the gate and block the street, with drivers waiting and nannies.
- A school bus fleet parked in a row. Graduation-day canopies (§23).
- Night: international campus floodlit pitch; house-schools dark.

**SELECT BY:**
- `kind=school_private`. House-school: any district, mid wealth, back streets, very frequent (several per block in sprawl like Alimosho/Lekki).
- College: sprawl and elite edges, collector road.
- International: elite (Ikoyi/VI type) or sprawl (Lekki type), wealth high, large plot, near the lagoon edge for the "Lagoon" one.

**Gidi mapping:** private and international schools ("Heritage International School", "Lagoon International School").

## 4. Floating / stilt school on the lagoon

**Real pattern:** Makoko-type floating school. A triangular A-frame 10 m high on a 10 x 10 m base, three levels, about 220 m² built area. Timber and bamboo, floating on about 250-256 recycled plastic barrels. Level 1 open play deck, level 2 two classrooms for about 60 pupils, top level a workshop/open space [src AKDN, architectmagazine, dezeen]. It collapsed in a 2016 storm; this informs our "decayed" state [src common knowledge, est]. Do NOT copy that design. Build our own "School on Stilts".

**Gidi design (our own):**
- **Looks like:** a two-storey stilt school on a timber platform at the edge of the stilt village. It has a wide deck, a bamboo screen façade and a pitched zinc roof with deep eaves. Canoes are moored to its ladder. A painted board reads "School on Stilts". Children arrive by canoe.
- **Kit parts:**
  - `stilt_platform`: 14x10 m deck at 1.5-2.0 m above water, on 0.15-0.2 m timber piles at 1.5-2 m centres (matches stilt houses, buildings §8).
  - `classroom_box`: 10x7 m, walls of plank and bamboo slats, window openings with wooden shutters.
  - Upper floor a 10x7 m room or an open covered deck.
  - Roof: zinc gable `#9DA3A6` rusting, with a 1.0 m overhang.
  - `barrel_raft` variant: a 10x10 m raft on blue barrels `#1F5FA8` in a 16x16 grid, with an A-frame or hip roof.
  - Ladder and canoe jetty, painted signboard 2x0.8 m, a flagpole, a rain-collection drum.
- **Colours:** weathered timber `#7A6650` to `#5A4B3C`, bamboo `#C9A86A`, blue barrels `#1F5FA8`, rust `#8A4B2A`. Murals in bright paint on plank walls.
- **Variants:** stilt-on-piles 60%, floating barrel raft 40% [est].
- **Condition:**
  - New: bright timber and a sharp sign.
  - Worn: greyed, sagging planks.
  - Abandoned: listing or partly sunk, roof gone, a frame left standing in the water.
- **Props and life:** dugout canoes, children in uniform in canoes, laundry, a teacher with a bell. At night, dark apart from a kerosene lamp.
- **SELECT BY:** `kind=school_floating`, waterfront district only, one per city, at the outer edge of the stilt village.
- **Gidi mapping:** School on Stilts.

## 5. Golf club and course (Ikoyi-type)

**Real pattern:** the oldest Lagos club (formed in 1938 by merging a European club and a golf club) sits on about 185 ha. It has an 18-hole parkland course, par 71, plus sports and social facilities [src Wikipedia, top100golfcourses]. Gidi's version is smaller and invented.

**Looks like:**
- Flat parkland fairways of mown grass between belts of mature trees (rain trees, palms, almonds), with drainage canals and ponds.
- A long, low 1950s-70s clubhouse with deep verandahs, a pitched roof and white walls. A car park full of SUVs with drivers.
- All of it is behind a perimeter wall (2.5-3 m, white or cream with a dark band). Gate kit with guards.
- Caddies in bibs carry bags. Golfers in white/pastel. Grounds staff use push mowers and cutlasses.

**Kit parts:**
- `fairway`: 30-50 m wide, 250-450 m long [est]. Rough strips 10-20 m. Tees 10x15 m. Greens 500-700 m² [est] oval, slightly raised, with a flag pin 2.1 m tall (yellow or red flag).
- `bunker`: kidney shapes 50-200 m² of sand `#D9C9A0`.
- Water hazards: ponds and canals with algae `#4E6B3A` and a concrete edge.
- `clubhouse`: 60x20 m [est], 1-2 storeys. White `#F2F0E8` walls, a red-brown tile or dark long-span roof `#5A3A2A`, a 3-4 m verandah with white columns, an awning terrace bar, a pro-shop, a scoreboard board.
- `caddie_shed`: open shed 10x5 m with benches; a golf-cart park.
- `perimeter_wall` (buildings §4 elite) 2.7 m with razor wire; a gatehouse with a barrier.
- Tee markers, yardage posts (white 150, yellow 100 etc.), a small rain shelter hut 3x3 m with a thatch or zinc roof.

**Colours:**
- Dry-season fairway `#9BA65A`, rainy-season fairway `#5E8C3A`, greens `#4F8A3B`, rough `#7C8A45`.
- Clubhouse white/cream with a green trim `#2E5E3A`.

**Variants:** Only one in Gidi. Its "more complex" form has extra sports (squash, tennis, pool and polo field nearby) [est].

**Condition:**
- Well kept on the course.
- Wear on the clubhouse: a 1970s look with AC units on the walls and newer annexes.
- Dry season: browned fairways.

**Props and life:**
- Early morning golfers, caddies, mowing crews, and egrets on the fairways.
- Weekend lunches at the terrace. Drivers wait under trees in parked cars.
- Night: the clubhouse is lit, with an event canopy for weddings and an AGM; the course is dark.

**SELECT BY:** `kind=golf_course`. Elite (Ikoyi type), lagoon-adjacent, low-lying land, very large parcel. One per city.

**Gidi mapping:** golf club and course (fairways, greens, bunkers, clubhouse, caddies, perimeter wall); polo grounds if wanted can sit beside it.

## 6. Estate club, clubhouse, pools, courts, polo

**Looks like:**
- In a gated estate (elite or Lekki-type sprawl): a clubhouse of 1-2 storeys facing a pool, a gym, a tennis court and a children's play area. All behind the estate gate.
- In individual elite compounds: a small pool (often visible from above as a turquoise rectangle) with pavers, loungers and a pool hut.
- Public basketball and tennis courts: fenced concrete courts on civic land or in schools, worn and with no nets.
- Polo: a large flat grass field (about 270x145 m [est, standard polo]) with a white board fence, small pavilion stands, a stable block and horse boxes. Polo is a real Lagos elite pastime [est].

**Kit parts:**
- `estate_clubhouse`: 25x15 m, modern box with a glass front, flat roof and a pergola terrace. Grey/white `#E9E7E2`/`#4A4D52`.
- `pool`: compound pool 8x4 to 12x5 m; club pool 25x12.5 m. Water `#2FB8C6`, tile edge white, deck pavers `#D8CDB8`. Loungers, umbrellas.
- `tennis_court`: 36.6x18.3 m overall [standard], green `#2F7D5B` or blue `#2E5FA8` acrylic with a red `#B0413E` surround, 3-4 m chain-link fence, lights on 8 m poles.
- `basketball_court`: 28x15 m concrete, hoops 3.05 m [standard], backboard often broken. Public courts have faded lines and graffiti.
- `polo_field`: 270x145 m grass, white boundary boards 0.25 m high, goalposts 3 m tall at 7.3 m apart [standard], a pavilion stand of 3-4 rows under a canopy, stable rows 3x3 m boxes.

**Variants:** compound pool in elite houses 30-50% [est, Banana Island type higher]; estate club in 40% of gated estates [est]; public courts 1-2 per civic district.

**Condition:**
- New: bright water.
- Worn: tiles stained, algae edges, faded court paint.
- Abandoned/half-built: an empty pool with green water, a court with cracked surface and weeds.

**Props and life:** lifeguards rare; staff in uniform; evening tennis under lights; a basketball pickup game at sunset in public courts; Sunday polo spectators with hats and canopies; horse grooms.

**SELECT BY:** wealth high (elite) or upper-mid gated estates (sprawl, Lekki type). Pools appear by `wealth >= 0.75` on house plots. Public courts in civic and dense mainland near stadiums and schools.

**Gidi mapping:** estate club and clubhouse, swimming pools in compounds, tennis/basketball courts, polo grounds.

## 7. Decaying 1970s national stadium ("National Bowl")

**Real pattern:** the Surulere stadium was commissioned on 4 December 1972 [src sportsvillagesquare]. It had a capacity of about 55,000, later 45,000 after the 1999 refit [src sportskeeda]. It has been neglected since about 2002 [src sportskeeda]. Its four floodlight towers are 62.18 m tall; one collapsed in 2023 and the stadium was closed [src sportsvillagesquare, VON]. Squatters and "area boys" occupied it, the government served eviction notices and demolished illegal structures [src guardian.ng]. Its compound became a commercial space while the main bowl decayed [src thisdaylive 2026]. It was used for church crusades [src search summary].

**Looks like:**
- An oval concrete bowl about 250 x 200 m outside [est]. Raked grey concrete terraces, a cantilevered roof canopy only over the main (west) stand, with the opposite stand open.
- Four very tall lattice floodlight masts at the corners with a tilted light head; one bent or fallen.
- A running track around the pitch, faded and broken. The pitch is overgrown or brown.
- Outside: a huge compound with indoor sports halls, a swimming pool complex, car parks and plazas now filled with traders' sheds, mechanics, churches, bus parks and squatter shacks.

**Kit parts:**
- `bowl_stands`: 25-35 rows of terraces [est] at 0.8 m tread x 0.4 m rise. Bowl top 20-25 m above ground [est]. Seats: plastic bucket seats in national green `#1E7B3A` and white on the main stand (mostly broken or missing), bare concrete steps elsewhere.
- `main_stand_roof`: a steel or concrete cantilever 20-25 m deep [est], with rusted sheeting and gaps.
- `vomitory` openings every 15-20 m, ramps and external stair towers on the outer façade. The outer façade has raked concrete ribs or columns at 6-8 m spacing [est].
- `floodlight_mast`: 62 m [src] lattice tower 3-4 m square at the base, with a head frame 10x6 m holding 60-100 lamps. Rust `#7A4A2E` and faded paint `#9A9C98`. Variant: collapsed onto the stand.
- `scoreboard`: a dead old board at one end, 15x6 m.
- `track`: 8 lanes, red `#9A4B3A` faded to pink-grey, patched with sand.
- `pitch`: 105x68 m, patchy `#7F8F4A`, with bare sand goalmouths.
- Compound: `indoor_hall` (dome or barrel roof, 60x40 m), an empty `olympic_pool` 50x25 m with green water, `squatter_shacks` (plank and zinc, 3x3 m, buildings §5/§8), trader container shops (buildings §6.2), parked tankers and buses.

**Colours:** raw concrete `#9C9A92` stained to `#6E6B63` with black water streaks `#3A3A36`; algae green `#5F6B3A` on terraces; rust `#7A4A2E`.

**Variants / condition (one object, but pick the state):**
- 1970s new (flashback only): white concrete, green seats, lit masts.
- Worn (1999 refit, about 2005): repainted, seats intact on the main stand.
- Decayed (the default): seats missing, weeds in the joints, one mast down, roof sheets gone, graffiti, pools of water.
- Squatted: shacks under the stand and in the vomitories, laundry on the railings, cook-fire smoke, goats.
- Under renovation: scaffolding, a tower crane, new seats on a third of the bowl.

**Props and life:**
- Day: footballers training on the track; athletes on the outer track; traders, mechanics; evangelical crusade canopies on the car park; danfos at a bus park by the gate.
- Night: dark bowl, cook-fires and a few bulbs under the stands; the street outside lit by stalls.

**SELECT BY:** `kind=stadium_national`, one per city, dense mainland (Surulere type), beside an expressway. Condition default "decayed+squatted".

**Gidi mapping:** National Bowl and its stands. The neighbouring hotel (§14) and viewing centres (§20) cluster nearby.

## 8. Football pitches (sand pitches and street pitches)

**Looks like:**
- Any open lot, school field or the space under a flyover becomes a pitch. Bare sand or laterite with grass only at the edges. Goalposts are welded steel pipes or bamboo, usually without nets.
- Weekend "novelty matches" with plastic chairs and a canopy for officials. Kids play barefoot.
- Community "mini-stadium": a fenced pitch with a low concrete stand on one side (3-6 rows) and a painted gate.

**Kit parts:**
- `pitch_sand`: 60-90 x 40-55 m [est], sand `#C9AE80`/laterite `#A0522D`, painted lines absent or chalk.
- `goalpost`: full size 7.32 x 2.44 m [standard], pipe 0.1 m, white paint flaking to rust. Small-sided 3x2 m. Bamboo variant.
- `mini_stand`: 20-40 m long, 3-6 steps, painted in club colours, with a zinc shelter on poles.
- `street_goal`: two stones or two tyres.

**Variants:** open-lot pitch 50%, school field 30%, under-bridge pitch 10%, mini-stadium 10% [est].

**Condition:** a fresh line-marking and nets for a tournament; otherwise worn; in the rainy season flooded puddles; the lot gets built on (half-built block on the pitch edge).

**Props and life:** matches 16:00-19:00 weekdays and all weekend; jerseys in club colours; spectators on the wall; a pure-water seller; a coach with a whistle. Night: dark, or a single floodlight on a pole from a viewing centre.

**SELECT BY:** any open plot > 2,000 m² without a building; school fields; spaces under flyovers (dense mainland). Probability higher in dense mainland and sprawl.

**Gidi mapping:** football pitches (sand, goalposts); viewing centres are §20.

## 9. Protest / heritage park with a stage ("Freedom Ground")

**Real pattern:** a former colonial prison on Lagos Island converted into a memorial and leisure park. It has an open-air stage and amphitheatre over the old prison yard, food courts, ponds, fountains, statues, a museum and gallery [src Wikipedia, discoverlagos]. A separate 50,000-capacity ceremonial ground on the old race course is used for parades and concerts [src culturetrip]. Gidi combines these as "Freedom Ground", a civic stage park with a protest history.

**Looks like:**
- A 2-4 ha [est] walled park in old island or civic. Brick and stone ruin walls kept as features, an amphitheatre with stepped seats around a stage, lawns, ponds and a food court of kiosks.
- An adjoining parade ground: a big open paved/grass rectangle with a covered grandstand on one side, flagpoles, and a monument.
- On protest days: crowds, placards, flags, police vans at the gate.

**Kit parts:**
- `amphitheatre`: semicircle radius 15-25 m, 8-12 tiered steps 0.45 m high [est], in concrete or brick.
- `stage`: 15x10 m, 1.2 m high, with a steel truss canopy (white membrane or long-span) 8 m tall and speaker towers.
- `ruin_wall`: brick `#8E4A35` remnant walls 3-4 m tall with arches.
- `pond_fountain`: 10-20 m pond with a jet.

**Props and life:** Friday-Sunday; crowds in matching aso-ebi; money spraying; MCs; traffic jam; police; after midnight the band; next day canopies being taken down and stacked on a truck.

**SELECT BY:** halls on main roads in dense mainland, sprawl, civic; street canopies on any residential street on Friday-Saturday events, with a probability by district (higher in dense mainland and sprawl).

**Gidi mapping:** event halls and party tents (owambe canopies, rented chairs).
