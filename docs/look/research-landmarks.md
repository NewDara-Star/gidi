# Lagos landmarks as patterns: a catalogue for Gidi's invented landmarks

Rule zero applies here more than anywhere else. A landmark is the thing a player will recognise, so **every Gidi landmark must be an original design**. Real Lagos structures appear below only as references for a *type*: what makes that kind of thing striking, how big it is, what it is made of and how it ages. Each item ends with 1-2 invented Gidi designs that share the spirit but not the silhouette of any real one.

Builds on `docs/look/research-buildings.md` (church 10.5, mosque 10.6, palace 10.16, bank tower 10.1) and `docs/look/research-street.md` (pedestrian bridge 2.6, billboards 2.7). This file does not repeat those. It covers the landmark-scale versions.

Tags: [src] = from a listed source; [est] = my estimate from photos and general knowledge. Hex colours are always [est].

Gidi districts referenced (from `web/districts_info.json`): hub (Oshodi/Ojuelegba/Surulere), capital (Ikeja), railside (Agege/Alimosho), foodgate (Kosofe/Ojota), port (Apapa), oldisland (Lagos Island), heights (Ikoyi/VI/Banana Island), frontier (Lekki/Ibeju-Lekki), watertown (Makoko/Bariga), oldshore (Ikorodu/Epe), border (Badagry), maze, jungle, bazaar, estate.

## Global rules for Gidi landmarks [est]

- **One silhouette, one colour, one light.** Every real Lagos icon reads at distance by a single shape (the hat, the pylon, the dome), a dominant colour (white, gold, green) and one night light (floodlight, crown LED, flare). Give each Gidi landmark exactly one of each and keep the rest plain.
- **Decay is the default** for anything public built 1960-1990: stained concrete `#8E8A80` with black run-off streaks `#3A3631` under every ledge, rust blooms `#8A4B2A`, weeds on top surfaces, missing panels, a hand-painted sign added later. Private and 2010+ landmarks stay clean.
- **Life always colonises the base.** Hawkers, a parked danfo, a kiosk, a banner, a lone security man. Even the cleanest tower has a generator and a gateman.
- **Height budget** (Gidi skyline): flare stack 120 m, civic tower 140-160 m, Bank Row 80-130 m, Cable Bridge pylon 85 m, stadium floodlight 55-60 m, cathedral tower 45 m, minarets 35-45 m, cultural hall 32 m.

## 1. 1970s national stadium bowl, decaying → **National Bowl**

**Reference pattern.** National Stadium, Surulere: designed c.1970 by Isaac Fola-Alade, opened 4 Dec 1972 for the 1973 All-Africa Games; 55,000 seats at opening, cut to 45,000 in 1999; record crowd 85,000 in 1980 [src Wikipedia]. Olympic pool (closed 1999) and a multipurpose indoor arena on the site [src]. Last national-team game in 2004; then abandoned, "taken over by area boys and squatters", used for church crusades, weddings and rallies [src Wikipedia, Leadership]. In April 2023 a floodlight mast collapsed; the ministry ordered the three other masts pulled down [src VON/Gazette]. Weeds, rodents, reptiles, trash in every corner; an expert claim that 40% of the stands could cave in [src Leadership]. Renovation from 2022-24 [src].

**Looks like:**
- A huge oval of grey-brown concrete, open to the sky, about 25-30 m tall at the rim [est], rising out of a ring of car park, bush and shacks.
- Outside: a row of slanted concrete raker beams (the zig-zag stepped supports of the seating) showing as giant sawtooth fins, with access ramps spiralling up.
- One main stand with a cantilever roof (steel or concrete shell) on the west side; the rest open terraces.
- Four lattice-steel floodlight masts at the corners, 50-60 m tall [est], each with a rectangular head of lamps; one bent or fallen.
- A blank scoreboard frame on the open end; faded national green-white paint on the parapet.

**Kit parts:**
- `bowl_ring`: oval plan 260 x 200 m outside, 105 x 68 m pitch + 8-lane track (400 m oval) inside [est]; generated as 24-36 segments so segments can be swapped for decayed variants.
- `raker_fin`: slanted beam 1.0 m wide, 1.5-2.5 m deep, 28 m long, slope 30-35 deg, at 7.5 m centres [est]; step seats 0.8 m tread x 0.4 m riser on top.
- `vomitory`: 3 m wide tunnel mouth every 3rd bay, dark interior.
- `ramp_spiral`: 4 external helical ramps, 4 m wide, 18 m diameter [est] (the 70s "concrete drum" look).
- `main_stand_roof`: cantilever 25-30 m deep, 120 m long, steel trusses with corrugated sheet; decayed version has sheets missing in a jagged band.
- `floodlight_mast`: 4-leg tapering lattice, 3 x 3 m base to 1 x 1 m top, 55 m; head 10 x 6 m frame with 48-60 lamps [est]; service ladder and caged platforms every 10 m.
- `scoreboard`: 20 x 8 m steel frame on 2 legs, panel face mostly blank or torn.
- `perimeter_fence`: 2.4 m block wall with railings, gate arches with stadium name in raised letters (invent).
- Side buildings: `pool_hall` 70 x 30 m flat roof with drained pool; `indoor_arena` drum 70 m diameter, 18 m tall [est].
- `ring_road`: 2-lane loop road 15 m out from the bowl, broken kerbs, car park of cracked asphalt with grass in the joints.

**Colours / materials:** Concrete `#9A958B` new, `#7E786E` stained, black streaks `#3A3631`, moss `#4E5B35`. Seats: faded plastic green `#3E7D4A`, white `#E6E2D6`, some red `#A63A2F`; missing seats leave bolt stubs. Parapet paint green `#2E7D32`/white. Masts galvanised `#8F9496` going rust `#8A4B2A`.

**Variants:** Decayed national (70% in Gidi canon, it is the National Bowl), mid-renovation (20%: scaffolding on one side, new seat colours on one stand, contractor billboard), renovated (10%: clean paint, LED boards).

**Condition states:**
- New (1972 look): clean concrete, painted lettering, all masts lit.
- Worn: streaks, faded seats, one mast dark, peeling paint.
- Decayed (default): trees growing from the upper terrace (2-6 m saplings), roof sheets missing, a fallen mast lying across the car park or bent over the stand, scoreboard empty, fence broken in 3-5 places, pool full of green water.
- Abandoned + occupied: shacks inside the vomitories and under the rakers, washing lines between raker fins, cooking smoke, a church banner on the stand.

**Props and life:** Day: footballers on a dusty half-pitch, a fitness group running the ring road, mechanics in the car park, a crusade tent (white, 30 x 15 m), touts at the gate, banner for a concert. Night: no stadium light, 2-3 fluorescent tubes in squatter rooms under the stand, a bonfire, generator hum; the dark mast silhouette against the city glow.

**SELECT BY:** unique; district `hub`, near main road and rail. Condition fixed `decayed`; squatter density from district danger.

**Gidi original designs (not Surulere):**
- **A. "The Bowl" (canon):** a perfect **circle** (not oval), 230 m across, with **32 raker fins painted alternate green and white** at the top 3 m (now flaking) so the rim reads as a striped crown at distance; a **single huge concrete scoreboard tower** (12 x 6 m plan, 40 m tall) standing *outside* the bowl like a bell tower, its face long dead; **three** masts standing and one snapped at 2/3 height, its head hanging. The stadium name in 2 m concrete letters along the ramp with three letters missing.
- **B. Alternative shell roof:** one stand under a thin **concrete wave canopy** (hyperbolic shell, 30 m cantilever) with a hole punched through it where a section fell; daylight shafts through it become a landmark shot.

## 2. Newer / mid-size stadium

**Reference.** Teslim Balogun Stadium (Surulere): about 24,325 seats, football-only, remodelled from 2021 to an all-covered stand plan [src Wikipedia/Vanguard]. Onikan rebuilt as Mobolaji Johnson Arena (small city arena) [src Guardian].

**Looks like:** rectangular football box tight to the pitch; 2-4 stands with white steel truss roofs; seats in bold colour blocks spelling patterns; LED floodlights on the roof edge instead of masts; glass entrance pavilion.

**Kit parts:** `stand` 105 m long, 18-22 m tall, roof cantilever 20 m, steel tube truss white `#F2F2F2`; `roof_light_strip` on the front edge; `entrance_pavilion` 30 x 12 m glazing; seats 0.5 m pitch in blocks (green `#1F8A4C`, yellow `#F2C230`, white). Pitch 105 x 68 m. Footprint ~170 x 130 m [est].

**Condition:** new (clean, white), worn (rusty truss joints, faded seats), half-built (one stand bare concrete with rebar sticking up).

**Life:** match day: thousands of fans, horn sellers, jersey hawkers, police vans; non-match day: gate shut, security, a car wash in the car park.

**SELECT BY:** civic/hub/oldisland; wealth medium; near main road.

**Gidi designs:** **"Lagoon Arena"** in oldisland: 15,000 seats, one long stand roof shaped like a **canoe hull upturned** (curved steel ribs, 22 m), others open. **"Hub Mini-Stadium"**: 10,000 seats next to the National Bowl, new, used for league games, emphasising the decay of its neighbour.

## 3. Cap/hat-shaped national cultural building

**Reference.** National Theatre, Iganmu: built 1973-76 by a Bulgarian contractor for FESTAC '77, plan adapted from the Palace of Culture and Sports, Varna; "well over 31 m" tall, covers ~23,000 m2; main hall 5,000 seats; widely compared to a military cap [src nationaltheatre.gov.ng, Wikipedia, Structurae]. Main hall diameter around 52 m [src blog, weak]. Long neglected, restored as a creative hub in the 2020s [src].

**Looks like:** a squat round drum on a wide splayed **"brim"**: a ring of folded concrete fins radiating outwards, like the peak of a cap seen from all sides; ribbed white-grey concrete with horizontal bands of dark glazing; sits alone on a huge forecourt surrounded by flyovers. At night, ring lights under the brim.

**Kit parts:** `drum` 75-90 m diameter, 22 m tall; `brim_fins` 36-48 radial folded plates, each 6-8 m deep, projecting 10-14 m, angled down 20 deg; `crown` stepped top 31-35 m; `glazing_band` 3 m tall dark `#2C3A42`; `plaza` 300 m radius forecourt of paving with planters; `ramp_bridges` connecting to roads [est].

**Colours / materials:** concrete off-white `#D9D5CC` restored, `#A8A296` worn; fin undersides darker; accent: national green or bronze lettering.

**Condition:** restored (clean white, lit brim, fountains running), worn (grey, grass in plaza joints, broken lamps), decayed (black streaks down every fin, water stains, plaza used as car park and truck stop).

**Life:** day: hawkers on the forecourt, wedding photo shoots, buses parked; night: event crowds or total darkness with a lone security fire.

**SELECT BY:** unique; civic or hub; at a motorway junction.

**Gidi designs (none is a cap):**
- **A. "The Crown Hall":** a 70 m drum topped by **seven tall concrete "beads" (piers) rising 12 m above the roof**, each capped with a light, so it reads as a bead crown; a ring arcade at ground level. 38 m total.
- **B. "The Drum House":** shaped like a **talking drum** (hourglass) 60 m tall, with vertical steel "strings" cables between top and bottom rings (lit warm white at night). Proposed site: foodgate/hub edge facing the expressway.

## 4. Roundabout statue / bust

**Reference.** Allen roundabout (Ikeja) carried full-length statues of a music icon and a statesman until removed for junction works in 2020 [src Daily Trust, TheCable]; a replacement 20-ft (6 m) bust of the statesman stands at an LTV junction [src Guardian/ThisDay]. Many junction sculptures have decayed or been vandalised, e.g. a broken corps member raising a flag at Ojuelegba [src Guardian].

**Looks like:** a circular island 15-40 m across, kerbed black-and-yellow, with grass, ixora shrubs and a few palms; in the centre a plinth 2-5 m tall clad in tiles, granite or painted render; on it a bronze/fibreglass figure or a bust 2-6 m, painted bronze `#7A5A35` or gold `#C9A44A`; a name plaque; floodlights at the base, often broken.

**Kit parts:** `island` r 8-20 m, kerb 0.3 m painted; `plinth` square or octagon 2-4 m wide, 2-5 m tall, stepped base; `figure` full-length 3-5 m or `bust` 1.5-6 m (head+shoulders, larger-than-life); `plaque` 1 x 0.6 m; `uplights` 4; optional `fence` 1 m railings; `flagpoles` 3.

**Variants:** bust (45%), standing figure (30%), seated or with props (10%), abstract (15%, see 6).

**Condition:** new (clean gold/bronze, flowers, lights), worn (patina green-black streaks, faded paint, missing plaque letters), decayed (fibreglass cracked, a hand broken off, a head missing, graffiti/posters on the plinth, a beggar sleeping at the base), removed (empty plinth, rebar stub).

**Life:** okada riders waiting in its shade, newspaper vendor, LASTMA-type traffic officer, election posters glued to the plinth, a banner tied between palms. Night: uplights (if working) or dark with headlight sweeps.

**SELECT BY:** roundabout node with main roads; capital and railside (canon busts), oldshore (Hunter Roundabout); wealth ↑ → new/clean.

**Gidi designs:**
- **A. "The Founder Bust" (capital):** 5 m gilded head of an invented civic founder wearing a fila cap, on a **round stepped plinth 4 m tall** clad in green terrazzo, ringed by 8 short lamp bollards.
- **B. "Hunter Roundabout" (oldshore):** invented founding hunter, 4 m standing figure with a bow and a dog, dark bronze, on a rough laterite-stone plinth; okada stand around it.
- **C. Railside market roundabout:** bust of an invented market queen with a headtie, painted (not bronze) and visibly repainted (headtie pink over old blue).

## 5. Masquerade / cultural figure statue

**Reference.** Giant white Eyo-type masquerade monuments: one at Tafawa Balewa Square, a "world's tallest" unveiled at the John Randle Centre in 2025 [src Discover Lagos]; Eyo figures wear white flowing cloth and a broad hat and carry a staff [src Wikipedia Eyo]. A 2018 recycled-aluminium masquerade at a VI park [src Brandspur].

**Looks like:** a tall faceless figure fully draped to the ground, white cloth sculpted as vertical folds, a wide-brimmed hat, a staff; sometimes 3-5 figures in a group. Gleaming white by day, floodlit from below at night.

**Kit parts:** `draped_body` cone 1.5-3 m base, 5-12 m tall [est]; `hat` disc 1.5-2.5 m with crown; `staff` 4-6 m pole; `plinth` low 1 m; group arrangement in a crescent.

**Colours:** cloth white `#F4F2EA`, hat colour code (Gidi invent: red `#B33A2E`, yellow `#E2B33C`, purple `#5D3A7A`, green, black); staff dark wood `#4A3324`.

**Condition:** new (bright white), worn (grey-green mildew at the hem `#8D9182`), decayed (cracks, chipped staff, posters).

**SELECT BY:** oldisland (cultural heart), squares, parks; event days add live masquerades.

**Gidi designs:** **"The White Procession"**: five 7 m draped figures in a curve on the Old Island square, each hat a different colour, invented masquerade (no Eyo regalia specifics: Gidi figures carry fans, not staffs, and wear **conical** not broad hats). **"The Drummers"** (hub): three 4 m bronze drummers (talking drum, bata, gong) on a long low plinth at a junction.

## 6. Abstract steel and recycled-metal sculpture

**Looks like:** stainless-steel spikes, twisted ribbons, interlocking rings or a stylised map-shape 6-15 m tall in a planted median; or colourful painted steel "flowers" and "cowries". Chrome by day, LED-lit at night (blue/white). Often sponsored by a bank or telco with a small logo plate.

**Kit parts:** `ribbon` swept extrusion 8-15 m, 0.5 m section; `rings` 3 interlocking 4-6 m diameter; `cowrie` 3 m shell form; `led_strips`; `sponsor_plate` 1.5 x 0.5 m; plinth 0.5 m.

**Colours:** stainless `#C9CCCE`, painted primary red/yellow/blue, corten `#8A4B2A`.

**Condition:** new shiny, worn (dull, LED half-out), decayed (rusting welds, a ring missing, used as a poster pole).

**SELECT BY:** heights, capital, frontier boulevards; medians of new roads; wealth high.

**Gidi designs:** **"The Cowrie Gate"**: two 9 m stainless cowrie shells leaning together over a park path (heights). **"Okada Wheel"**: a 7 m ring of welded scrap motorbike wheels and chains, painted yellow, at the hub (made by the street-art collective, a story hook).

## 7. Commemorative square with gate, arches and horse statues → **Old Island square with fountains**

**Reference.** Tafawa Balewa Square: about 14.5 ha, built 1972 on a former race-course; gate with four white horses and seven red eagles (removed 2023 amid protests); a Remembrance Arcade honouring war dead; stands for parades [src Guardian, ThisDay, Legacy 1995, Lonely Planet].

**Looks like:** a vast open paved parade ground with grandstands on one side, flagpoles, a long colonnade; the entrance gate is the icon: a wide portal with **rearing white horses** on pedestals and **red eagles** on posts; fountains and a cenotaph.

**Kit parts:** `parade_ground` 300 x 200 m paving; `grandstand` 120 m long, 8-10 tiers, canopy; `arcade` colonnade 80-150 m long, 6 m tall, columns at 4 m; `gate` 40-60 m wide with 2-4 pillars 8-10 m tall; `horse_statue` 3-4 m (rearing, white `#F1EFEA` fibreglass/concrete) on 3 m plinths; `eagle` 2 m span red `#B3262E` on posts; `fountain` circular basin 12-20 m with central jet tier 4 m; `cenotaph` 8-12 m obelisk; `flagpoles` 12-20 m in a row.

**Condition:** ceremonial (painted fresh before national days), worn (fountains dry, basins full of rubbish and green water), decayed (horses with missing legs, eagles chipped, gate removed leaving pedestals), commercialised (event tents, car park, shops built into the arcade).

**Life:** event days: tents, generators, stage, crowds; normal days: hawkers, driving-school cars, football on the paving, people sleeping in the arcade; night: dark, a few lamps, couples, security.

**SELECT BY:** oldisland only (canon: "Fenced square with fountains").

**Gidi designs:**
- **A. "Liberty Square" (Old Island):** square plan 180 x 180 m, fenced with green railings; the gate is **two tall pylons topped by bronze canoes held by paddlers** (not horses); four **tiered fountains** in the quadrants (two dry, one running, one used for washing), a central 18 m **sun-disc column**; arcade on the lagoon side with 24 arches.
- **B. Rider statue variant:** a single horseman (invented warrior chief) at the square's centre, 6 m on a 5 m plinth; horse in black bronze, chief's robe painted indigo.

## 8. Independence arch / city gate / "welcome to" monument

**Pattern** [est]: on expressway entries to the state there are gateway structures: a portal frame spanning the carriageway with "Welcome to ..." lettering and the state slogan, flanked by sculpture or pillars; also arches at estate and town entries (town name, chief's crest). Older ones are concrete arches painted in state colours; newer ones are steel space-frames with LED text.

**Kit parts:** `portal` span 30-45 m over a dual carriageway, clear height 6.5 m, beam 3 m deep with lettering 1.5 m high; `pillars` 3 x 3 m, 12-18 m tall with finials; `side_sculpture`; `led_text`; town-arch variant: span 10-14 m, height 7 m, painted.

**Colours:** state-colour paint (Gidi: invent e.g. aqua `#1E8C8C` + gold `#D2A53A`), concrete white; town arches cream with green/red bands.

**Condition:** new (crisp, lit), worn (faded slogan of an earlier governor painted over), decayed (letters missing, a truck-hit pillar, posters).

**Life:** traffic police checkpoint, hawkers in go-slow, a toll or levy post, sometimes a bus-stop under it.

**SELECT BY:** city boundary on main roads (foodgate north entry, border west), town entries in oldshore.

**Gidi designs:** **"The North Gate"** (foodgate): twin 20 m pillars shaped like **stacked yam tubers/baskets** (food-gate theme) joined by a beam reading "WELCOME TO GIDI" plus an invented slogan; LED band. **"Old Quarter Arch"** (oldshore): a cream concrete arch with a carved crest of crossed paddles, 9 m.

## 9. Clock tower

**Reference.** The Cathedral Church of Christ had a clock from 1869 and a colonial-government tower; the old tower was destroyed by lightning around 1937 [src cathedral site]. Market and town clock towers exist in Yoruba towns [est].

**Looks like:** a square shaft 15-30 m with four clock faces near the top, a small pyramid or dome roof, often in the middle of a roundabout or market square; faces show different times or are stopped; painted cream with a coloured band.

**Kit parts:** `shaft` 3-4 m square, 18-25 m; `clock_head` 4 faces 2-2.5 m diameter, white with black numerals; `roof` pyramid/dome 3-4 m with a finial; `base` 6 m plinth with plaque and steps.

**Condition:** working (lit faces at night), stopped (each face different time, one face cracked), decayed (bird nests, streaks, a phone-mast antenna strapped on top).

**SELECT BY:** railside market roundabout, oldshore town centre, oldisland market.

**Gidi design:** **"Four-Time Tower"**: 22 m cream tower in the railside market roundabout where each face is stuck at a different hour (locals give directions by "the 4 o'clock side"); lit faces flicker with NEPA cuts.

## 10. Colonial-era civic buildings → **Heritage Strip**, oldisland, port

**Reference.** Colonial Secretariat (Broad Street, completed 1895): "English" style with twin towers, neglected, cracked, squatters [src Asiri]. Her Majesty's Broad Street Prison (1885 brick, demolished 1979) [src Freedom Park sources]. Old courts and city hall along the Marina [src Commonwealth Walkway].

**Looks like:** 2-3 storey long blocks with arcaded ground floors and verandas above, deep eaves, hipped red/rust roofs, tall shuttered windows, imported red brick or rendered cream walls, symmetrical façades with a central pediment or twin towers.

**Kit parts:** `block` 40-90 m long, 12-16 m deep, storey height 4.2-4.8 m; `arcade` round arches 3 m span; `veranda` 2.5 m deep with timber/iron balustrade; `roof` hipped, 25-30 deg, corrugated or clay tile; `tower` 2 at ends, 5 x 5 m, +6 m above eaves with pyramid roofs; `shutters` louvred, green `#3E6B4A`; `flagpole`.

**Colours:** cream `#E8DCC0`, ochre `#C79A5A`, brick `#9B4A35`, roof rust `#7E3B26` or red `#A13D2D`, shutters green or brown.

**Condition:** restored (fresh cream, white trim, lawn), worn (peeling, damp stains), decayed (roof sagging, trees in gutters, broken shutters, squatter washing on the veranda), ruin (roofless walls).

**SELECT BY:** oldisland waterfront, border Heritage Strip (canon: "old colonial buildings with balconies"), port (Old Reserve Avenue houses).

**Gidi designs:** **"Old Customs House"** (Heritage Strip): 60 m long, 2 storeys, 15 arches, twin square towers with tin pyramid roofs, one tower roof gone. **"The Old Court"** (oldisland): cream 3-storey with a portico of 6 columns and a stopped clock in the pediment.

**Gidi design:** **"Heights Golf Club"**: white clubhouse with a **green copper-look pyramid roof** and a long veranda overlooking a lagoon-side 18th green; a caddies' shed under a mango tree.

## 35. Toll plaza (landmark scale)

**Reference.** Lekki toll plaza (frontier main road) and the bridge plaza; tolling paused after Oct 2020 events, resumed later [src Wikipedia, Guardian].

**Kit parts:** `canopy` 70-100 m wide x 20 m deep, 6.5 m clear; `booths` 8-12, 2 x 3 m; `barrier_arms`; `admin_block`; `cameras`; `led_signs`.

**Gidi design:** **frontier toll plaza** on "one long road down the peninsula": a **saw-tooth canopy** of 10 bays in white and road-sign green; one booth burnt and boarded, a memorial mural on the admin wall.
