# Gidi: 15 districts (map v0.3)

13 October 2026. Built from the 20-LGA research (`lagos-lgas-research.md`). Places that felt alike were merged into one district. Sizes come from the map's polygons: 21.4 km² of district land in an 8.8 × 6.7 km frame, plus the lagoon, the ocean, and farms and bush that aren't built on. All names are working titles. Every real name, rite and person stays out of the game (see the sacred list in the research).

**Okada rule.** Okada are banned (taskforce seizes the bike) on the Long Bridge and in Old Island, the Heights, the Capital and the Port. The Hub, the Maze and Food Gate get random sweeps. Everywhere else is okada country.

## Act 1: Survive (open from the start, mainland)

**1. Jungle City, 1.3 km²** (Ajegunle + Festac)
Reclaimed marsh packed between the port and the expressway. Face-me-I-face-you rows, rusty roofs, school-party hotels, internet cafés where the scam boys work, a whole music scene from cheap studios. Floods every rainy season. **Tunde's room is here.** His yahoo-route friend lives here too.
*Oja:* small parcels, phones. *Outside missions:* DJ and studio side stories, flood days, the friend's café.

**2. The Maze, 1.0 km²** (Mushin + Shomolu)
The densest district: lanes of second-hand car parts, house-front print shops running at 4 a.m., street "ticketing" boys charging every parking spot, a canal that splits two gangs, market streets where trucks can't fit. **The steal and the "Ole!" chase happen here.**
*Oja:* car parts, forged papers. *Outside missions:* fencing parts, print runs before a raid, ticketing turf, blackout riots.

**3. The Hub, 1.1 km²** (Oshodi + Ojuelegba/Surulere)
Where every route meets: a giant bus interchange with skywalks above and the under-bridge world below (touts, pickpockets, food sellers), motor parks, a decaying 1970s stadium held by squatters, an old highlife hotel. **The boss's motor park is here; the gate where he pulls Tunde in sits between the Maze and the Hub.**
*Oja:* whatever the boss says. *Outside missions:* okada fares, the toll run (paying, later collecting), union faction fights, stadium hideout.

**4. Railside, 2.3 km²** (Agege + Ifako-Ijaiye + Alimosho)
The commuter sprawl along the railway: bread bakeries at dawn, the cattle market and abattoir, a big northern community by the tracks, a market roundabout everything jams around, a landfill, an old colonial waterworks, pipelines that people tap at night. Okada heartland.
*Oja:* bread at dawn, meat, fuel. *Outside missions:* bread runs, outrunning trains, bike-seizure riots, the pipeline-night set piece.

**5. Food Gate, 1.8 km²** (Kosofe)
The main road in from the north: the food market where trucks from the north unload pepper and tomatoes before dawn, a big bus park, a protest park, creek villages behind it you can only reach by boat. Holds the mainland end of the Long Bridge, with its checkpoint.
*Oja:* produce that spoils (freshness timer). *Outside missions:* dawn unloading rush, riot from a road accident, creek smuggling.

## Act 2: Hustle

**6. The Port, 1.3 km²** (Apapa + Mile 2 + the prison)
Containers, tank farms and a leafy colonial reserve strangled by trucks. Tankers queue for days, there's a collector every few hundred metres, and the market under the bridge has no law at night. Holds the prison. **Where the oja comes in.**
*Oja:* the container that isn't what the paperwork says. *Outside missions:* the toll run through 10+ collectors, tanker-fire set piece, weaving the truck canyon.

**7. The Bazaar, 1.2 km²** (Ojo's electronics market + Trade Fair)
A self-governing market of second-hand electronics with its own court, container jail, taskforce and lenders. Next door, a sinking livestock-and-scrap market with a back-alley pill trade. An army barracks and a broken expressway.
*Oja:* electronics, pirated discs, pills. *Outside missions:* market-court jobs, demolition-day rescue of stock, chop-shop minigame.

**8. The Capital, 1.1 km²** (Ikeja)
Government quarter with wide boulevards; a maze of houses turned into phone malls (swap scams, crowds that form around any argument); an all-night strip around a roundabout; a live-music shrine; the airport fence; barracks with a disaster in their past. Okada banned.
*Oja:* phones, documents, people. *Outside missions:* phone fencing, protest day closures, night-strip rides.

**9. The Estate, 1.0 km²** (Magodo, Omole, Ikeja GRA)
Gated streets, gatemen, big houses, generators humming, the politicians and the men above the boss. Quiet, which makes trouble loud.
*Oja:* deliveries to men you don't question. *Outside missions:* gateman informants, a land-case siege.

**10. Old Island, 1.0 km²** (Lagos Island)
The oldest town: the palace, cloth markets run by market-women leaders, rotting returnee houses with arches and shutters, bank towers on the waterfront 300 m away, lanes only porters can enter. Crossing here means the Long Bridge (okada banned) or the port bridge. Okada banned.
*Oja:* cloth, cash. *Outside missions:* market-queen jobs, alley porter runs, an invented festival day that closes the main road.

**11. Old Shore, 2.2 km²** (Ikorodu + Epe)
The old royal towns on the far side of the lagoon: a fish market at dawn, barges and canoes, a ferry jetty, old quarters with their own kings. On invented curfew nights the town empties and the roads change. Okada heartland.
*Oja:* fish (freshness timer), goods by ferry. *Outside missions:* ferry runs, curfew nights, fish runs before the heat.

**12. The Border, 1.5 km²** (Badagry)
The far-west frontier: coconut beaches, an old colonial heritage strip, a long road of checkpoints run by different agencies, bush paths around them. Rice and fuel cross every day. Okada heartland.
*Oja:* smuggled rice and fuel. *Outside missions:* rice runs on bush paths, the checkpoint gauntlet, creek boats.

## Act 3: Empire

**13. Water Town, 0.9 km²** (Makoko + Bariga + Agboyi + the sawmill)
Stilt houses under the Long Bridge, canoe lanes, the sawmill quarter with timber rafts and sawdust, a stilt bar. The state wants the land; the bulldozer barges are coming. **The land war ends here.**
*Oja:* anything moved by canoe. *Outside missions:* canoe getaways, sawmill chases, demolition night.

**14. The Heights, 1.2 km²** (Ikoyi, VI, Banana Island)
Towers, embassies, clubs, mansions behind walls, a tolled cable bridge, Sunday beach crowds. The richest ground, built on cleared fishing villages. Okada banned.
*Oja:* money, secrets. *Outside missions:* driver jobs for the rich, club nights, Sunday beach.

**15. The Frontier, 2.6 km²** (Lekki + Ibeju-Lekki)
One long road down the peninsula past a toll plaza, half-built estates, plots with two owners, a refinery glowing on the horizon, a beach eaten by the sea. Every fence is a fight.
*Oja:* land papers, site security. *Outside missions:* fence fights, ocean-surge night, refinery convoy heist.

## What got merged, and why

- **Mushin + Shomolu → the Maze:** both dense working-class districts where the trade lives inside the homes (parts, print).
- **Oshodi + Surulere's Ojuelegba → the Hub:** both are junctions where everything passes, with motor parks and under-bridge life. Surulere's stadium comes along.
- **Agege + Ifako-Ijaiye + Alimosho → Railside:** all outer commuter sprawl along the rail and expressway, okada country, markets around roundabouts.
- **Ajegunle + Festac → Jungle City:** both crowded, flood-prone, next to the port, with music and scam cafés.
- **Apapa + Mile 2 → the Port.** **Ojo + Trade Fair → the Bazaar.**
- **Ikorodu + Epe → Old Shore:** both old lagoon towns with kings, ferries, fish and curfews.
- **Lekki + Ibeju-Lekki → the Frontier:** one continuous strip of new estates and land war.
- **Lagos Mainland was split:** its markets went to the Maze, Makoko and the sawmill to Water Town, and its old railway houses can appear along the rail line.
- **Ikeja and Kosofe stayed separate** because they're too different from everything else. Magodo, Omole and Ikeja GRA became their own district, the Estate.

## Honest notes

- Railside, Food Gate, Old Shore and the Frontier are the biggest districts because they're sprawl and should feel thinner. The inner districts are under 1.2 km² and should be packed.
- v0.3 (owner, 2026-10-13): the Port and Jungle City grew from 0.9 to 1.3 km² each, on land pushed out into the sea. That fits the real places: Ajegunle is reclaimed marsh and Apapa's port is built out into the water. Nothing else shrank.
