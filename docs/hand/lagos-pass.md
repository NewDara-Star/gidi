# The Lagos pass (owner, 19 Oct)

A place in Gidi is "Lagos" only when the owner has looked at it, rendered, and said so. Getting one spot there, all the way,
teaches the technique; every decision made on the way becomes a rule other places can use (the data carries it), but each
place earns its own pass when the owner has looked at it. This takes years; places are improved in updates as they pass.

## How a spot gets its pass
1. **Reference frames:** the owner's photos of real Lagos places like it (`~/Real-world/research/refs/`, study only) and the
   two videos; zoomed in, everything in them listed.
2. **The shot list:** every element in those frames, below, each with what makes it read as Lagos.
3. **Build each element:** a kit piece (Blender, part by part), a Meshy sculpture (statues only), a Megascans surface, a
   person or crowd (characters), an effect (haze, smoke, dust).
4. **Place it by hand** in the spot (hand-edit files on the drawing board; data first, the kit draws it).
5. **The look:** light, haze, dirt and grime, wet patches, decals; a photo-mode render.
6. **The owner vets it.** Passed, or a list of what still reads wrong. Back to 3.
7. **Rules out:** what worked becomes data rules or kit defaults, so other places start closer; they still need their own pass.

## Spot 1: the Àdàrú motor park on its main road
Refs: `refs/lagos-life/` 01-13 (09: a motor park beside the expressway; 12: Lagos Island traffic; 11: a jam with umbrellas and
billboards; 13: a street from a balcony; 01-08: hawkers), `refs/games/` (the two videos), `refs/vehicles/danfo/`.

**Vehicles:** danfos parked in rows and double-parked on the road edge, doors open, conductors at the doors; danfos jammed
three deep in the road; okadas weaving and parked in clusters; kekes; a few private cars and a lorry; a molue.
**People:** a dense crowd on the verge and between the rows; conductors calling routes; agberos with tickets; passengers
waiting with bags; hawkers walking between the cars with head loads: a basin of drinks (01), a crate of drinks on a head pad
(02), loaves of bread in a clear bag on a tray (07, 08), a glass box of puff-puff or coconut (06), stacked pots of food (05);
a push-cart ice-cream seller (03, 04: our own brand, no real one); traders sitting under umbrellas; people eating at plastic
tables (13).
**Stalls and furniture:** big parasol umbrellas in mixed colours (sponsor-print style, our brands); tables, plastic chairs and
benches; watermelon and fruit on wheelbarrows; cool boxes, jerrycans, buckets, crates, nylon bags; a kiosk with a phone-credit
sign; tyres and a car tyre seat.
**Street edge:** concrete barriers and a railing between the road and the park; broken interlocking pavers; a drainage gutter
with slabs; litter, sachet-water bags, puddles; overhead power and phone wires everywhere; tall billboards; painted
signboards; posters on walls; satellite dishes, AC units, water tanks on the buildings; sun-faded paint.
**Air and light:** haze, dust kicked up, exhaust smoke from a danfo, low warm sun or overcast grey (09 is overcast).
**Sound later:** conductors calling stops (the district names, said aloud).
