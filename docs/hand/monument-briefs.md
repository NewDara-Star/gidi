# Monuments: what each needs (19 Oct)

The owner's rule: Meshy for statues and monuments nobody walks into; structures people walk into or under are built in
Blender part by part. Credits are spent in two steps: a concept picture first (nano-banana-2, 6 credits), the owner approves
it, only then the 3D (meshy-7.1 multi-image, 2K, 30 credits). Invented figures only: no real person's likeness.

## Words are enough: concept picture, then 3D
| Monument | Where | Brief for the concept picture |
|---|---|---|
| The Founder | Àdàrú, at Ladi Lane's south mouth | a bronze statue of a broad-shouldered elder in agbada and fila, one hand on a walking staff, the other raised in welcome, on a square stone plinth; weathered green-bronze streaks; invented, no real person |
| The Saxophonist | The Capital, on the Strip | a bronze saxophonist mid-solo, leaning back, eyes closed, open shirt and flared trousers (1970s), on a round plinth; the energy of the city's music, no real musician |
| The Hunter | Ẹ̀gbẹ́-Irin, on the old road | a bronze traditional hunter in a smock with amulets, a dane gun over one shoulder, a dog at his side, on a rough stone plinth |
| The Gate Riders (two) | Old Island, Parade Square's gates | a bronze rider on a rearing horse, the rider in a durbar robe and turban with a spear raised; a matching pair facing each other |
| The Masquerade | Old Island | a tall Eyo-style masquerade in white flowing cloth with a broad hat and a staff, in polished white stone or painted bronze, on a low plinth |
| The Scrap Rider | Ìbùdó, by Gidi Interchange | a sculpture of an okada and its rider welded from scrap: gears, chains, exhaust pipes, rebar and car panels, rusty and bright in places, on a concrete block |
| Three Elders | Òkè-Ọlà or The Estate | three seated elders in bronze, talking, one with a chewing stick, wrapped cloth and caps, on a long low plinth; quiet old money |

## In place, for the menu
Each finished monument gets its surroundings built up and a photo-mode picture where it stands; those pictures cycle on the
main menu beside the menu items (the owner's idea).
