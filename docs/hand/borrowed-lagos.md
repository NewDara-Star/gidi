# Borrowed from Lagos: real places reimagined in Gidi (16 Oct 2026)

The owner's direction: "take inspiration from the places to build the area... also from places that didn't inspire the districts, to give a taste of everywhere, tastefully and tactfully... research the actual Lagos map and see things you could take and creatively integrate."

## How it works
- **Position:** each real landmark is mapped onto Gidi with the same fit that placed the districts (each study area's real centre against its Gidi anchor; median miss 23 m). "Lands at" is where it falls; the Gidi spot can shift to fit the plan.
- **What we take:** what makes the place recognisable (a silhouette, a use, a chaos, a sound), never its outline or its name. Every name below is invented.
- **Tact:** heritage and sacred places are treated with respect. They're safe ground, never a crime site, and never played for laughs.
- **Status:** a list for the hand pass. Each one is placed by hand, district by district, like the Maze.
