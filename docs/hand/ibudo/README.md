# Ìbùdó, spot 1: the market road under the Ring Road flyover (design v1, 19 Oct)

Oshodi, studied on Google Earth and Street View (`~/Real-world/research/google/ibudo/`, notes there), rebuilt one-for-one
first, then made ours (the owner's method: like the fonts and the danfo). `design_v1.png`: metres, north up, 320 x 320 m.

One-for-one (from Oshodi):
- the expressway rides a 22 m flyover over the old motor road and the rail, on pillars every 30 m;
- Market Road (after Oshodi Rd): 10 m, mud, no kerb, passes under the flyover's foot;
- the market behind a two-storey shop row, its gate on Market Road (a painted arch, red bars);
- shop rows both sides: lock-ups with shutters below, balconies with doors above, washing and banners, outside stairs;
- traders on planks and tarps right on the road edge, parasols; danfos jammed along the road and stopped under the flyover;
- the terminal (the oval) across the rail, a footbridge to it; billboard walls; dense rusty-roof housing south (Orile).

Made ours: invented names (Ìbùdó Market, Ìbùdó Terminal, our shops and brands), the footbridge in our own truss design,
the twists still to choose with the owner. The orange box is the video-1 play strip.

Then: the owner approves the plan; it goes into the map by hand edit (it replaces the generated Ìbùdó here, which doesn't
match: its flyover isn't over the Ring Road); then the playable build in Unreal from the project's assets.
