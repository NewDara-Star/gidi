# The Maze and the Hub: what makes them unmistakably Lagos (agreed 16 Oct 2026)

The first hand-made districts: Act 1 opens here (`decisions/story/act-1-draft.md`). Each item says what it is, where it goes in Gidi, how it gets built (the hand files, `scripts/hand.py`), and where it comes from:
- **photo:** the owner's reference photos (`~/gidi-data/research/refs/REVIEW.md`)
- **study:** the deep study (`docs/studies/`)
- **ism:** the running Lagos-isms log (`docs/LAGOS-ISMS.md`)
- **brief:** the district brief (`web/districts_info.json`)
- **story:** the Act 1 draft

**Agreed (16 Oct):** the owner left the choice to Claude ("Decide. I trust your judgment"). All 23 items go in as written; the names are settled below.

## Names (settled 16 Oct)
- **Pedro Print Row becomes Press Row; Daleko Grain Yard becomes Gari Yard.** Ladi Lane stays (invented; a nod to Ladipo, not a copy), and so does National Bowl.

**What was weighed:**
- **"Pedro Print Row"** is the real Pedro Road print district in Shomolu. Suggestions: **Press Row**, **Ink Lane**, **Copier Street**.
- **"Ladi Lane Parts Market"** is invented, but close to the real Ladipo auto-parts market in Mushin, its inspiration. It's in the locked opening and the story, so it's the owner's call: keep it (a nod, not a copy) or change it.
- **"Daleko Grain Yard"** is the real Daleko market in Mushin. Suggestions: **Gari Yard**, **Sack Yard**, **Eleja Grain Yard** (an invented Yoruba-style name).

## The Maze (Mushin and Shomolu)
**The opening:** Ladi Lane Parts Market (story, brief).
1. **The approach.** Tunde's walk up to the market: the controls tutorial. A street of house-front shops leading to the market's east mouth: a phone-case wheelbarrow man with an earpiece, a fruit seller, Mama Put's stall with plastic chairs and a radio (photo assets 1, 9; streets 18).
2. **Ladi Lane itself.** A long straight aisle, 4–6 m wide, of second-hand car parts stacked to the roofline. Lock-up sheds both sides, umbrellas over the gaps (brief; photo markets). It's the chase line: straight, with crates, awnings and a pushed trolley to duck and vault.
3. **The steal's stall** at the east end, and **Mama Put's** at the mouth (story).
4. **The far (west) mouth.** Where the crowd closes in, then the **boss's canopy**: plastic chairs, a table, a big umbrella (story).
5. **Tunde's room.** A back room in a face-me-I-face-you compound, under 3 minutes' walk from the market (story, living test).

**The streets:**
6. **The parts trade spilling onto the road.** Engines and doors on the walkway, a mechanic under a car in the carriageway, the lane kept clear by shouting (ism 6, photo assets 8).
7. **Press Row.** Print kiosks bolted onto the fronts of 2–3 storey houses: laminating machines, paper bales, ink drums, running at 4 a.m. (brief).
8. **Gang Canal.** An open concrete channel with green-black water and plastic. One footbridge is the gang line; elsewhere, plank crossings. Streets that dead-end at the canal (brief; photo gutters 3, 9, 10; ism 1).
9. **Transformer 44.** One transformer on a pole feeding a whole block, with a nest of wires and a generator beside it (brief).
10. **The rail edge.** Streets that dead-end at the tracks, with people climbing over (ism 2: Mushin is the real place for it).
11. **Parking boys.** A "ticketing" spot on every street that will take a car: a boy, a ticket book, a chair (brief).
12. **The buka under the flyover,** next to the mechanics (brief).
13. **Surfaces:** worn tar on the mains; red earth and mud behind the market; interlock in the newer closes (study; owner, 16 Oct).
14. **Betting shops** on corners, and a church in a shopfront with plastic chairs (study: churches are the most-listed place, 13 per km²).

## The Hub (Oshodi and Ojuelegba)
**The boss's world:** the motor park (story).
15. **The boss's motor park.** A yard of danfos nose to tail, a union office shack, a levy table, agberos with ticket books. The okada park beside it (story, brief; photo markets 6, 19).
16. **The gate** (story): the park's entrance, where the levy is paid.
17. **Under the Bridge.** Under the flyover: danfos packed nose to tail, umbrellas, food sellers, touts, pickpockets (photo assets 3: Ojuelegba).
18. **Gidi Interchange.** The bus terminal with skywalks above and CCTV towers; the market on the rail tracks beside it, traders on the rails (photo markets 22, 23: Oshodi).
19. **Bus stops on the big roads.** A police kiosk (red and white), a billboard, a green overhead direction sign, a keke rank (photo assets 2, 6).
20. **The okada ban edge.** The Hub gets random sweeps: a task force truck parked at a junction, seized bikes in its back (brief).
21. **National Bowl.** The 1970s stadium: a cracked concrete bowl, squatter tents, a pitch of sand (brief). (Check: the National Stadium in Surulere is real; "National Bowl" is close. Rename?)
22. **Stadium Highlife Hotel.** An old hotel with a neon sign (brief).
23. **Surfaces:** worn tar and red earth; mud in the park yard after rain (study, photo).

## How it gets built
Each ticked item becomes hand edits in `web/hand/maze-v2.json` and `web/hand/hub-v2.json`: exact footprints, furniture, roads, surfaces and labels, with a note on every one. The opening is then locked again (`web/locked.json`, in the v2 frame). The kit draws what it can. What it couldn't draw: the parts stacks, print kiosks, the canal footbridge, the buka, the levy table, the police kiosk, the overhead sign.
