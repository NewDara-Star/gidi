## 2. Findings by MAP-DONE-LAYERS group (by eye)

### A: ground, streets, water
- **A12 water: canals are missing.** A canal network runs through Idi-Araba. It goes west along Daniyan St / Omosolape, through the LUTH campus SW-NE, with a branch north along the Alao Adegboyega line toward Jibowu, and on toward Ojuelegba. South of the Healing Cross chapel it is about 17-22 m wide with dark water (S10b). An open drain with a tree strip, about 11 m with its edges, runs NW-SE through Ladipo north, from Jimade St past SMEDAN to Fatai Atere Way (about Gidi 12425,5306 to 12605,5440). Our map has no canal record.
- **Road surfaces:**
  - Main roads are tar with raised concrete kerbs and walkways: Isolo Rd (V8) and Akanro St (V9, kerbed on one side, sandy earth shoulder on the other).
  - Side streets and lanes are sand or laterite with puddles (V1, V4, V11).
  - The Ikorodu Rd service road is sand-dusted, held off the main carriageway and BRT lanes by a concrete barrier and a blue steel fence (V2).
  - Vono Rd is narrow tar, about 6-7 m, with grass verges (V7).
  - The expressway side road (V13) is wide, with dashed white lane markings and a kerbed separator.
- **Drains:** open concrete drains along walls and walkways on Palm Ave (V3), the lane at Moshalasi (V4) and by the station (V11, a raised walkway with an open drain).
- **Bare ground:** a bare-earth strip about 30 m each side of the rail at the station (S6). The Bishop Aggey field is bare brown earth (S7).

### D: roofs and colour

## 3. How this differs from map/d15 now
1. **The NW belt** is drawn as 3-4 storey flats where the real ground is 1-storey sheds and warehouses: the expressway strip, SMEDAN, Paramo, Daleko market and the Akanro warehouse. This also bears on D15-03 being over and D15-05 being under in the buildings note.
2. **LUTH** is dense `shed` records at 44%. The real campus is 4-6 storey wards in lawns and trees at about 22% (buildings note). It needs a hospital-like kind and storeys, thinning, and trees.
3. **Open land is drawn as houses:** NIMET, the Bishop Aggey field, the CKC grove and the Healing Cross compound.
4. **The Vono Rd factory** is under. The halls are almost wall to wall; we have 4 sheds.
5. **No canal or drain records** for the Idi-Araba canal network or the Ladipo north drain.
6. **Places:** one market against at least 9 real ones. No LG office, post office or correctional centre kinds. Two police places against at least 2 named on Google (Olosan, LUTH) plus others. Oduduwa and the Daleko school have no school place at their spots.
7. **Roofs:** painted long-span is too high (25.8% against about 10-15%) and green is too common. There are no near-black roofs.
8. **Trees:** 92, all at road edges. Groves and lawns are missing.
9. **Storeys:** shack and kiosk_row records at 2 storeys (137 and 41) where they are 1-storey timber on the street.
10. **Street names:** the 23 names listed in section 1 have no hit in STREETS.md.
11. **Furniture not yet recorded as a kind:** whether our map holds any of these was not checked record by record.
    - the traffic signal at Ladipo / Agege Motor Rd;
    - lane markings on the expressway side road;
    - the BRT median, fence and stations;
    - solar lamps;
    - okada and keke ranks;
    - container lock-ups;
    - razor wire on factory walls.
12. **LUTH's wall colour:** ochre with a green band by eye, against the drawn cream.
