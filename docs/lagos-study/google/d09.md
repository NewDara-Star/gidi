# d09 Ayégúnlẹ̀: the Google look (28 Oct)

Study note. Real names belong here only, never in map records. Everything in it was read in Chrome on Google Maps, the Google satellite and Street View, and it is **by eye** unless it says "scripted". The scripted numbers describe our map (`map/d09/plan.json`, `map/d09/district.json`, `map/d09/STREETS.md`) and all come from one script in the repo, `docs/lagos-study/google/scripts/d09_look.py` (run with `~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python -I`; it prints 215 lines and writes nothing). Nothing was copied: no screenshots, pictures or traced shapes are in the repo, and the signs and logos seen are described but not reproduced.

Real place: Ajegunle, Amukoko and Olodi-Apapa (Ajeromi-Ifelodun), between the Lagos-Badagry Expressway (NW edge) and the Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway (SW edge).

Frame: Gidi x = (UTM31N east − 500525)/3, y = 10500 − (north − 707825)/3 (the same as `scripts/working_bar.gidi()`). Every position below was converted by the script and its inside/outside test is against the d09 outline, which runs x 11458-13158 and y 7742-9258 (1,391,942 m², scripted).

Scale check: read off Google's own scale bar, not from two known points. With "600m" in the URL the view is about z17, where 100 m is about 85 px (about 1.18 m/px); with "436m" it is about z18, where 50 m is about 98 px (about 0.51 m/px). The widths and sizes below come from this, by eye, and are real metres. The F points (features inside a view) were placed by counting pixels from the view's centre at that scale, so they are good to about 20-40 m.
