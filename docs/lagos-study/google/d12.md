# d12 Agunda: the Google look (28 Oct)

Study note. Real names belong here only, never in map records. Everything in it was read in Chrome on Google Maps, the Google satellite and Street View, and it is **by eye** unless it says "scripted". The scripted numbers describe our map (`map/d12/plan.json`, `map/d12/district.json`, `map/d12/STREETS.md`) and Google Open Buildings v3 (the Lagos file and the per-tile thresholds that `scripts/working_bar.py` reads), and all come from one script in the repo, `docs/lagos-study/google/scripts/d12_look.py` (run with `~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python -I`; it prints 301 lines in about 20 s and writes nothing). Nothing was copied: no screenshots, pictures or traced shapes are in the repo, and the signs and logos seen are described but not reproduced.

Real place: Aguda (Surulere), Coker and Orile Iganmu, with a lobe running south-east to Ijora Badia. Bounded by the Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway (west edge), the Ijesha/Lawanson side (north), Adelabu St and Babs Animashaun Rd (north-east), the Lagos-Badagry Expressway (south-west of the lobe, and its north-east edge), Iganmu Rd (the lobe's west edge) and the Ijora interchange (east). There is no district study note for d12 in `docs/lagos-study/districts/`; the place was read from `map/d12/district.json` (its note names Aguda, Coker and Orile) and from the views themselves.

Frame: Gidi x = (UTM31N east − 500525)/3, y = 10500 − (north − 707825)/3 (the same as `scripts/working_bar.gidi()`). Every position below was converted by the script and its inside/outside test is against the d12 outline, which runs x 11725-13242 and y 6842-8325 (965,702 m², scripted).

Scale check: read off Google's own scale bar, not from two known points. With "600m" in the URL the view opens at "871m", about z17, where 100 m is about 85 px (about 1.18 m/px); with "300m" it opens at "436m", about z18, about 0.5 m/px. The left search panel covers the left third of each view, so the view centres were moved about 170 m west of what was to be read. The widths and sizes below come from this, by eye, and are real metres. The F points were placed by counting pixels from the view's centre at that scale, so they are good to about 20-40 m.

## 2. What the real place has (by eye)
- **Water:** a canal network: the north canal from the Ijesha side south-west to the west canal by Sanya St (F5, F6, F7); the Abata canal from Orile east, a 30-40 m green band (F8, F9, F36); the winding canal under the Coker Rd bridge (F10, S19), about 20-25 m; the Amukoko creek and the east-lobe canal (F28, F30, F31); a creek behind Census Market (F4). A large marsh fills the lobe's middle (F25, about 500 × 400 m), with the Saka Alao fill heap pushing into it (F26).
- **Rail:** the Blue Line runs in the Lagos-Badagry Expressway's median, with an elevated station at Orile (V4, F18).
- **Main roads:** the two expressways, Coker Rd, Babs Animashaun Rd, Adelabu St, Iganmu Rd and Gaskiya Rd (both dual carriageways, V5, V6), Olatunde Onimole St.
- **Power:** the TCN Akangba substation (F2) with high-voltage pylon lines leaving it over the houses (V10, V12).
- **Markets:** Aguda Ultra Modern Market (F1), Ijesha Market (F15), Census Market's shack row (V12), Ijora 7up Market (F35), the stalls on the expressway service road (V4).
- **Grounds and yards:** the sandy school field (F12), Sanya Junior Grammar (F17), the bush ground by the substation (F3), the rubble lots (F20, F23), the Ijora Badia dump ground with ponds (F33), the Ascon/Total truck and container depot (F29).
- **Civil:** the Coker-Aguda LG secretariat (F11), Orile Police Station (F19), the Aguda Central Mosque (F13), St Thomas of Villanova (F14), St Michael's Anglican (F22), many small private schools.
- **Roofs:** the grid north of the Abata canal is roof to roof with small courtyards, 2-storey rows and 3-4 storey walk-ups (V1, V7, V9); Orile, Coker and Ijora Badia are tighter and rustier, more 1-2 storey; zinc lock-up rows line the main streets (V1, V3, V12).
- **Vehicles:** yellow kekes (V5, V6), danfos and coaches (V11), lorries (V8), okadas (V2).

## 3. How this differs from map/d12 now
1. **Cover far under.** Our cover of the outline is 15.25% against Google's 40.79% (90%) - 45.10% (80%) (scripted); our median r30 cover over 50 spots is 14% against Google's 34%, and at 29 of them Google's is more than twice ours (scripted). The district is far below the working bar's density row.
2. **No water.** The canals, the creeks and the marsh have no records (the map holds no water type, scripted). Points: F5 12238,7019; F6 12102,7255; F7 11976,7550; F8 12448,7428; F10 12109,7727; F28 12813,7933; F30 12865,8025; F4 12669,7218.
3. **Houses in the marsh.** Where Google has 0-1% cover, ours has houses: F25 (2 compound houses, 9%), F26 (1, 10%), S12 (a walkup, 10%), F38 (a walkup of 559 m² where a school stands) (scripted counts at by-eye spots). Google's own cover marks the marsh's extent; ours ignores it.
4. **Markets and big civil sites.** 0 market places in the district (scripted); Aguda Ultra Modern Market (F1) has shops under flats, Ijesha Market (F15) and Ijora 7up Market (F35) nothing. The TCN substation (F2) has no houses but no plot, wall or pylon line (scripted: 3 plots in the district, 2 compounds and a school ground; no pole or wire records). The Orile rail station (F18) is not drawn.
5. **Street names.** 123 of 191 names read are not in STREETS.md (scripted). Coker Rd, a main road with a canal bridge, has no record named for it (lanes and Bale Street 10-11 m off); Babs Animashaun Rd at 12658,7568 is an unnamed lane; Mogaji St and Erelu-Danisa St are unnamed lanes.
6. **Roads drawn as 7 m lanes.** 724 of 882 road records are 7 m lanes (scripted); Iganmu Rd and Gaskiya Rd are dual carriageways with medians (V5, V6, by eye), Iganmu Rd is drawn as a 10.75 m main road (scripted); at the V5 point Gaskiya Rd lies 7 m outside the outline and our nearest record is an unnamed 7 m lane, though STREETS.md has a Gaskiya Road (scripted).
7. **Open land drawn or left** (scripted counts): the rubble lot F23 holds a shack; the school field F12 has no ground; the depot F29 and the dump ground F33 are empty, which matches.
