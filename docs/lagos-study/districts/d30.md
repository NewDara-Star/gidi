## 1. What the district is, and what it is not

- **The frame's d30** is "Isolo-Ladipo": Isolo, Isolo north-east, Cele, Ladipo, Papa Ajao, Ilasamaja, Ajao Estate;
  borders the Isolo canal south, the Oshodi-Apapa Expressway, the airport's south fence [EC]. Frame outline 1.29 km²
  in game (11.6 km² real), 85 points, bbox x 11325-12992, y 4858-6692 [OB][LIV].
- **Truth check on its working name: there is almost no Ladipo left in it.** The owner moved Mùṣá's border north-west on
  23 Oct "so the whole Ladipo market is in Mùṣá" [D15]. The drawn Mùṣá outline takes 0.168 km² of d30's frame (x 12142-
  12992, y 5236-6342), and with it Papa Ajao (the frame's place point falls inside drawn Mùṣá) and every Overture
  vehicle-parts pin: 42 vehicle_parts_store, 34 automotive_service and 27 auto_dealer pins lie in the cut, 3, 14 and 10
  in what is left [OV]. OSM's "Ladipo Market" point (6.5442, 3.3418) is in Mùṣá [OSM]. Ladipo and Papa Ajao are in
  Mushin LGA [W-LM][LGA]; what is left is Oshodi-Isolo's Isolo, Ajao Estate and Ilasamaja wards [W-OI].
- **What is left (1.124 km² game, ~10.1 km² real)** is four things: the old **Isolo town** round the Oba's palace and
  the central mosques; the **Isolo Industrial Estate** strip west of the expressway (Promasidor, warehouses); **Ajao
  Estate** by the airport road, with airport hotels; **Cele and Ilasamaja** at the south end. And a fifth that is not
  Isolo at all (next point).
- **Truth check: the north-east lobe is Oshodi, not Ladipo.** The frame's "Ladipo" and "Ilasamaja" place points
  (6.5439, 3.3500 and 6.5503, 3.3457) sit in a lobe of about 0.25 km² game (corrected on check: was 0.23; 0.246 km² east of the expressway's motorway line) between the expressway and Agege Motor Road,
  north of drawn Mùṣá [OB]. What stands there is a block of federal compounds at Cappa, Oshodi: the Federal Institute
  of Industrial Research (FIIRO, "3 FIIRO Road, near Cappa bus stop, off Agege Motor Road, Oshodi") [FIIRO], the
  Nigerian Armed Forces Resettlement Centre (a 47.5 ha real barracks polygon, 42.8 ha of it inside the line (corrected on check: was "a 43 ha polygon")), Digital Bridge Institute, NIMET, a Federal
  Ministry of Housing pin, the Federal Neuro-Psychiatric Hospital Oshodi and a Federal Medical Centre point [OV][OSM].
  Every other source puts Ilasamaja 3.5 km south, by Cele (section 4). So the frame's two labels there are misplaced
  (*guess*: gazetteer points carried into the frame).
- **Truth check: the frame outline reaches into the Oshodi interchange.** Terminal 1 (6.5549, 3.3512) and Terminal 2
  (6.5558, 3.3508) of the Oshodi Transport Interchange fall inside d30's frame outline, 30 m and 5 m from d32's line;
  Terminal 3 and the Oshodi Red Line station fall in d29's [OSM][OB]. The interchange is Ọ̀ṣọ́dé's by name and by its
  LIVING sheet [LIV]. Where its terminals go is a border call for the owner, not this note's.
- The name Ìṣọ́lẹ̀, "keeping watch over the land" (NAMES.md), sits well on the old town with its own Oba [MIR][WILL] (that it is an Awori town: not checked, neither page says Awori).

## 3. Main roads (from the frame, OSM and Overture; widths mostly not known)

Frame-study roads inside what is left, game metres of line [OB]: Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway 2,578 (primary pieces)
+ 2,279 (motorway) + 134 (bridge); Mushin Road 1,214; Agege Motor Road 1,168 (trunk); unnamed motorway 854; Osolo Way
802; unnamed primary 533; standard-gauge rail 499 + unnamed rail 158; International Airport Road 454 + 355; Okota
Road 333; Egbe Road 125; Isolo Road 81 + 67 (bridge). 12.1 km in all. Overture adds 23.7 km of residential and 15.7 km
of service road inside the line [OV].

- **Oshodi-Apapa Expressway** (Overture and the frame call it Apapa-Oworonshoki): motorway, ref F265, one-way
  carriageways, with "primary" frontage roads beside it [OSM]. Built 1975-1978 as the road to Tincan and Apapa ports;
  it "virtually collapsed"; rebuilt by Dangote Group / Hitech from April 2019, "virtually ready" April 2021 [W-OAE].
  Mùṣá measured the whole corridor at **41 m** at 6.52569, 3.33561 (z19), split as 25 m of carriageways and median plus
  a 7 m service road each side (the split is an estimate) [D15]. A high median that pedestrians climb over; residents
  ask for footbridges at Toyota and Iyana-Itire [GUA-H] (not checked: Guardian 403). Stops named along it: Cele, Ilasa(maja), Iyana-Isolo, Five
  Star, Toyota, Oshodi [GUA-H][NM-A][WV-O]; that order south to north is partly *guess* (Ilasamaja lies between Cele
  and Berliet [GUA-H], not checked; Five Star is by Aswani [NM-A]); positions of Five Star and Toyota not found.
- **International Airport Road**: motorway, ref F269, two lanes per carriageway on the ways tagged [OSM]; the LGA brief
  calls it 10-lane, works begun late 2017 [LGA]. It runs along d30's north edge (6.549-6.563 N) and meets the expressway
  at Overture's "Airport Interchange" (12190, 5135) [OV].
- **Agege Motor Road**: trunk, ref A5, one-way pieces of two lanes [OSM]; Mùṣá measured 21.5 m (two carriageways and a
  striped kerbed median) at 6.53379, 3.35349 [D15]. In d30 only along the north-east lobe, with the rail beside it.
- **Mushin Road** (Overture's name for the road from Isolo town east past Iyana-Isolo and on to Mushin; 6.0 km real in
  all): primary, one-way pieces west, secondary further on [OV][OSM]. The "Isolo-NNPC road" works: both sides from
  Iyana-Isolo to Isolo General Hospital done in June 2025, then stalled [GUA-H snippet] (not checked: Guardian 403). *Guess*: that stretch is this
  road west of the expressway.
- **Isolo Road**: primary [OSM]; in Overture it runs only east of the expressway (6.528-6.532 N, 3.334-3.354 E), crossing
  it on a 288 m bridge [OV][D15]; 16 m kerb to kerb in Mùṣá [D15]. "The Iyana-Isolo overhead bridge" on the expressway
  was shut 7-9 a.m. on 10 Dec 2023 for inspection [NM-B].
- **Osolo Way**: secondary, 2.4 km real, north-south from about 6.530 to 6.551, from the Iyana-Isolo side up to the
  Airport Road at Ajao Estate [OV]; a diversion route "Mushin Road to Osolo Way, then Airport Road to Oshodi" [NM-B];
  restaurants and Aswani market listed on it [WV-I]. The district's own spine (*guess*).
- **Okota Road**: primary, one-way pieces of two lanes (a dual road), 4.4 km real, from Isolo junction (about 6.529,
  3.318) south through Cele [OSM][OV]. "Cele Roundabout, access Cele Bridge to Okota Road" [NM-B]. GIGM's "Cele/Okota
  Terminal, Isolo" is at 103 Okota Road [WV-I][WV-O].
- **Egbe Road**: primary, one-way pieces, from Isolo junction west to Ejigbo [OSM][OV].
- **Isolo junction** (*guess* at the name): Overture traffic signals named "Isolo Ejigbo/Mushin Road" at about 6.5291,
  3.3180 (11543, 5875), where Egbe Road, Okota Road and Mushin Road meet, about 85 game m (250 m real) from the General Hospital [OV][OSM].
- **Industrial estate roads**: Cowbell Way (tertiary, 2 lanes, two-way, 60 km/h; 6.5264-6.5270 N) [OSM][OV];
  "Isolo Industrial Estate Road which links to Apapa-Oshodi Expressway" [IFC]; Abimbola Way (1.2 km real), Chivita
  Avenue (unpaved), Aswani Road, "Aswani Mechanic Village" (a named residential way) [OV].
- **Town streets**: Akinbaiye Street (the palace street, unclassified, unpaved), Baale Shekoni Street, Baale Street,
  Kogberegbe, Okeho, Church Street; Ajao Estate's Lateef Salami Street, Asa Afariogun Street; Ire-Akari Estate Road;
  Godwin Omonua Street [OV][OSM]. Most residential pieces are tagged unpaved [OV] (*guess*, as in d16: many are worn tar).
- **Rail**: the Lagos-Ibadan standard-gauge line and an unnamed line run along Agege Motor Road in the north-east lobe
  [OB][OV]; no station inside (Oshodi station is at 6.5573, 3.3514, in d29's frame) [OSM].
- Widths: none tagged in OSM or Overture for any road here [OSM][OV]; only Mùṣá's three measures above.

## 5. What it is like

- **Fabric** [OB] (places.csv, real-scale figures): Isolo, dense core, 3,054 buildings/km², median footprint 86 m²,
  mean height 5.7 m, grid 0.48 (organic); Cele, dense core, 2,464/km², 96 m², 6.7 m, grid 0.58; Isolo north-east
  (the industrial estate side), "industry", 1,897/km², 39% big roofs, 7.9 m; Ajao Estate, planned grid 0.74, 2,328/km²,
  99 m²; the Cappa lobe ("Ladipo" and "Ilasamaja" in the frame), "industry", 991-1,385/km², half its roofs big, 8.8-9.2 m.
  So: an organic old town in the south-west, a factory strip along the expressway, a gridded estate in the north, and
  the federal compounds in the north-east.
- **Land use** [OV]: of the outline, 52% residential and 21% industrial by Overture's polygons (Overture also has a
  stray "Pacific Islands Heritage Marine National Monument" polygon over the whole box: noise).
- **Houses**: self-contain rooms, block-of-flats, bungalows and older duplexes, "older industrial zones quieter at
  night" [LGA]. LIVING: people packed at 40k/km² in the homes between the factories; class C [LIV].
- **People**: a kingdom with its own Oba, the Osolo [MIR][WILL] ("Awori": not checked, neither page says it); Isolo LCDA [W-OI]. The Igbo-trader picture in
  LIVING belongs to Ladipo [LIV][LGA], which is now Mùṣá's.
- **Trades**: factories and warehouses on the estate (food, steel fabrication, building materials around Promasidor)
  [IFC]; logistics and storage firms (37 Overture pins) and fashion shops (63) [OV]; Aswani's used-clothes trade
  [NM-A][PUL]; Cele's food and goods market [WV-O]; airport hotels [OV].
- **Moving**: okada banned in Oshodi-Isolo and Isolo LCDA since 1 Sept 2022 [LGA]; keke on most roads, ₦100-300
  [WV-I]; danfo and long-distance coaches from Cele (GIGM) [WV-O]; trucks from the ports on the expressway [W-OAE][ST].
- **By hour** [IFC][WV-O][PUL][LGA]: factory shifts round the clock at Promasidor [IFC] (LIVING's 7 and 19 shift change
  is from knowledge [LIV]); Aswani's market day is Tuesday [PUL] (corrected on check: was "busiest on Tuesdays"; Pulse says only "usually held on
  Tuesdays"); Cele market open "as late as 11pm" [WV-O] (the late-afternoon start [GUA-H]: not checked); the
  expressway jammed between Iyana-Isolo and Toyota, once for "close to three hours" over one pothole [GUA-H] (not checked: 403); Friday
  prayers at the central mosques (*guess*).
- **Water**: Ilasamaja is low-lying and floods every rainy season, fed from the Aswani / Iyana-Isolo axis through a
