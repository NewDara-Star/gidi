## 1. What the district is, and what it is not

- Members: Ilupeju, and "Shogunle, east of Agege Motor Road" [EC row 29][OB]. Frame outline 0.513 km² in game (4.62
  km² real at 1:3), one ring of 48 points, bbox x 12575-13342, y 4275-5458 (6.540-6.5725 N, 3.346-3.367 E) [OB].
- **Truth check on its working picture: most of the outline is not "the gentle town".**
  - **The north 37% is an army base.** OSM's "Ikeja Cantonment" (alt name Nigerian Army Cantonment, landuse military)
    covers 191,086 m² of the 513,494 m² outline; 92.6% of that polygon lies in d29, 7.3% in d35, 0.1% in d34 [OSM][OB].
    So the place the frame calls "Shogunle, east of Agege Motor Road" (fabric "barracks" [OB]) **is** the cantonment,
    and the district named "Ikeja GRA-Cantonment" (d34) holds almost none of it. Overture also puts an "Ikeja Millitary
    Cantonment" point at 6.5618, 3.3608 (game 13121, 4668) [OV].
  - Real facts on it: home of HQ 9 Brigade [W-IC][PRN]; renamed "T.A. Lagbaja Cantonment, Ikeja" on 2 Mar 2025, with a
    refurbished main gate, new digital boards, remodelled NCO quarters [PRN]; the 27 Jan 2002 armoury explosion began
    as a fire in a street market next to the base, killed at least 700 to over 1,100, most in the panic and stampedes
    [W-IC] ([W-AX] says at least 1,100 and gives no 700: attribution corrected on check), many drowned in a canal
    "parallel to the Isolo-Oshodi expressway" [W-AX][LGA 1224, 1639]. The repo's LGA notes already
    say: never the real victims, never the cantonment's real name [LGA 1684]. *Guess*: the canal of 2002 is Oke-Afa, in
    Ìṣọ́lẹ̀/Òkòtó's land, not in d29 (W-AX names no canal; the LGA note says Oke-Afa).
  - **A further 3% is the army's market**, "The Arena" (Nigerian Army Shopping Arena), 15,489 m², wholly in d29, by
    Agege Motor Road [OSM]; commissioned 14 Apr 2009, container shops selling bales of clothes, burned 30 Dec 2025 and
    the August before [S-AR]; Bolade Market stands opposite it [RIP].
  - **About 25% is industrial land** (two OSM industrial polygons, 80,332 and 50,077 m², both wholly inside) [OSM]:
    the Ilupeju Industrial Estate, along Agege Motor Road and the bypass [LM][LIV]. Today it is light manufacturing,
    packaging and offices; heavy plants (Nigerian Foundries, Nispo Porcelain) have moved out to Otta and Agbara [LM].
    Nestlé Nigeria's and GSK's head offices are on Industrial Avenue [S-CO] (offices, not factories, per the same
    summaries).
  - **The west edge takes in Oshodi's rail station and Terminal 3 of the Oshodi bus interchange** (section 4) [OSM].
  - The rest, the residential Ilupeju estate on both sides of Coker Road (corrected on check: was "west of Coker
    Road"; Overture puts Johnson, Olutanda, Ilaka and Ashimowu Jibowu Streets east of it, see section 3), is the
    "relatively peaceful area" of [DT]. **Part of
    real residential Ilupeju is not in d29**: the streets east of Coker Road and south of y 5025 (Aina George, Oba Nle
    Aro, Olusoji Idowu's south end, Association Avenue) are in Ṣọmọlá's (d27) drawn outline, among them the Indian
    Language School (Wikipedia's point 6.55188, 3.36416 = game 13245, 5033) [W-ILS][MAP].
- LGA: Mushin [NHM][LM addresses "Ilupeju Industrial Estate, Mushin"]; Overture puts the Odi-Olowo/Ojuwoye LCDA's office
  on Town Planning Way (13104, 4856) [OV]. LIVING's "[K, check]" on the LGA can be closed as Mushin.
- The name: NAMES.md row 29 gives Ìlúpẹ̀lẹ́, "the gentle town" (sound-alike of Ilupeju). The evidence says the gentle
  part is the estate core only; whether the cantonment stays a base in Gidi is the owner's call (Gidi is its own city),
  but the note must say the real north third is a walled army base.

## 3. Main roads (frame, OSM, Overture; real widths mostly not known)

Frame roads inside the outline, game metres [OB]: rail 2,308 (Lagos-Ibadan SGR 1,415 + an unnamed line 893);
Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway 2,178 (motorway 1,078, secondary 1,100, the service roads); Town Planning Way 669; Ikorodu
Road 946 (primary 584, motorway 362); Agege Motor Road 423; Coker Road 369; unnamed motorway 263 and secondary 261. 7.5
km in all. Overture has 24.3 km of road inside, 15.1 km of it unnamed [OV].

- **Agege Motor Road** (the west edge; trunk, ref A5, one-way pieces of 2 lanes, so a split carriageway; paved) [OSM].
  The frame's outline follows it from y 4400 to 5400, a median 4 m from its nearest carriageway line, 81% of the edge
  within 10 m, at most 23 m (corrected on check: was "to within a few metres") [OB][OV]. Mùṣá drew it 21.5 m [MAP]. Firms
  have addresses on it: Vitapur (326/328), Haffar (361), Biostadt (387) [LM].
- **The rail corridor runs inside d29 along the whole west edge** (y 4358-5458), its west line 3-7 game m east of
  Agege Motor Road's east carriageway line from y 4400 to 5350, 21 m at y 5400 (corrected on check: was "5-30 game m";
  measured on Overture's lines) [OB][OV]: the
  standard-gauge Lagos-Ibadan line (2 passenger lines, gauge 1435) and a second line OSM tags 1067 mm narrow gauge
  (Overture calls all three pieces standard gauge: the sources disagree) [OSM][OV]. The Red Line "shares the
  right-of-way with the Lagos–Kano Standard Gauge Railway" [W-RL] (corrected on check: [W-RL] names Lagos–Kano; the
  note had set it under the Lagos-Ibadan line); Mùṣá read it as 4 tracks between concrete walls [MAP].
- **Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway** (motorway, ref F265, one-way carriageways, bridge pieces) [OSM]: crosses the middle

  4790-4886 (corrected on check: was x 12803-13347, y 4791-4888) [OV]; for most of its
  length "a six-lane expressway with two one-way service roads parallel to the expressway" [W-AOE]. Overture's service
  roads under the same name span y 4734-4908 [OV]. Gbàgede drew it 25 m [MAP]. It is the line between the two member
  places (`borders.csv`: Shogunle east | Ilupeju, big road 1.85 km) [OB].
- **Town Planning Way** (secondary, two-way and one-way pieces, paved) [OSM]: x 12821-13339, y 4840-4911, just south of
