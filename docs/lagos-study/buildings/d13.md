# d13 Sọ̀rọ̀lérè: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Sọ̀rọ̀lérè is drawn (`map/d13/`: 738 houses, 15.1% cover); this
compares the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline. The owner was born at Randle General
Hospital, Surulere: its ground has its own section below.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651) from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`: each building's median height over pixels with
presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (under 4.5 m 1 storey, 4.5-7.5 2, 7.5-10.5 3, then on by 3 m).
[OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail), `overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m.
[GM] Google Maps satellite and labels, looked at by eye on 27 Oct (study, never copied). [SV] Google Street View,
one panorama (Oct 2019), looked at by eye.

**How** (re-run: `docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_blocks.py d13 --json x.json`, then
`d17_fabric.py x.json d13`; the grounds and Randle: `d13_landmarks.py d13`):

- Real street blocks: the outline less Overture's street segments (motorway to living_street, unclassified included;
  service roads, paths and footways not) and the rail lines, each cut at an **assumed** full width (motorway 30 m,
  trunk 24, primary 18, secondary 14, tertiary 10, residential and unclassified 7, rail 10; not measured, a divider
  only). **174 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 75 slivers under that (19,377 m² together) are left out of the table.
  The cut streets take 12.9% of the outline at those widths.
- Per block: as d17.md (centroid-in count at 90%, footprint union clipped to the block, median and largest footprint,
  2.5D heights, Overture places and land use, our houses and roof cover in the same block in Gidi metres, and the share
  of the Gidi block under our carriageways).
- The fabric column is `d17_fabric.py`'s rule draft (its FABRIC order is in d17.md). Three blocks are set by hand from

- `d13_landmarks.py` (written for this study; it imports `d17_blocks.py`) measures each non-residential Overture

- Overture's land-use layer carries a mis-placed polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box; it is

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The gap is cover, and it has to be found between our roads.** Google's 33.5% against our 15.1%. Our carriageways
   take 33.3% of the outline against 12.9% for the real streets at the assumed widths, so the land left between our
   roads must be about 50% roofed (33.6 / 66.7) to reach Google's low bound, which is what the real median block holds
   (50%). Our median block is 20%; only 8 of 174 blocks reach Google's. In count: 738 houses against about 1,270
   (11,429 / 9) at 1:3.
2. **Sọ̀rọ̀lérè is denser and lower-grained than Saba**: whole cover 33.5% (Saba 29.0%), median block 50% (Saba 45%),
   median footprint 135 m² (Saba 143). 62% of the readable heights are 2 storeys, 27% 1 storey (Saba 25%), 8% 3.
   21 blocks read as 1-storey bungalow blocks (block median height under 4.5 m); 16 of them sit together in the
   middle of the district (Gidi x 12930-13340, y 6675-6890: D13-42, -43, -58, -66 to -68, -73, -75, -83 to -85, -89,
   -96 to -98, -107), with median footprints of 70-120 m² at 36-66% cover. Our draft has face_me_bungalow 156 of 738 (21%); the real 1-storey share is 27%.
3. **The furthest short** (Google's cover less ours, in Gidi m² of roof): D13-123 (the stadium complex's edges;
   9,197), D13-141 (the big south-west block, 1,087 buildings; ours 68; 7,390), D13-86 (railway land and Tejuosho;
   7,308), D13-06 (north, 578 buildings, ours 34; 6,870), D13-88 (ours 6% against 36%; 4,713), D13-142, D13-27,
   D13-87, D13-116, D13-138, D13-46, D13-59 (Randle's block; 2,020). The full list is below the table.
4. **The landmarks, measured on their own** (`d13_landmarks.py`; real m² inside the outline):
   - **Tejuosho Shopping Centre** (Overture retail, 52,883 m²): 59 Google buildings at **65% cover**, the tallest
     ground in the district (2.5D storeys 3: 13, 4: 10, 6: 10, 7: 8, 8: 6, 5: 2, 9: 1, 10: 1) and the district's
     largest footprint (19,461 m²), a multi-storey market building; 59 Overture places (clothing, fabric, jewellery,
     computers). Ours: a market plot with 5 houses (walkup 3, 3 storeys) at 18%. Not looked at on the satellite.
   - **The railway land** by it (Overture railway, 140,406 m²: 132 buildings, 34%; Yaba Railway Station is named in
     D13-86) and a commercial strip (29,583 m², 37 buildings of median 50 m², 1-storey mostly: stalls, *guess*).
   - **The National Stadium complex** (D13-123, 752,454 m², 17% cover): the bowl (Overture stadium 38,559 m², 3 Google
     buildings: the stands, 3,363-4,835 m²), Teslim Balogun Stadium (the 7,373 m² roof on Overture grass and pitches),
     Legacy Pitch, the indoor sports hall, dark ponds, Overture wetland 120,262 m² and farmland 38,770 m² with 1 and 2
     buildings [GM, OV]. Ours: 33 houses at 6% and a compound plot. Its open ground is real; the short roof is at its
     edges (618 Google buildings in the block, mostly on the streets round it).
   - **Dorman Long Engineering** (Overture industrial, 12,457 m²: 8 buildings, 19%, 2-3 storeys) in D13-01; ours: a
     compound plot, no house inside.
   - **Royal Estate** (D13-162): rows of long salmon-roofed slab blocks [GM], 2.5D 14 of 29 readable at 4 storeys or
     more; 17% cover only because the estate keeps open ground between the rows. Ours: 1 house, 4%.
   - **Government College Lagos** (D13-169, 97,385 m²): long blue-roofed classroom blocks round open ground [GM], 17%.
   - 10 school grounds measured (Overture school, 4,578-32,246 m²; our map walls 11): 15-51% cover, 1-3 storeys, median roofs 150-418 m²;
     5 of them hold no house of ours inside, though Google has 4-25 buildings in each (`d13_landmarks.py` output).
5. **Randle General Hospital is a campus of many low blocks, not two big ones** (section below). Our cover on its
   ground already matches (39% against 42%), but in 2 houses of 388 and 431 m² (our houses are real size), each about a third of the real main block's 1,313 m²; the real ground is a campus of many low blocks.

## Randle General Hospital

Where it is. Overture's land use 'hospital' named Randle General Hospital: 18,984 m² real inside the outline,
centroid 6.50805 N 3.35750 E (Gidi 13000, 6648), about 167 m east-west by 182 m north-south (bounding box); 87% of it
(16,534 m²) lies in block D13-59, it is 14% of that block. Its edges are Randle Avenue (tertiary) on the west, Ishola
Street on the north-east, Abina Street on the south, Oni Street at its east tip [OV segments within 0 m]. Two Overture
hospital points stand on it: "Randle General Hospital", 48 Randle Ave (confidence 0.79), and "Lagos State General
Hospital", 68 Randle Ave (0.66). Google Maps labels the same wedge "Randle General Hospital Surulere" and "Lagos State
General Hospital" [GM, 27 Oct]. A third Overture point named "Randle General Hospital" with the address Gbaja St
(6.50436 N 3.35970 E, confidence 0.81) stands 382 m south-east of the ground, in the same block; no building is
labelled so there on Google Maps at the view looked at, and it is taken for a mis-placed point (*not checked* on the
ground).

What is on it [GOB, 25D]: **46 buildings at 42% cover**, median 112 m², 1-2 storeys (16 one-storey, 24 two, 1 three
of 41 read).
- **The main block**, at the north by Randle Avenue (6.50840 N 3.35726 E): 1,313 m², about 32 by 41 m, 9.0 m high
  (3 storeys). On the satellite it is the one big terracotta (orange) roof in the wedge [GM].
- **Its neighbours**: a 768 m² block south of it (about 32 by 40 m, 4.5 m, 2 storeys), two of 447 and 415 m² east of
  it (7.0 m, 2 storeys; on the satellite a pale blue roof east of the orange one), and a 313 m² block between.
- **The south half**: about 15 pavilion-like blocks of 100-300 m², long and narrow (6-16 m by 14-32 m), 1-2 storeys
  (3.0-6.5 m), in rows with open courts between them [GOB footprints; GM: grey roofs with courtyards, a long blue-grey
  roof, two small orange roofs at the south edge by Abina Street].
- **Small things**: 20 buildings under 100 m² (gatehouses, kiosks, sheds, *guess*), the smallest 7 m².
- **The street front** [SV, Randle Ave, Oct 2019, the panorama at 66 Randle Ave]: a low rendered wall with pillars
  behind big shade trees (one a mango in fruit), a round sign on a post with a stack of department boards at the gate,
  and behind it a beige 2-storey block whose upper floor is a pierced breeze-block screen. Lagos yellow taxis parked
  nose-in along the wall. Trees line the Randle Avenue side on the satellite too [GM].
- Around it within 150 m [OV]: Ansar-ud-Deen Grammar School (3 m), Surulere Hospital (11 m), shops, churches and a
  hotel; Randle Mechanic Village 125 m off on Randle Avenue.

Ours (map/d13, hand pass 5, BUILDINGS.md): a compound plot over Overture's ground less the carriageways, a wall and a
gate, a clinic place, and two houses: b-rgh4b6ee, walkup, 3 storeys, 388 m² (real size), and b-rgh3f25f,
face_me_bungalow, 1 storey, 431 m² (real size). Cover on the ground: ours 39%, real 42%. The real main
block is 1,313 m² (146 Gidi m²); at Gidi's 1:3 the real ground holds about 5 buildings (46 / 9). What a hand pass could
draw from this, as study only: the 3-storey main block at the north by Randle Avenue, a 2-storey block south of it,
and 2-3 low ward rows with courts in the south half, with trees on the Randle Avenue wall. The hospital house type is
already owed (BUILDINGS.md hand pass 5).

(real rows of 4+-storey slabs, ours one house), the 5 school grounds with no house of ours, and Randle (cover right,
grain wrong).
