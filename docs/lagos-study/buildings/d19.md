(Makoko, Iwaya and the rest) stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Makoro is drawn (`map/d19/`: 286

## Land or water: the data

- **Overture cannot say**: its land/land polygon is the land side of the sea coastline and holds the whole Lagos
  Lagoon as land (3.41 E 6.49 N is inside it), so every building reads "on land" there.
- **WorldCover 2021 calls the stilt town built-up**: 89% built, 2% water in D19-31; 30 of Google's buildings stand on
  a water pixel in the whole district. It does not tell stilts from land.
- **The DSM flattens the open lagoon to exactly 0 m** just east of the outline; inside the outline only 4 of Google's
  8,615 buildings (90%) stand on a 0 m pixel. So the outline stops where the open water starts, and the stilt town is
  inside it.
- **The DSM is under 3 m beneath 5,942 of the 8,615 buildings** (cells: 163 of 250 "low", 1.53 km²). Low ground and
  water under stilts both read low, so this alone does not separate them.
- **The grain does.** Cells whose Google median footprint is under 50 m² ("fine") form one wedge in the south-east,
  69 of its 72 cells in the low zone; by eye on the satellite [GM] at 6.4965 N 3.3925 E, the same wedge is roofs of
  about 5-8 m packed between dark water channels, with the open lagoon and loose houses on it beyond (outside the
  outline). At 6.4935 N 3.3880 E a channel running north-west to south-east splits larger rusty compound roofs on its
  west (land) from the fine roofs on its east. [WP]: "A third of the community is built on stilts along the lagoon and
  the rest is on the land"; four of its six villages (Adogbo, Migbewhe, Oko Agbon, Yanshiwhe) are on the water, two
  (Apollo, Sogunro) on land.

**Reading: Makoro's east is a stilt town over the water, about 0.66 km² real (28% of the outline, near Wikipedia's
third), east of about Gidi x 14,000-14,150 (cell centres) to the border, y 6,900 to 7,320, with a thin strip on the
east edge reaching north to y 6,750; the rest is land.** The edge is a judgement from the grain, the DSM and a look, not a surveyed shoreline.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The stilt town is short by an order of magnitude in houses.** Google sees 3,483 buildings there at 90% and 7,636
   at any confidence (more than double: stilt roofs come out at low confidence); at Gidi's 1:3 that is 390 to 850
   houses where we have 65. By eye the roofs are nearly continuous between the channels, so the any-confidence 42% is
   nearer than the 90% figure's 24% (*by eye, not measured*). Small: median 37 m², 55% under 40 m².
2. **Our stilt-town houses are the wrong kinds and too tall.** 18 of our 26 walk-ups and 32 compound houses stand in
   the fine wedge; the 2.5D reads a median 8.0 m there (3 storeys) against 5.0 m on the land, and 991 of the 3,271
   readings over the water are 4 storeys or more. STUDY.md already flags this ("Heights on water are suspect").
   All 14 of our 4-storey houses (every one a walkup, at Gidi 14231-14342, 7041-7222) stand in the wedge's cells,
   where BUILDINGS.md's 8 tall readings were. The water side wants
   `shack` (home) of 1-2 storeys on platforms, not walk-ups. **The map has no field for a house on stilts over water**
   (`docs/MAP-FORMAT.md` building: no water level or stilt; a `ground` patch can be `water` with a `z`); that is a
   schema gap to raise, not to work round.
3. **The channels are the streets.** Overture has no street in the east 1.31 km²; the satellite shows dark water
   channels between rows of roofs, the widest running north-west to south-east. Our east half has houses with no roads
   between them (BUILDINGS.md); it needs water lanes (canoe ways) drawn by hand on the channels' lines after a closer
   study, then the houses packed along them.
4. **The land side is packed 2-storey compounds**: the 32 land blocks (0.86 km²) hold 2,548 buildings at a median block
   cover of 50% (32%-77%), block median footprints around 150 m²; ours there are 141 houses. Furthest short: D19-13
   (Iwaya, 8,012 m² of roof short), D19-28 (5,814), D19-09 (2,757); in D19-04, D19-20, D19-29 and D19-30 ours are
   5-14% against 49-67%.
5. **The water edge has moved since the data.** Open Buildings v3 predates the clearances that began on 23 December
   2025 along a high-tension line [AN]: reports give about 3,000 homes taken down by 20 January 2026 and a cleared band
   that grew from 30 m to a reported 522 m from the line (search summary; not read in full). Overture's power line
   crosses the outline for 588 m real along the east edge, Gidi x 14,431-14,450, y 6,775-6,968, with 71 of Google's
   buildings within 30 m of it. Whether Gidi shows the town before or after is the owner's choice (LIVING.md sheet 19
   already treats the clearances with care).
