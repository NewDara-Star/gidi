# d27 Ṣọmọlá (Somolu-Bariga): the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 28 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Ṣọmọlá is drawn (`map/d27/`: 1,988 houses, 16.7% cover); this
compares the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651), read from line 257 of
`thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`. [25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`:
each building's median height over pixels with presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (under 4.5 m 1
storey, 4.5-7.5 2, 7.5-10.5 3, then on by 3 m). [OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail),
`overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m. [GM] Google Maps satellite with labels and Street View, looked at
by eye on 28 Oct (study, never copied).

- The shared block script `d17_blocks.py` (written for d17 and d19), unchanged. The outline is `map/d27/district.json`

- Real street blocks: the outline less Overture's street segments (motorway to living_street; 2,839 segments) and 3
  rail segments, each cut at an **assumed** full width (motorway 30 m, trunk 24, primary 18, secondary 14, tertiary 10,
  residential and unclassified 7, rail 10; a divider only, not measured). **516 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 80
  slivers under that (32,706 m² together) are left out. The cut streets take 14.2% of the outline at those widths.
- Per block: Google buildings whose centroid is in the block (90% threshold), their footprint union clipped to the block
  over the block's area (cover), median and largest footprint, heights and storeys [25D], Overture places and land
  use, and our houses (centroid in the block taken to Gidi metres, roof cover clipped to it, and the share of the Gidi
  block under our own carriageways). Our houses are compared as drawn (real size); cover is a share, so the 1:3 scale
  does not enter it. Only the "about N at Gidi's scale" counts in the furthest-below list divide Google's count by 9
  (the block is a ninth of the area at 1:3).
- The fabric column is `d17_fabric.py`'s rule draft (institution land use over 40%, then cover under 12% open, small
  footprints shacks, big roofs, median height 7.5 m walk-ups, 8 places a hectare shops on the fronts, cover under 30%
  loose, median height under 4.5 m bungalows, else packed 2-storey compounds). Four d27 blocks have hand labels added
  to its OVERRIDE table, each marked ***guess***: they come from one look at the satellite at z17, not from a
  measurement (D27-166, D27-235, D27-338, D27-372). The other 512 are the rule's, **not checked block by block**.
- Compound house against face-me-I-face-you cannot be told apart from footprints and heights. The kinds column gives
  both; the split stays the drafter's (`draft_kinds.py`) or Street View's.
- Overture's land-use layer holds a mis-placed polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box; the
  fabric script skips it (`SKIP_LU`), and it is ignored here.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **Ṣọmọlá is dense, and ours is at well under half** (corrected by the main session: the agent had it the densest; Ìtìrí 47.0%, Mùṣá 43.3%, Ayégúnlẹ̀ 42.0% and Agunda 40.6% are denser). Google covers 39.2% of the
   outline (Saba 29.0%); ours 16.7%. Our carriageways take 35.1% of the outline (the real streets 14.2% at the assumed
   widths), so to reach 39.3% the land between our roads has to be about 61% roofed (39.3 / 64.9). The real median
   block is 55.6% roofed and the packed blocks 51% by area, so the fill has to be as tight as the tightest real
   blocks: party walls, no yards, lanes as the only gaps. Count: 27,553 real buildings is about 3,060 at 1:3 against
   our 1,988.
2. **It is a town of 2-storey compounds of about 145 m²**: 401 of 516 blocks, 7.23 km² of 12.0, are the rule's
   "packed 2-storey compounds"; 68% of readable heights are 2 storeys, 23% 1, 8% 3, and only 71 buildings in 25,589
   read 5 storeys or more. Our storeys already match (73% 2-storey). What is short is the count and the cover, in
   every fabric (table above: ours is 35-50% of Google's cover in every built row, the hospital 27%).
3. **Ebute Ilaje is a shack town on the lagoon, and our map has it at a quarter of its cover.** D27-372 (Gidi 14528,
   5691; 330,242 m² real) holds 1,359 buildings at 43% cover, median 70.6 m², 416 (31%) under 40 m², 11 with their
   centroid on a WorldCover water pixel. By eye [GM]: tiny roofs packed right to the water west of Third Mainland
   Bridge, some standing in the water (Overture "RCCG Ebute Ilaje"; no Street View there). Ours: 34 houses, 10%; the
   largest roof shortfall in the district (11,962 m² of roof in Gidi metres). It wants `shack (home)` packed at the
   water's edge (the map has no stilts field on a house: the `platform` object is a frontage's plinth, 0-1.5 m), with
   compounds inland.
4. **Ṣọmọlá is Lagos's printing quarter, and that is a ground-floor use, not a house type.** 149 Overture places are
   `printing_service` (the fourth commonest category); of the 124 named ones, 84 sit in blocks with centroids inside
   Gidi x 13350-13650, y 5400-5750 (median 13531, 5587), the west of the district round Akeju and Oguntolu
   Streets [GM]. Street View on Akeju Street (Jul 2021 [GM]): one-storey fronts with timber shutters and printed posters,
   an old 2-storey house with a balcony beside it, 3-storey flats behind. These are `compound_house` and
   `shop_under_flats` with `lock_ups` ground floors; the presses are a `use` for the life pass (not decided here).
5. **The open land is all on the lagoon side, east of the bridge, and it is real.** D27-166 (0.69 km² real: WorldCover
   water 23%, wetland 19%, grass 16%; 674 buildings, 10% cover) is Oworonshoki's lagoon edge: compounds on the land
   side (Oworo Palace, Gulf Flower International School [GM]), water and a scrub point the rest. D27-235 (0.12 km²,
   Overture brownfield 66%, WorldCover grass 64%, 8% cover) is open sand and grass fill; D27-338 (0.06 km², 13%) is Dr
   Kayode Okunade Estate [GM], detached houses on loop streets with open sand plots: no `house_type` in the schema is a
   detached house (nearest `compound_house`). Our cover there (11%, 8%, 2%) is already near or under Google's low
   figures: leave them open, fill the land-side edges. Inland, the big institution is the National Orthopaedic
   Hospital Igbobi (D27-407, Overture hospital land use 61%; by eye [GM] separate low blocks in green ground with a
   sports ground south, Igbobi College and Angus Memorial Senior High School beside it): 22% real, 6% ours.

## How our map differs, block by block

The last column of the table is ours: houses (centroid in the block), our roof cover of the Gidi block, and the share
of the Gidi block under our carriageways. Our kinds are compound_house 1,286, face_me_bungalow 311, shack 146, walkup
130, shop_under_flats 102, kiosk_row 10, shed 3. The real fabric agrees in kind (no tower blocks; 7 big-roof blocks,
0.04 km²; 7 walk-up blocks, 0.09 km², six of them in the west, Gidi x 13215-13465 (D27-53, 73, 244, 276, 277, 466), and D27-294 at 13987, 5580).
It differs in count and cover everywhere, and most at the lagoon (D27-372).

Thirteen blocks are over 100,000 m² real (26.4% of the outline, 5,947 of 27,553 buildings): there Overture has too
few streets to cut real blocks, and one "block" is many. D27-105 (360,058 m², 863 buildings) is north Bariga, which
by eye [GM] is a fine street grid with a canal along Shittu Animashaun Street, almost none of it in Overture's
dividers. The furthest-below list is led by these big blocks for that reason; read them as areas, not blocks.

  blocks); only D27-269's polygon is named ("Market"); none of the 7 was looked at.
