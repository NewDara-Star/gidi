# d16 Ìbùdó Mẹ́ta: the real buildings, block by block (Ebute Metta, Oyingbo, Otto, Iddo)

Study only, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent. Real names stay in this file and never go into `map/` records.
Every number below comes from `docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d16_blocks.py` (run it with `--md` to print the tables
again; about 15 s). Read alongside the district note `docs/lagos-study/districts/d16.md` and `map/d16/BUILDINGS.md`.

## How it was read

- **Outline**: `map/d16/district.json` (the frame-study ring, 475,276 m² in Gidi metres; real 4.28 km²). Real positions
  moved in with `scripts/working_bar.py`'s `gidi()`.
- **Blocks**: the outline less Overture's real streets (road classes motorway to unclassified, the same set as
  `working_bar.py`, plus rail lines), each line cut 0.5 m either side; the parts of a segment Overture flags `is_bridge`
  are left out, so the Third Mainland and Eko bridge decks do not split the ground under them. That gives 122 street
  blocks (`B001`-`B122`) and **five blocks too big to read whole** (over 8,000 m² Gidi), because Overture is missing the
  streets inside them: **M1** 149,308 m² (the whole west and south strip: the railway compound, Otto, Iddo), **M2**
  84,069 m² (the Oko-Baba waterfront), **M3** 29,504 m² (Iddo north of the market), **M4** 25,450 m² (Ebute Metta west,
  by the railway), **M5** 14,143 m² (the LSDPC estate in the north-east of the grid). Those were cut again by Overture's
  minor ways (service roads, paths), then by an 80 m Gidi (240 m real) square grid; a grid cell under 300 m² joined its
  neighbour. **The M cells are grid cells, not real blocks**: their edges are not streets. 259 rows in all over
  446,176 m²; 68 slivers under 300 m² (6,097 m², 1.3% of the outline) are dropped.
- **Buildings**: Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0) at the tile's own 90%-precision threshold, 0.749 (the 80% one,
  0.651, gives the upper cover figure), from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`, as `working_bar.py` does.
  Count = footprints whose centre is in the block; cover = footprints cut to the block; median and largest = Google's
  `area_in_meters` (real m²).
- **Storeys**: Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, tile `tile_qNSfrvnK9X8.tif` (it holds all of d16): each footprint's
  median height over its pixels with presence at least 0.5, bands read as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` reads them
  (band 2 height, band 3 presence; the run prints presence max 1.00, height max 53.0 m), storeys = max(1, (h + 1.5) // 3).
  5,501 of the 6,569 footprints at 90% in the window have a height. The 2.5D grid is an effective 4 m: Mùṣá's
  Street View check (draft_storeys.py's note) found 1-3 storey readings right and one tall reading wrong, so 4+ is
  the least sure band here.
- **Places and land use**: Overture places (point in block) and Overture land use (a polygon covering 10% of the block,
  or half of the polygon in it). One Overture land-use polygon whose box wraps the globe (a Pacific marine monument) is
  thrown out. Names that name another part of Lagos (Ladipo, Ikeja, Shomolu, Oworonshoki...) are marked
  "[names elsewhere]": they are mislocated or branch points, not what stands there.
- **Land cover**: ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m), pixel centres moved into Gidi metres; shown where a class is 10% or more.
- **Ours**: `map/d16/*.json` buildings with their centre in the block; our cover is our footprints cut to the block.
  The map is 1:3 in position but houses are real size, so at equal cover a Gidi block holds about a ninth of its real
  twin's houses: the count column gives Google's count and, in brackets, that count / 9.
- **Fabric (reading)**: a rule on the numbers, not a measurement; the rules, in order: water if water + wetland is
  half the block and under 5 houses; railway station if a station or terminus place is in it; railway land, cemetery,
  school ground from Overture land use (30% or more); open ground under 5 houses and under 10% cover; shacks and small
  sheds where 10 or more houses have a median under 60 m²; sheds and yards where land use is industrial or brownfield
  and there are under 15 houses per 1,000 m² Gidi; market by land use or a market place, at 25% cover or more; walk-up
  flats where the median reads 3 storeys or 40% read 3 or more; big old houses where the median footprint is 200 m² or
  more; otherwise compound houses and face-me-I-face-you. "Shops on the front" is added where the block touches a
  trunk, primary or secondary street or holds four or more shop-type places. The map kinds and ground floors are the
  schema's (`docs/MAP-FORMAT.md`: house kinds, ground floors) that such a fabric takes.

## Looked at on Google Maps (satellite, 18z, 27 Oct; study only, nothing copied)

Four views, by eye; **no Street View was opened**, and the satellite's date is not shown (credit: Airbus, CNES, Maxar).
- **Oko-Baba waterfront** (6.4870 N, 3.3910 E: M2-05, M2-16, M2-17): west of Tapa and Oko-Baba streets the grid of
  2-3 storey blocks; east of it, down to the water, a carpet of small rusty roofs on brown ground, with logs rafted in
  the lagoon. That is the sawmill and the Otumara settlement as the picture shows them, **before** the 2025 move
  and demolitions (study note [TRIB][GUA-H]). Google v3 there: 30-34 m² median, 10-18% cover, 1 storey (M2-05, M2-16).

  green open patches; car-trade labels on Maps (a motors dealer, a paint shop). Overture calls this land **brownfield**
  (M1-34 98%, M1-35 59%, M1-36 99%, M1-37 60%, M1-40 50%); Google v3 counts 70-152 buildings in each of those cells
  (medians 57-151 m²). It is lived in.
- **Ebute Metta grid and the railway compound** (6.4895 N, 3.3800 E: B004-B021, M1-08 to M1-13): east of Murtala
  Muhammed Way a regular grid (Borno Way, Simpson, Berkeley, McCullum, Moloney, Glover, Carter streets on the labels)
  of big rectangular roofs; Evans Square an open sand pitch; "Central Mosque" labelled on its north side. West of the

  along the creek, and big sheds to the south.

Not looked at: every other block; no Street View anywhere (fronts, ground floors and storeys are read from data only).

## What the district's fabric is

- **Google**: 6,518 buildings inside the outline (90% threshold), cover **26.49%** (90%) to **30.45%** (80%), median
  footprint **130 m²**; storeys read for 5,455 of those inside: 1 storey 1,198 (22%), 2 storeys 3,288 (60%), 3 storeys
  775 (14%), 4+ 194 (4%). Overture has 683 places inside.
- **A two-storey town.** 60% of buildings read 2 storeys. 3-4 storey walk-ups cluster in the middle of the Ebute
  Metta grid and along Murtala Muhammed Way: B025 (14 of 23 read 4+), B087 (13 of 32 read 4+), B001 and B028 (the
  LSDPC estate blocks: 20 of 24 and 6 of 6 read 3).
- **Three fabrics by place**:
  1. **The Ebute Metta / Ago Egba grid** (the street blocks B001-B114, centre and north-east): regular blocks of about
     1,150-1,250 m² Gidi (about 10,500 m² real, 100 m by 100 m), **41% cover**, 2,849 buildings, medians 120-330 m²,
     mostly 2 storeys with a 3-storey share; churches in almost every block (St Jude's cathedral and school B057,
     St Paul's Catholic B050, Bishop Tugwell Anglican B010, Christ Apostolic B034, dozens of smaller ones).
  2. **Otto and the west strip** (M1, M4): denser and smaller, median 57-150 m², 1-2 storeys, crooked lanes Overture
     does not have; the railway compound (M1-08 to M1-12) is the exception, 7-13% cover under trees, 1 storey.
  3. **The waterfronts** (M2 east, M3 south): shacks and small sheds (median 30-48 m² in M2's shack cells, 1 storey) at 5-18% cover, bare
     fill and water edge; Iddo's markets, the termini and yards.
- **Fabric mix (by block area, the reading above)**: compound houses and face-me-I-face-you 45% (122 blocks); shacks
  and small sheds 12% (14); big old houses 9% (30); sheds and yards 9% (18); walk-up flats 7% (32); open ground 7%
  (19) and 3% with a few houses (7); market 3% (4); water 1%; railway land and stations 2%; a school ground and a
  cemetery. (The full list is under the table.)

## How our map differs (map/d16 as of this run)

- **Ours: 395 houses, cover 11.39%**, median footprint 109 m²; Google 26.49-30.45% (working_bar.py prints the same
  three figures). At real house size the real cover needs about 6,518 / 9 = **724** houses; we have 395.
- **The shortfall is everywhere, not in one place.** Roof area short of Google's 90% cover, by area (table below):
  street blocks (B) 29,393 m² (ours 21% against 41%), M1 23,913 m² (10% against 26%), M2 7,640 (2% against 11%), M3
  3,804, M4 3,595, M5 2,477. In every fabric ours is 40-50% of Google's (compound houses 15% against 35%, walk-ups 16%
  against 39%, big old houses 18% against 35%), except the market blocks (10% against 42%) and the railway land and
  stations (0-2% against 27-45%: nothing drawn there).
- **Storeys match well**: ours 1 storey 69 (17%), 2: 253 (64%), 3: 65 (16%), 4: 8 (2%), against Google's 22 / 60 /
  14 / 4%.
- **Kinds**: ours compound_house 202, face_me_bungalow 56, walkup 56, shop_under_flats 55, shack 21, kiosk_row 2,
  shed 2, market_block 1. Missing kinds the real fabric asks for: `colonial_house` (0 drawn; the grid's big old houses,
  30 blocks, median 200 m² or more), `shed` beyond 2 (Iddo's yards and the termini: sheds and yards 9% of the area,
  plus the two stations), `station_hall` (0; Lagos Terminus), `church` (0 drawn as buildings, though churches are in
  most grid blocks; the places exist as points), `mosque` (0 as buildings).
- **Blocks that are empty on our map but built in Google** (ours 0 houses): e.g. M1-18 (16 buildings, 33%), M4-11
  (32 buildings, 44%, the Lagos Mainland Local Government point), M4-13 (19, 44%, NRC and FMC), M4-14 (19, 44%); nearly empty: M3-03
  (20, 51%, Iddo terminus: 1 house of ours), M1-53 (53 buildings, 47%, 1 house of ours), M1-48 (138 buildings, 58%: 1 house of ours).

## Five findings that matter for drawing

1. **Fill, do not re-shape.** Our cover is 11.39% against Google's 26.49-30.45%; the gap is about half in every
   fabric and every part, and the biggest piece of it (29,393 m² of roof) is the Ebute Metta grid's street blocks,
   then Otto (M1, 23,913 m²). Storeys already match (64% two-storey against 60%).
2. **The grid's houses are big and old**: medians 150-330 m² in B001-B114, many over 200 m², 2 storeys with a
   3-storey share; d16's map draws no `colonial_house` (the kind is in the schema) and draws the grid mostly as `compound_house`.
3. **Otto is lived-in, packed housing, not brownfield**: Overture's brownfield tag over M1-34 to M1-37 and M1-40
   (50-99% of each) meets 70-152 Google buildings per cell (medians 57-151 m², 1-2 storeys), and the satellite by eye shows dense roofs.
4. **The waterfronts in Google's data predate 2025**: M2 (Oko-Baba) reads shacks and sheds at 30-48 m², 1 storey, and
   the satellite shows the sawmill with logs in the water, but the sawmill moved in May 2025 and Otumara was demolished
   in March 2025 (district note). Google's cover there is not today's; how much to fill is the owner's call (he chose to
   clear the sawmill ground, `map/d16/BUILDINGS.md` hand pass 4).
5. **Named places the note missed are in the data**: the Oloto's palace (M3-01), Lisabi Hall (B026), two mosques
   (Ansur-ur-Deen at 13911, 7449; Masjid Ahlus-sunnah B015) and the railway compound's NRC, FMC and club (M4-13, M4-14);
   Lagos Terminus has two candidate points (M1-65 and M3-03).

## What could not be read

- Street View: not opened; ground floors (`lock_ups`, `home`...) are a reading from street class and places, not seen.
- The dates of Google v3's imagery per block (the dataset gives none per building here) and of the Maps satellite.
- Real blocks inside M1-M5: Overture is missing those streets, so their rows are 80 m grid cells.
- Storeys for 1,068 of the 6,569 footprints (no 2.5D pixels with presence 0.5 or more), including all of B121, B122
  and most open cells.
- Google v3 false positives: Evans Square (B014) holds one 3,154 m² footprint at confidence 0.945 where the satellite
  shows an open sand pitch, so B014's 59% cover is wrong by eye; others like it were not hunted.
- Overture place names are often wrong for the spot (13 blocks carry names of other districts); none of them is
  used as fact here.
