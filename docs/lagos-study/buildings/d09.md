stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Ayégúnlẹ̀ is drawn (`map/d09/`: 1,472 houses, 16.0% cover); this
compares the drawing with the real buildings inside the same outline. There is no district study note for d09 in
`docs/lagos-study/districts/`; the map's own notes (`map/d09/STREETS.md`, `BUILDINGS.md`) were read.

precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651) from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`: each building's median height over pixels with
presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (max(1, (h + 1.5) // 3)). [OV] Overture (places, land use, street
segments, rail), `overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m. [GM] Google Maps satellite with labels, looked at

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The "empty band" across the middle is not empty, and Overture's wetland is mostly houses.** `BUILDINGS.md` hand
   pass 4 left the band x 11,500-12,450, y 8,440-8,570 open as wetland and scrub (read in the data, not on the
   satellite). Google holds **1,733 buildings at 36% cover** in that band (1,111,500 m² real), WorldCover reads it 86%
   built; ours is 97 houses at 12%. Overture's wetland polygons in d09 (1,069,939 m² real) hold 1,713 Google buildings
   at 37% cover on 93% WorldCover built. On the satellite [GM] the band is a narrow green **canal** (labelled "canal",
   Canal Rd beside it) running east-west, with dense rusty-roofed compounds to its banks on both sides; only its
   **west end** is green ground with trees, beside Signals Barracks and a grey open lot (around 6.456 N 3.322 E, the
   west of D09-129). So: draw the canal as a narrow open strip and pack houses to it; keep open only the west end.
   This contradicts the band reading in `BUILDINGS.md` and NOW's owed item on painting the band as wetland scrub.
2. **Density: Ayégúnlẹ̀ is among the densest districts studied** (Google 42.0% cover; Ìtìrí 47.0%, Mùṣá 43.3%, Agunda 40.6%, Saba 29.0%; corrected by the main session, the agent had it the densest). Our roads take 31.9%
   of the outline, so to reach 42% the land left between our roads must be about 62% roofed (42.0 / 68.1), above the
   real median block (54.7%). In the middle half of the 236 packed blocks the real runs 20-28 houses a hectare, 49-65%
   cover, block median footprints 182-264 m². Filling up means packing every block to its real cover and more, plus
   the band (finding 1); count at 1:3 is about 2,480 houses against our 1,472.
3. **Ours is too low.** Real: 66% two-storey, 15.3% three and up (3,204 of 20,877); ours 60.5% two-storey, 7.7% three
   and up and 31.8% one-storey (real 18.7%). The 3-storey blocks are in the north-east by the Badagry Expressway and
   Boundary (D09-02, D09-10, D09-35, D09-41, D09-45: median height 7.5-8 m, 3-storey readings about equal to
   2-storey) and the south (D09-321 to D09-338, Gidi y 9,018-9,080). Readings of 5+ storeys (187) are not checked here; at
   D09-353 they are blur over school blocks and stands (`BUILDINGS.md` hand pass 2 checked six there).
4. **The "big roofs" are mostly long rooming houses, not sheds.** The rule calls 59 blocks "big roofs" (block median
   footprint 300 m² or more); their median of medians is 317 m², median height 5.5 m, median cover 62%. The one looked
   at closely (D09-331, Olodi-Apapa, z18 [GM]) is deep narrow plots with long rusty roofs end-on to the street, some
   2-3 storey: rooming houses that Google draws as long footprints. *Guess* that most of the 59 are the same; the
   sheds are where Overture says industrial or the labels say a yard (the Julius Berger yard in D09-201, a bare lot with
   long sheds by the Oworonshoki-Apapa Expressway). Our 15 sheds and 104 walkups should not grow to 59 blocks of
   sheds.
5. **The landmarks** [OV land use, GM labels]: Signals Barracks (Overture military, 301,356 m² real in d09, 18% cover;
   labelled "Nigeria Army Signal Barracks"), the Julius Berger yard (Overture industrial, part of 236,816 m², 19%
   cover), Boundary Market (37,382 m², 45% cover; our plot is its echo), the Olodi-Apapa school cluster and pitches on
   the creek (D09-353: Maracana Stadium and playing field, Reservation Secondary, Olodi-Apapa Junior Secondary, Mokoya
   Senior High, Bola Ige Millennium Model School, Odion Ighalo Stadium), Ajeromi General Hospital, Dorcas Memorial
   College School, the Ajeromi-Ifelodun Youth Recreation ground and Nathaniel Idowu Football Field near the canal
   (labels, 6.455-6.458 N), Alakoto Market and St Matthew Catholic Church in Amukoko (labels east of D09-17's canal; which block
   holds them not checked), Ajeromi
   Ifelodun Senior High School (D09-242). Canals run through the fabric in at least three places seen: the east-west
   canal by Canal Rd (finding 1), the one through Amukoko (D09-17, dark water east-west) and the one by James Igbe St
   (D09-201), plus a north-south channel in Olodi-Apapa (by eye, 6.443 N 3.338 E); WorldCover reads them as built
   (they are under 10 m wide), so they are not in the numbers.
