# d18 Lagoon University: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
(the University of Lagos, Iwaya and the rest) stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Lagoon University is
drawn (`map/d18/`: 144 houses, 7.3% cover, 2 landmark plots: the campus ground and its clinic); this compares the
drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0) at the 90% precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence
0.749; 80%: 0.651), as Saba's study (`d17.md`). [25D] Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, banded as
`scripts/draft_storeys.py`. [OV] Overture places, land use and street segments. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m).
[GM] Google Maps satellite with its labels, looked at by eye on 27 Oct (study, never copied). [WB] `scripts/working_bar.py d18`,
run 27 Oct.

**How** (re-run: `docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d18_campus.py --json x.json`, then `d18_table.py x.json`):
- The outline is `map/d18/district.json` taken back to real metres (inverse of `working_bar.gidi()`): 2,662,297 m²
  real (295,811 m² in Gidi).
- `d17_blocks.py d18` as it stands gives **23 blocks**, four of them 0.29-0.99 km²: the campus's Overture roads
  (residential and service) end in the grounds and close few loops. Adding service roads and tracks as dividers was
  tried: 28 blocks, the big four barely smaller. So `d18_campus.py` runs the same `d17_blocks.study()` unchanged but
  cuts every block of 100,000 m² or more into 200 m real squares on the UTM grid (67 m in Gidi): **127 blocks**
  ("D18c-nn"; the town blocks in the south are the same pieces as `d17_blocks.py`'s D18-13 to D18-23), 56 slivers
  under 2,000 m² (25,097 m²) left out. The cut streets take 5.8% of the outline at the assumed widths.
- **Zone per block** (`d18_campus.zone()`, a rule draft from Overture only): *town* where Overture's residential land
  use "Iwaya" covers 40% or more (or there is buildings but neither university land use nor wetland); *wetland edge*
  where WorldCover mangrove + water + wetland is 30% or more; *hostel* where a place is named for a hall of residence
  (a list of names from Overture, not checked against the university's own list) or is in Overture's hostel category;
  *staff housing* where a place is named quarters / staff / guest house, or an Overture street named in the
  script runs along the block (Kosoko Drive, Ozolua Road, Abdul Atta Road, Alvan Ikoku, College of Medicine Road,
  Ransome Kuti Road, Abdul Karimu Street: chosen by eye on [GM], see below); *academic* for faculty, department,
  library, senate, auditorium and the like inside the university land use; else *campus*; *open ground* with no
  Google building. A block can hold several tags; the **primary** (first of town, hostel, staff housing, academic,
  campus, wetland edge, open ground) picks the kinds column. Five blocks are set by hand (`d18_table.OVERRIDE`,
  `FABRIC`): D18c-09, -10, -17 (campus, outside Overture's university polygon but among campus labels), D18c-126
  (Iwaya's side of the creek), D18c-127 (the staff high-rise blocks). The rest are the rule's, **not checked block by
  block against the satellite**.
- Town blocks take Saba's fabric rule (`d17_fabric.fabric()`); campus zones take kinds by zone (`d18_table.KINDS`),
  all *guess*: the map has no kind for a hall of residence, a faculty block or a detached house in a yard.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The district is at half the real cover.** Ours 7.30% against Google's 14.67%-16.12% [WB]: about 21,800-26,100 m²
   of roof short in Gidi (0.1467 × 295,811 = 43,395 m² needed at the low end; ours 21,587). Inside the blocks the
   shortfall is 18,593 m², and **52% of it is Iwaya's town** (9,618 m²: 30.2% real cover, ours 9.2%, 39 houses where
   Google's 871 are about 97 at 1:3); then the halls (3,446), the faculties (2,758) and the rest of the campus (2,355).
   Staff housing is near (10.3% real, 9.5% ours) and the wetland is right (1.6% against 1.0%).
2. **Iwaya is Saba's fabric, packed tighter**: 24 blocks in the south and south-west, packed 2-storey compound houses
   (434 of 720 readable heights are 2 storeys, 268 one), median footprint about 148 m², the inner blocks 50-66%
   roofed (D18c-106 55%, -115 62%, -120 to -124 64-66%). The six furthest-short blocks of the district are five of
   these (D18c-106, -115, -105, -116, -125) and one faculty block (D18c-43). The Olu of Iwaya's palace (Overture
   place "Palace Of The Olu Of Iwaya") is in D18c-125.
3. **The campus is not compounds.** Our 104 houses in the campus blocks are mostly compound_house and
   face_me_bungalow; the real campus is (a) halls of residence: long blocks along Newhall Path and University Road
   (Madam Tinubu, Fagunwa, New Hall in D18c-06; Eni-Njoku D18c-07; Moremi D18c-04, -33; Mariere D18c-34; King Jaja,
   its quadrangle and the Akingbola postgraduate hall D18c-50 to -52; Kofo Ademola D18c-42; Amina D18c-58; Biobaku and Henry Carr Postgraduate
   D18c-60), 16.8% cover, 44 of 204 readable heights at 3 storeys or more; (b) faculties round the Senate House and
   the main auditorium (D18c-19) and Engineering, Architecture, Science (D18c-35, -37, -53), 22.9% cover, the tallest
   zone; (c) staff housing: detached houses and small blocks in green yards on cul-de-sacs (Kosoko Drive's loops,
   Ozolua Road, Abdul Atta Road, Alvan Ikoku, College of Medicine Road, Ransome Kuti Road by the Vice-Chancellor's
   lodge, by eye [GM]), 10.3% cover, block median footprint about 103 m²; and the high-rise staff blocks on Abdul
   Karimu Street (D18c-118, -127; Google Maps labels "University Of Lagos High Rise Block B" and "Block C"; 2.5D
   5-9 storeys). The map has **no kind for a hall, a faculty block or a detached house in a yard**: walkup,
   school and colonial_house are the nearest (*guess*; a kind gap for the main session, not a study call).
4. **Big, low and green, with real water.** WorldCover reads 30% of the outline as trees and 9% as mangrove + water;
   14 wetland-edge blocks (0.37 km², 39 buildings, 1.6% cover) along the lagoon on the east and the mangrove in the
   north and north-west (by eye [GM]: mangrove north of Madam Tinubu Hall, the lagoon shore along Oduduwa Crescent
   and Abdul Karimu Street). Keep these open; the sports complex and its track (round D18c-43), the cricket oval, and
   the "Computer Open field" among Kosoko Drive's loops are open ground too.
5. **A creek splits Iwaya from the campus in the south-east** (by eye [GM] at 6.5065 N 3.3950 E): packed small
   rusty roofs and a cleared grey patch with very small structures on Iwaya's west bank (D18c-126: 54 buildings,
   median 32 m², 1 storey), the high-rise staff blocks in green ground on the east bank (D18c-127). A second channel
   curves west-east between the campus's International School / ISL football field and Iwaya (about 6.5105 N, by eye).
   The seam to Makoro (d19) and the lagoon edge should keep these channels.

## What was looked at and what was not

- Looked at on Google Maps satellite with labels [GM], 27 Oct: the north (6.5185 N 3.3960 E: the halls on Newhall
  Path, Faculty of Law, the Senate and auditorium, Kosoko Drive's cul-de-sacs, King Jaja Hall, the lagoon shore on
  Oduduwa Crescent); the south (6.5115 N 3.3930 E: Akinpelu Adesola Road, the staff streets Ozolua Road, Abdul Atta
  Road, Alvan Ikoku and College of Medicine Road, the International School, the channel and Iwaya south of it); the
  south-east at z18 (6.5065 N 3.3950 E: the creek, Iwaya's west bank, the High Rise Blocks B and C); the west
  (6.5165 N 3.3885 E: the main gate side, the sports complex and track, Ransome Kuti Road and the Vice-Chancellor's
  lodge); and the 24-storey reading at z18 (below). Not looked at: block by block; no Street View anywhere.
- **The tallest 2.5D readings are not trusted**: 73 m (24 storeys) on a 1,847 m² footprint at Gidi 14111, 6242
  (6.51906 N 3.38763 E) is, by eye [GM], a large building site with a half-built structure and a big round-roofed
  hall beside it (a shadow, a crane or scaffold may have raised the reading); 37 m on 1,920 m² at 14100, 6209 is the
  site's yard north of it. Not checked on Street View. The 5-9 storey readings at 14442-14458, 6691-6752 match the
  labelled high-rise blocks.
- Hall names come from Overture points; whether each point stands on its hall was **not checked** (Moremi Hall has
  two points, in D18c-04 and D18c-33). D18c-88 is tagged hostel only by an Overture "hostel" place (Harmony Haven,
  likely private lodging) beside a fuel station and banks on Akinpelu Adesola Road: *rule*, read it as a mixed strip.
- The staff-housing street list is a by-eye reading of the labels; the university's own estate map was not read.
- 200 m squares are not real blocks: inside the campus they cut across grounds and buildings, so cover per square
  is a density reading, not a plot. Overture misses lanes in Iwaya too, so some town "blocks" hold several real ones.
- Overture's land-use layer carries a mis-placed polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box; it is
  skipped, as in d16, d17 and d19.
