# d12 Agunda: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Agunda is drawn (`map/d12/`: 963 houses, 15.2% cover); this
compares the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline.

**What Agunda draws from.** There is no district study note for d12 in `docs/lagos-study/districts/`. The outline is
the frame study's "Aguda-Coker-Orile" (`map/d12/STREETS.md`; `docs/lagos-study/LIVING.md` row 12: Surulere /
Ajeromi-Ifelodun, class D "poor and packed", 25k people/km², Coker market, the Iganmu/Iponri industrial edge).
Inside the outline, the names read in this study: Ijeshatedo (an Overture place in D12-83), the Palace of the Baale of
Orile Iganmu (Overture, D12-202), Orile Iganmu, Coker Rd, Babs Animashaun Rd, Eric Moore, Iganmu, Ijora Badia and
Ijora 7up Market (Google Maps labels, by eye, 27 Oct).

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651), `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`, each building's median height over pixels with
presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py`. [OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail),
`overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m. [GM] Google Maps satellite with labels, looked at by eye on 27 Oct
(study, never copied). [WB] `scripts/working_bar.py d12`, run 27 Oct.

**How** (re-run, from the repo root):
```
~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_blocks.py d12 --json x.json
~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_fabric.py x.json d12
~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d12_cells.py --json cells.json
```
- The outline is `map/d12/district.json` taken back to real metres (UTM 31N): 8,691,320 m² real (965,702 m² Gidi).
- Real street blocks: the outline less Overture's street segments (2,573 segments, motorway to living_street) and 6
  rail segments, each cut at an **assumed** width (motorway 30 m, trunk 24, primary 18, secondary 14, tertiary 10,
  residential and unclassified 7, rail 10; a divider only, not measured). **259 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 76
  slivers (36,710 m² together) left out. The cut streets take 12.5% of the outline at those widths.
- Per block, the measures of `d17.md` (count by centroid, cover = footprint union clipped to the block, median and
  largest footprint, 2.5D storeys, WorldCover, Overture places and land use, our houses, our cover and the share of
  the Gidi block under our carriageways).
- **New for d12** (`scripts/d12_cells.py`): Overture has no streets through two very large "blocks", D12-226 (1.15
  km² real, the east lobe) and D12-139 (0.82 km², the middle). The script cuts every block of 150,000 m² and more
  (also D12-83 and D12-145) into 150 m real cells (50 m in Gidi) and reads the same measures per cell. It imports
  `d17_blocks.py`, so the block numbering is the same.
- The fabric column is the rule draft of `d17_fabric.py` (FABRIC, as in d17.md). Two blocks are set by hand
  (OVERRIDE, added for d12): D12-226 (split by cell, below) and D12-139 (packed compounds along a canal, by eye).
  The rest are the rule's, **not checked block by block against the satellite**. Compound house against
  face-me-I-face-you cannot be told apart from footprints and heights (as d17.md); the kinds column gives both.
- Overture's land-use layer carries a mis-placed polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box: it
  is skipped (it appears in every block's raw JSON at 100%). The land/land polygon also covers everything and is skipped.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **We hold 15% where Google holds 41% (Saba, d17, is 29% real).** Median real block 55.8%
   cover; in the packed blocks (cover 30% and more, 10 houses and more, D12-226 left out) the middle half runs 24.7-30.9
   houses a hectare, 50.7-61.9% cover, block median footprints 163-219 m². Our carriageways take 30.7% of the outline
   (real streets 12.5% at the assumed widths), so to reach [WB]'s lower bound of 40.79% the land between our roads must
   be about 59% roofed (40.79 / 69.3): as tight as the packed real blocks, with no room for yards between houses.
2. **The storey mix is already right; the count is not.** Ours is 18/68/13/2% for 1/2/3/4 storeys against the real
   18/67/13/2% (of 16,571 read). What is short is houses: 963 where about 2,031 would match Google's count at 1:3,
   and they are too small or too spread: our cover is 15% at about half the real count.
3. **The east lobe is half real open ground.** D12-226: 31 of its 83 cells (0.56 km² real, about 62,000 m² in Gidi)
   are at 3.9% cover; leave them open (scrub, a mini football pitch, a fuel terminal's land), and pack the other half
   to its 41% (ours 11%). Our 8% in the open half is already more than Google's 3.9%.
4. **The furthest short** (Google's cover less ours, in Gidi m² of roof): D12-139 (23,022 m²; the middle, along the
   canal: [WB]'s block at 12432,7449 is in it), D12-226 (18,725; most of it the built half), D12-145 (6,125), D12-83
   (6,120), D12-40 (5,577; ours 9% against 59%), D12-111 (4,409), D12-99 (3,788), D12-105 (3,724), D12-181 (3,594),
   D12-43 (3,204), D12-209 (2,581; ours 8% against 65%), D12-253 (2,536). The list is below the table.
5. **The big roofs are in the Iganmu belt on the east side, not in the houses.** Overture industrial land use covers
   40% and more of D12-74 (76%; by Babs Animashaun / Ijesha, 3.347 E 6.494 N), D12-140 (43%), D12-157 (98%; Total and
   "Scaffold Equipment Nigeria" places), D12-180 (65%), D12-230 (78%), D12-258 (86%), D12-259 (63%). On the satellite
   [GM] east of Babs Animashaun Rd and Immam Dauda St to Eric Moore are long grey warehouse sheds in rows. Overall only
   456 buildings of 500 m² and more in 18,276 (2.5%). Our hand pass 4 walled 3 plots (two factory compounds and a
   school ground; `map/d12/BUILDINGS.md`); which real grounds they are was *not checked*.

and D12-259 ours 1% against 18%.

- Looked at on Google Maps satellite with labels [GM], 27 Oct: the east lobe at about 1,100 m width (6.4740 N 3.3565 E:

  Centre); the Iganmu sheds at about 550 m (6.4830 N 3.3490 E: D12-140, D12-157, Babs Animashaun Rd, Eric Moore).
  Not looked at: every other block; no Street View.
- The canal: D12-139's near-empty cells (7 cells, 48,142 m² real, under 5% cover) follow a dark channel by eye;
  WorldCover reads them "built", so the canal's position is by eye only, not from any data file read here.
- Storeys of 5 and more (136 buildings) are 2.5D readings, which over-read on some buildings (Mùṣá); one cell
  (D12-226.78) has a median of 23 m over 10 buildings and D12-139.82 39.5 m over 1: not checked on Street View.
- The block cut uses assumed widths; Overture misses many lanes, so many "blocks" hold several real blocks
  (D12-139 and D12-226 most). Cover is a per-area figure and is not changed by that; houses per block are.
- [WB] and this script differ by 0.2 points on the whole-outline cover (40.79% against 40.6%); this script counts
  only buildings whose centroid is in the outline. Not traced further.
- Not read: Coker market's position (LIVING.md names it; Overture has no place by that name here, only "Aguda Market"
  in D12-95 and one retail land use, 33% of D12-54);
  Agunda's real bus stops and motor parks (NOW owes them a map study).
