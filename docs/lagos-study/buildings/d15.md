# d15 Mùṣá: the real buildings, block by block, against our drawing

Building study, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
(Mushin, Ladipo, LUTH, Idi-Araba and the rest) stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Mùṣá is the most
worked district (`map/d15/BUILDINGS.md`: 65 hand passes and the drafts; 4,268 houses). Its cover is 43.22% against
Google's 43.26%-47.65% (`scripts/working_bar.py d15`, re-run 27 Oct: FAIL density by 0.04 points, about 490 m² of
roof in Gidi). So this study is not about the total: it asks **where** ours is over or under Google, block by block,
and whether our house kinds, storeys and sizes fit each block's real fabric.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0) at the 90% precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence
0.749; 80%: 0.651), as Saba's study (`d17.md`). [25D] Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, banded as
`scripts/draft_storeys.py` (BUILDINGS.md hand pass 42 found 2.5D over-reads the tallest: 35 read at 13.5-20.5 m were
1-4 storeys in Street View, so the 4+ counts below are high). [MS] Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL),
`~/gidi-data/research/data/ms-buildings/`: the source `scripts/draft_buildings.py` copied our houses from; used here
only to tell a source difference from a drawing one. [OV] Overture places, land use, street segments, rail.
[WC] ESA WorldCover 2021. **Not looked at in a browser this pass**: no block below was checked on Google Maps,
satellite or Street View by this agent; every fabric label is the rule's draft from the numbers.

**How** (re-run, about 20 s each):

- The outline is `map/d15/district.json` taken back to real metres: 10,929,834 m² real (1,214,426 m² in Gidi).
- Real street blocks as in `d17.md` (Overture streets cut at assumed widths, motorway 30 m to residential 7 m; rail
  10 m): **485 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 71 slivers (32,228 m²) left out. The real streets at those widths take
  14.6% of the outline; **our carriageways take 28.6%** (full real widths on a 1:3 map).
- Our houses are compared in the same block, taken to Gidi metres (1:3), by centroid; our cover is our roofs' union
  clipped to the block. Houses stay at real size in Gidi (`draft_buildings.py`: "never scaled"), so the real count at
  Gidi's scale is Google's count / 9.
- Because our streets do not lie exactly where the real ones do and are wider, a single block's figure is noisy. Two
  steadier views are given: "ours net" (our roof over the block's land left free of our carriageways), and a 100 m Gidi
  grid (300 m real; 132 cells at least 30% inside the outline), Google and ours each as a roof union clipped to the cell.
- `d15_fit.py` re-cuts the blocks and asserts each block's area matches the JSON to 1 m² (it does, 485 of 485).
- Overture's land-use layer holds a polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box (it shows in the
  whole-outline row at 1.0): a bad polygon, ignored (`d17_fabric.py` SKIP_LU). Some Overture place names are noise
  (D15-392 "London, Ethihad Stadium manchester city"; D15-479 "National Assembly, Abuja"): left in the table as read,
  not taken as buildings.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The total matches because two errors cancel.** Ours is over Google in 166 of 485 blocks and within 5 points in
   116; summed block by block, ours is 49,056 m² (Gidi) of roof over where it is over and 65,897 m² under where it is
   under. The 100 m grid says the same: of 132 cells, 49 within 5 points, 35 over by 5+, 48 under by 5+. The two big
   overs are campuses and estates drawn as packed housing; the big unders are the factory and hall strips and blocks
   our roads eat.
2. **LUTH is drawn twice as full as it is.** Inside Overture's LUTH land use (244,581 m² real) Google's cover is 22.4%
   (23.5% at 0.651 and at any confidence), ours 44.3%; inside our own plot pl-luthcamp, Google 22.5%, ours 45.0%: about
   6,000 m² of roof too many in Gidi, more than the whole district's shortfall to the bar. Its block D15-392 is the
   furthest over (Google 33%, ours 44%, 6,613 m²), and Idi-Araba south of it, D15-428 (the Chapel of the Healing Cross,
   the LUTH Catholic chaplaincy), is next (Google 26%, ours 52%, 4,748 m²). The real campus is low blocks in open
   ground (*guess* from the 22% cover and BUILDINGS.md pass 11's "greens between"; not looked at by this agent).
   The two school grounds' neighbours are over the same way: D15-286 (Archbishop Aggey Memorial Secondary School;
   Google 26%, ours 52%) and D15-311 (Ilasamaja Road Primary School; Google 26%, ours 57%).
3. **The factories and halls are drawn half empty.** The Vono Road factory plot pl-b27facto: Google 62.4%, ours 26.9%
   (block D15-26: Google 58%, ours 23%, 4 houses, 2,208 m² short; Google's largest roof there is 7,340 m²). The
   expressway strip by Ladipo, D15-05: Google 60%, ours 50%, 3,094 m² short, the furthest under. Blocks where Google has
   15%+ of its buildings at 500 m² and up and ours has under 5% sheds or big houses: D15-25, D15-38 (Google median 267 m²,
   ours 55), D15-413, D15-285, D15-27. District-wide Google has 720 roofs of 500 m² and up (80 at 1:3); we have 58,
   35 of them sheds. Ladipo Market itself is right (Overture's polygon: Google 42.5%, ours 43.5%).
4. **Our houses are half the real size and 1.64 times as many.** Median 83.6 m² against Google's 168.6 and
   Microsoft's 201.7, so the small size is the drawing's, not the source's; 24% of ours are under 40 m² against 11%
   of Google's and 6.5% of Microsoft's. In 192 of the 295 blocks with 10+ Google and 5+ of our houses, our median is
   under 0.6 of Google's (median over those blocks: Google 178 m², ours 81). Of our 1,024 houses under 40 m², 285 are
   shacks and 87 kiosk rows, but 302 are compound houses, 166 bungalows, 135 shop-under-flats and 47 walk-ups; 737 of
   the 1,024 carry the drafter's "moved" note (copied, not trimmed), 241 "trim" and 41 "cut". *Guess*: the frontage
   draft kept small real footprints that fit between our lanes and dropped big ones that clashed. In the packed-compound
   blocks Google's block medians run 152-213 m² (middle half; median 180); our compound_house median is 97.5 m². This is a hand-pass matter (fewer,
   bigger houses at the same cover), not a builder change.
5. **Storeys fit; the shortfall's other half is roads.** Our storey mix matches Google's within 2 points at every
   band (2 storeys 64.9% against 65.2%), and only 4 fit blocks have ours 0.5+ storeys higher on average (D15-24,
   D15-85, D15-309, D15-253) and 14 lower (biggest: D15-476 on Ishaga Road, Google 2.26 against ours 1.56; D15-225 and
   D15-224 on Ikorodu Road, Google 3.0-3.2 against 2.2-2.3; D15-159 on Ladipo Street). Of the 319 blocks under Google,
   151 (22,364 m²) are under only because our carriageways take their land: their net-of-roads cover is at or over
   Google's (D15-284 on Ikorodu Road: 61% of it under our roads; D15-246 by Agege Motor Road 59%; D15-153 on Isolo Road 40%). The road comes first, so
   these stay. The other 168 (43,533 m²) are true gaps on the land, led by D15-05, D15-26, D15-303 (Ogungbaiye Street /
   Agege Motor Road), D15-109 (Agege Motor Road / Olorunsogo Street), D15-43 (Ilupeju Road / Sokunbi Street), D15-394
   (Agege Motor Road / Ikorodu Road), D15-431 (Apena Street) and D15-461 (Wuraola Street). 15 blocks with Google cover of
   30%+ hold none of our houses (D15-50, D15-123, D15-148, D15-173, D15-341, D15-369, D15-374, D15-417, D15-420,
   D15-472 to D15-474, D15-477, D15-483, D15-485; 8,684 m² Gidi, median 47% under our roads; seven on the south edge
   along Itire Road and Western Avenue, two on Ojekunle Street by the expressway, the rest scattered).

Also: shops. Our lock-up and kiosk share is 50%+ in 19 fit blocks where Overture has under 2 places a hectare (D15-129
on Palm Avenue / Isolo Road, all 11 of ours shops; D15-180 71%; D15-27 70%; D15-43 67%; D15-05 65%), so the
"busy street" rule may have made lock-ups of quiet fronts there; and two blocks with 8+ places a hectare have none
(D15-181, 11.7 a hectare, Methodist Church; D15-401, 8.0). Overture places are thin in Lagos, so these want Street View
before any change. Shacks: 9 fit blocks where our shack share is 20 points over Google's share under 40 m² (D15-446
Okoh Drive, 62% shacks against Google 7% small; D15-447, D15-10, D15-265, D15-344).
