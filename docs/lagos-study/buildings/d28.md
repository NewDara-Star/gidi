# d28 Gbàgede: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 28 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Gbàgede is drawn (`map/d28/`: 1,703 houses, 13.0% cover); this
compares the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline. The owner's grandmother's house was on Ajayi
Aina Street in front of the big Deeper Life church, and he went to Twinkle Star nursery there: both are inside the
outline and have their own section below.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651) from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`: each building's median height over pixels with
presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (under 4.5 m 1 storey, 4.5-7.5 2, 7.5-10.5 3, then on by 3 m).
[OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail), `overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m.
[GM] Google Maps satellite and labels, looked at by eye on 28 Oct (study, never copied). [SV] Google Street View, one
panorama ("4 Ajayi Aina St", Mar 2024), looked at by eye both ways. [WEB] web pages, each named where used.

**How** (re-run, from `~/gidi`, `P=~/.venvs/gidi/bin/python`):
- `$P -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_blocks.py d28 --json x.json` (the blocks; about 20 s), then
  `$P -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_fabric.py x.json d28` (the table, the furthest-short list, the mix).
- `$P -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d12_cells.py d28 --min 200000 --cell 250 --json cells.json` (the 12 big
  blocks cut into 250 m cells; the `d28` argument was added to `d12_cells.py` for this study, d12 stays its default).
- `$P -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d13_landmarks.py d28` (every non-residential Overture ground on its own).
- `$P -I docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d28_landmarks.py` (written for this study: the Deeper Life ground, Ajayi
  Aina Street, Bakare Dawodu Street and Twinkle Star, building by building).
- `$P -I scripts/working_bar.py d28` (the gate's own reading, for comparison).
- The outline is `map/d28/district.json`, taken back to real metres (UTM 31N) by the inverse of `working_bar.gidi()`:
  15,174,958 m² real (1,686,106 m² in Gidi).
- Real street blocks: the outline less Overture's street segments (motorway to living_street, unclassified included;
  service roads, paths and footways not) and the rail lines, cut at **assumed** full widths (motorway 30 m, trunk 24,
  primary 18, secondary 14, tertiary 10, residential and unclassified 7, rail 10; not measured, a divider only).
  **255 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 79 slivers under that (32,791 m² together) are left out. The cut streets take
  11.4% of the outline at those widths. 3,207 Overture divider segments and 3 rail segments are in the box.
- Per block: as d17.md (centroid-in count at 90%, footprint union clipped to the block, median and largest footprint,
  2.5D heights, Overture places and land use, our houses and roof cover in the same block in Gidi metres, the share of
  the Gidi block under our carriageways). Our footprints are real size (map/d28); nothing of ours is multiplied by 9.
- The fabric column is `d17_fabric.py`'s rule draft (its order is in d17.md). No d28 block was given a hand override:
  the rule's label stands for every block and is **not checked block by block against the satellite**.
- Overture's land-use polygon named for a Pacific marine monument covers the whole box; it is skipped, as before.
- Compound house against face-me-I-face-you cannot be told apart from footprints and heights. *Guess*, as d17: the
  packed 2-storey blocks are mostly compound and rooming houses; not read from any source.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **The gap is cover, and half of it is in the north-centre.** Google's 27.3% against our 13.0%; in count 1,703
   houses against about 2,747 (24,720 / 9) at 1:3. Our carriageways take 28.0% of the outline against 11.4% for the
   real streets, so the land between our roads must be about 38% roofed (27.4 / 72.0) to reach Google's low bound;
   the real median block is 46%, ours 20%. The furthest short (Gidi m² of roof): D28-126 (Ifako, Estaport and the
   estates north of the church; 3,433 real buildings, ours 171; 38,124), D28-127 (the block west of Ajayi Aina; 940,
   ours 36; 13,328), D28-133 (the Oworonshoki lagoon edge; 10,490), D28-152, D28-57, D28-55, D28-22, D28-60, D28-54,
   D28-229, D28-255, D28-146. `working_bar`'s own list starts with the same block (at Gidi 14013,4639: ours 14%,
   Google 33%, 43,185 m² short).
2. **Gbàgede is a 2-storey compound town like Saba, a little looser**: whole cover 27.3% (Saba 29.0%, Sọ̀rọ̀lérè
   33.5%), median footprint 127 m², 62% of readable heights 2 storeys, 26% 1, 11% 3. The 191 packed blocks run 23-31
   houses a hectare (middle half), 42-56% cover, block median footprints 130-197 m². Our storeys already match the mix
   (66% 2-storey); the count and cover do not.
3. **The east is real open ground and water, and is drawn about right.** D28-133 (1.33 km² real: WC built 41%,
   grass 19%, wetland 14%, water 10%; Overture wetland 26%; 54 of its Google buildings on water pixels; median
   footprint 62 m², 621 of 1,853 under 40 m²) and D28-57, D28-75 (wetland, mangrove 22%) are the Oworonshoki and
   Agboyi lagoon edge: small houses and shacks round wet ground. Our 6% there against Google's 13% is short, but the
   open ground is real; add small houses at its dry edges, not a packed fabric.
4. **The grounds are few but big** (`d13_landmarks.py d28`, real m² inside the outline):
   - **Gbagada's old industrial estate, now offices** (Overture industrial 219,143 m², D28-202 and D28-230): 195
     Google buildings at 44% cover, the tallest ground in the district (2.5D storeys 3: 37, 4: 14, 5: 6, 6: 1; D28-202
     alone 5: 9, 6: 7, 7: 2) and the district's largest roof (10,450 m²); Overture names The Zone tech park, Zone
     Tech Park, Regus, Workforce Group, Zinox and Swansea University's Africa office. Ours there: 9 houses, 12% cover,
     shed 2, face_me_bungalow 5: offices and big sheds, not bungalows.
   - **The Apostolic Church's LAWNA headquarters ground** (Overture religious 118,420 m², D28-19, north): 61 buildings
     at 20%, one of 10,439 m² (2.5D 7 storeys; a tall hall, *guess*). Ours: 22 houses, no hall.
   - **Gbagada General Hospital** (Overture hospital 109,112 m², D28-231): 66 buildings at 13%, median 90 m², largest
     3,738 m², 1-4 storeys. Ours: 7 houses at 9% and a compound plot.
   - **Chinatown** (Overture retail 8,179 m², D28-22, by Ogudu Road; largest roof in the block 9,093 m²): ours 2
     houses at 6% there. Not looked at on the satellite.
   - **Gbagada Landfill** (10,812 m²) and an unnamed landfill (11,450 m²): no buildings, rightly none of ours.
   - 13 school grounds (Ogudu GRA School Complex 47,982 m², Lagos Baptist Academy 34,581, Ogudu Junior Grammar 16,288,
     Estaport Schools 15,282 and smaller): 8-65% cover, 1-3 storeys; Lagos Baptist Academy has 37 Google buildings
     and 1 of ours and no plot of ours.
5. **The Deeper Life headquarters is one of the biggest roofs in Lagos and ours is a tenth of it** (section below).
   The real auditorium is a fan-shaped hall of 4,703 m² (about 74 by 108 m, 23 m high) [GOB, 25D, GM]; ours is a
   442 m² church house of 2 storeys. At real size it cannot fit: the whole ground is 3,897 m² in Gidi. How a landmark
   bigger than its 1:3 ground is drawn is the owner's call (scaled hall, or a ground drawn larger than 1:3); not
   decided here.

## The Deeper Life church, Ajayi Aina Street and Twinkle Star

**Where they are.** All three are inside the outline, within 160 m of each other, about 750 m from its edge
(`d28_landmarks.py`).
- **The church.** Overture place "Deeper Life Bible Church" (pentecostal_place_of_worship, address 10 Oke Owo St,
  confidence 0.89) at 6.55633 N 3.38790 E, Gidi 14119,4868, block D28-172. Google Maps labels the site "Deeper Life
  Bible Church International..." and "DEEPER LIFE HQ CAR PARK" [GM, 28 Oct]. Wikipedia's Deeper Christian Life
  Ministry page puts the ministry's headquarters church in Gbagada, commissioned in April 2018 [WEB, read 28 Oct];
  its William Kumuyi page says the auditorium could seat up to 30,000 and gives the opening as 24 April 2018 [WEB, read
  28 Oct]. That this hall is the one on Wikipedia is by the Street View sign "HEADQUARTERS GBAGADA, LAGOS" on the
  building behind the fence [SV] and Google's label; no source gives the street address.
- **Its ground.** Overture land use 'religious' (unnamed) round the point: 35,076 m² real (3,897 m² in Gidi), about
  219 m east-west by 328 m north-south (bounding box), centroid Gidi 14129,4867. It also holds Overture's "Ifako
  District Church, Gbagada Lagos" and "Elbethel School" points. Google Maps places a second "Deeper Life Church" label
  a few hundred metres east of the hall, beyond Ajayi Aina (distance by eye, not measured); what that is was *not
  checked*.
- **Ajayi Aina Street.** Overture: 6 tertiary segments, 428 m, from 6.55572 N 3.38856 E (Gidi 14144,4891), at the
  ground's south-east corner, north-east to 6.55835 N 3.39139 E (Gidi 14248,4794); its nearest point is 100 m from the
  church point. Three Overture places on it say so in their addresses: "Ajayi Aina St, opposite Deeper life Bible
  Church Ifako", "6 Ajayi Aina street, opposite deeper life church, ifako", "4 Ajayi Aina St". Ours: rd-bc889bb3, 8.5 m
  wide, 145 m long, 2 m (Hausdorff) from the real street taken to Gidi.
- **Twinkle Star.** Google Maps has "Twinkle Star Nursery and Primary School" (elementary school), "Plot 23 Bakare
  Dawodu St, Gbagada", plus code H94Q+7M, its pin at 6.5556825 N 3.3891698 E (Gidi 14166,4892) [GM, read 28 Oct];
  finelib gives the same address (23 Bakare Dawodu Street, contact Mrs Elizabeth B. Diji) and Edusko says it was
  founded on 16 September 1986 by Mrs E.B. Diji, day school, nursery and primary [WEB, read 28 Oct]. It is not in
  Overture by name. The pin is 158 m from the church point, 64 m from the church ground, 53 m from Ajayi Aina and 26 m
  from Bakare Dawodu Street, in block D28-206, on a Google building of 449 m² (about 15 by 30 m, 7.5 m: 3 storeys
  [25D]). Bakare Dawodu Street (Overture: 4 residential segments, 532 m) leaves Ajayi Aina at its south end, opposite
  the church, and runs south-east; 83 Google buildings have their centroid within 30 m of it (median 226 m², storeys 1:
  17, 2: 50, 3: 10, 4: 1). The place photo on Google Maps shows a black gate and a 2-storey house front [GM, by eye];
  its Street View was not opened.

**What is on the church ground** [GOB, 25D]: 19 buildings at 31.7% cover.
- **The auditorium**: 4,703 m², about 74 by 108 m (rotated rectangle), 23.0 m high (2.5D reads "8 storeys", but it is
  one tall hall), its centre on the church point. On the satellite a fan-shaped roof of pale ribbed panels opening to
