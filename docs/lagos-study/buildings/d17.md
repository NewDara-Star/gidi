# d17 Saba: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 27 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Saba is drawn (`map/d17/`: 831 houses, 14.4% cover); this compares
the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651) from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/` (tiles qNSfrvnK9X8, shazo_x29JA): each
building's median height over pixels with presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (under 4.5 m 1 storey,
4.5-7.5 2, 7.5-10.5 3, then on by 3 m). [OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail), `overture-lagos/`.
[WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m. [GM] Google Maps satellite and labels, looked at by eye on 27 Oct (study, never copied).

**How** (re-run: `docs/lagos-study/buildings/scripts/d17_blocks.py d17 --json x.json`, then
`d17_fabric.py x.json d17`):
- The outline is `map/d17/district.json`, taken back to real metres (UTM 31N) by the inverse of
  `working_bar.gidi()`: 6,381,575 m² real (709,064 m² in Gidi).
- Real street blocks: the outline less Overture's street segments (motorway to living_street, unclassified included;
  service roads, paths and footways not) and the rail lines, each cut at an **assumed** full width (motorway 30 m,
  trunk 24, primary 18, secondary 14, tertiary 10, residential and unclassified 7, rail 10; not measured, a divider
  only). **206 blocks** of 2,000 m² and more; 75 slivers under that (24,716 m² together) are left out of the table.
  The cut streets take 11.2% of the outline at those widths.
- Per block: buildings whose centroid is in the block (90% threshold), their footprint union clipped to the block over
  the block's area (cover), median and largest footprint, heights and storeys [25D], Overture places and land use, and
  our map's houses (centroid in the block taken to Gidi metres, roof cover clipped to it, and the share of the Gidi
  block under our own carriageways, centreline buffered by width / 2, flat ends, as `working_bar.py`).
- The fabric column is a rule draft (`d17_fabric.py`, FABRIC: institution land use over 40% of the block, then cover
  under 12% open, median under 60 m² with 35% under 40 m² shacks, median 300 m² or a quarter over 500 m² big roofs,
  median height 7.5 m walk-ups, 8 places a hectare shops on the fronts, cover under 30% loose, median height under
  4.5 m bungalows, else packed 2-storey compounds). Four blocks are set by hand from the satellite and Google's labels
  (OVERRIDE in the script): D17-49, D17-81, D17-91, D17-103. The rest are the rule's, **not checked block by block
  against the satellite**.
- Compound house against face-me-I-face-you cannot be told apart from footprints and heights: both are 1-2 storey
  rectangles of 100-300 m². The kinds column gives both; the split stays the drafter's (`draft_kinds.py`) or the hand
  pass's, by Street View. *Guess* that the "packed compounds" blocks are mostly face-me rooming houses and old
  compound houses, from the median of 143 m² and 2 storeys; not read from any source.

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **Our roads eat a third of the land.** Our carriageways cover 32.4% of Saba's outline against 11.2% for the real
   streets at the assumed widths, because Gidi draws roads at full real width on a 1:3 map. To reach Google's 29% over
   the whole outline, the land left between our roads must be about 43% roofed (29 / 67.6), which is what the real
   blocks hold (median block 45%). Filling up means packing the land between roads as tightly as the real blocks are,
   not adding roads or lanes.
2. **Saba is a town of 2-storey compounds of 140-200 m²**: in the 134 "packed" blocks the middle half run 22-32
   houses a hectare, 42-56% cover, block median footprints 140-195 m² (district median 143 m²);
   62% of the readable heights are 2 storeys, 10% 3, and 66 buildings in the whole district read 5 storeys or more
   (scattered: D17-55 has 1 each at 7, 8, 9, 12 and 14 storeys; D17-143 one each at 11 and 14; D17-171 at 6, 7, 7, 9).
   Our storeys are already near the real mix (68% 2-storey); what is short is the count: 831 houses where 1,110 would
   match Google's count at 1:3.
3. **The furthest short** (Google's cover less ours, in Gidi m² of roof): D17-89 (round Harvey Road: the health
   centre, the state College of Health Technology, Yaba Psychiatric Hospital's points; 8,092 m²), D17-62 (Yaba
   College of Technology and the streets round it; 5,511), D17-145 (the block holding Methodist Girls' High School; ours 5%
   against 36%; 3,265), D17-49 (Military Hospital; 2,571), D17-74 (2,447), D17-66 (Abule Ijesha by the Federal College
   of Education; 2,129), D17-127, D17-197, D17-27, D17-128, D17-52, D17-03. The full list is below the table.
4. **The landmarks are big, low and green**: Yaba College of Technology (47% of D17-62 by Overture land use), the
   Federal College of Education (Technical) Akoka (D17-57, D17-67, part of D17-66), Igbobi College (62% of D17-05),
   the Military Hospital (D17-49, separate low blocks in green ground by eye [GM]; 2.5D: 56 one-storey, 39 two, 7 three), Atan Cemetery (D17-91,
   4% roofed), the barracks east of Harvey Road (D17-103, streets labelled "An Barrack" [GM]), the Nigerian Railway
   Corporation's ground (D17-185, Overture railway land use 85%), the Federal Psychiatric Hospital (D17-107) and a
   big yard by Murtala Muhammed Way (D17-81, Overture commercial 88%, unnamed [OV]: an open lot packed with small
   white objects, cars or stalls, beside long sheds, by eye [GM]). Our hand pass 4 walled 9 plots from Overture's
   land use (BUILDINGS.md, a barracks among them); which of these real grounds each plot is was *not checked*.
   Saba as a whole has few big roofs: 457 buildings of 500 m² or more in 9,991 (4.6%).
5. **Akoka's lagoon edge is real open ground** (D17-52, 0.67 km² real: Overture wetland 47%, WorldCover water 23%,
   mangrove 25%, built 35%): 539 buildings at 11% cover, 133 of them under 40 m² (waterfront shacks, *guess*), with
   St. Augustine's College of Education. Our 8% there is near Google's 11%; leave it open, with small houses at the
   water's edge.

## How our map differs, block by block

The last column of the table is ours: houses (centroid in the block), our roof cover of the Gidi block, and the share of
the Gidi block under our carriageways. Where that share is 40% or more (D17-07 44%, D17-18 63%, D17-177 52%, D17-198
60%, among others), a small real block is mostly road in Gidi: it is a few metres wide once our full-width roads
cross it, and its houses cannot all fit. Kinds: ours are compound_house 433, face_me_bungalow 154, shop_under_flats 123,
walkup 64, shack 44, kiosk_row 7, shed 6. The real fabric agrees with that mix in kind (no block reads as towers;
the big-roof blocks are few, 8 blocks, 0.06 km²); it differs in count and cover.

- Storeys over 4 (66 buildings) are 2.5D readings, which over-read on some buildings (Mùṣá, `draft_storeys.py`); not

- Overture's land-use layer carries a mis-placed polygon named for a Pacific marine monument over the whole box; it is
  skipped. Overture's land/land polygon holds the whole area and says nothing about water.
