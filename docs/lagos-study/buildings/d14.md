# d14 Ìtìrí: the real buildings, block by block

Building study, 28 Oct 2026, by the building-study agent (`.claude/agents/building-study.md`). Study only: real names
stay in this file and never go into `map/` records. Ìtìrí is drawn (`map/d14/`: 687 houses, 18.75% cover); this
compares the drawing against the real buildings inside the same outline. There is no district study note for d14 in
`docs/lagos-study/districts/` (not read: none exists); `map/d14/BUILDINGS.md` and `STREETS.md` were read.

**Sources.** [GOB] Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0), `~/gidi-data/research/data/open-buildings-v3/`, at the 90%
precision threshold of S2 tile 103 (confidence 0.749; 80%: 0.651) from `thresholds/score_thresholds_s2_level_4.csv`.
[25D] Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal 2023, `open-buildings-2023/`: each building's median height over pixels with
presence >= 0.5, banded as `scripts/draft_storeys.py` (under 4.5 m 1 storey, 4.5-7.5 2, 7.5-10.5 3, then on by 3 m).
[OV] Overture (places, land use, street segments, rail), `overture-lagos/`. [WC] ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m.
[GM] Google Maps satellite and labels, and two Street View panoramas, looked at by eye on 28 Oct (study, never copied;
no pictures saved).

## The five things that matter for drawing

1. **Ìtìrí is the densest district studied so far, and ours holds 40% of it.** Google's cover is 47.0% inside the
   outline (Saba 29.0%, `d17.md`); the median block is 57% under roof. Ours is 18.75%; only 2 of 229 blocks reach
   Google's figure. At Gidi's 1:3 the real count is about 1,335 houses against our 687, and the roof short is about
   161,600 m² (47.0% - 18.75% of 571,912 m²). BUILDINGS.md says our houses were drafted from Microsoft footprints
   (37.5% under the district there); Google's 47-52% is the figure the working bar gates on (CLAUDE.md, "Google first").
2. **Our roads take 29% of the land against the real 14%.** With every real street kept at full width on a 1:3 frame
   (STREETS.md, 367 roads, 41 km/km²), what is left between them is half the real block depth; the blocks themselves
   still need 57% roof. Filling to Google's cover means houses packed wall to wall to the carriageway edge, 2 storeys,
   footprints near 180 m² (real median 184 m²; ours 133 m²).
3. **It is a 2-storey town: 69% of read buildings are 2 storeys, 20% one, 9% three, 1% four and up.** Our storeys
   (72/18/8/2%) already match; nothing to change there. 3-storey walk-ups with balconies stand among 1-storey
   bungalows with a wooden shop stall in front on a sand street (Street View, Kushimo St, Jul 2024); 2-storey compound
   houses behind block walls on a sand street (Street View, Olaide Awoye St, Apr 2024).
4. **Industry is a strip on the west edge, along the Apapa-Oshodi Expressway, not spread through.** 43 roofs of
   1,000 m²+ (122,831 m²); the big ones: Dana Pharmaceuticals / Dana Plast and LK Plast (D14-03, 12,610 and 9,892 m²
   roofs, 74% of that block's roof in 6 sheds); Odo Olowu Warehouse / Bharat Ventures (D14-222, 10,737 m²); Ibeto
   Warehouse (D14-135, 8,429 and 5,655 m²); a strip of sheds between the expressway and the canal in D14-41's north
   (Multivista West Africa label; 7 roofs of 1,688-5,026 m², 9-14.5 m tall by [25D]). Ours has 6 sheds and one walled
   factory plot (pl-itr58634, 5,945 m²); D14-03, D14-135 and D14-222 hold 9, 3 and 5 of our houses at 12-15% cover.
5. **Overture's street net has holes, so one "block" (D14-41) is 0.42 km² of the town joined up.** 14 dead-end
   segment ends lie in or at it (6 within 40 m of another segment); the satellite centred inside it shows a full grid
   of streets (labels Kushimo St, Oremerin St, Oluwashina St). Its 918 buildings and 45% cover are an area average,
   not a block; it is the largest roof short in the district (13,265 m² by `d17_fabric.py`). The real blocks there
   look about 70 m deep by eye (*guess*, not measured); the hand pass should take its streets from Google Maps, not Overture.

Smaller: no water or wetland ground (WorldCover 100% built); only 8 Overture land-use grounds that are not
residential (2 school grounds 18,775 and 8,985 m², an industrial ground 53,504 m², a church ground 5,216 m², a pitch
1,891 m², 2 scrub patches, a 120 m² industrial scrap), so the institutions are mostly single buildings among the
houses (92 schools and 138 churches as Overture points, most of them private schools and small churches in houses).

## How our map differs, block by block

- Cover: real block median 57%, ours 21%. 23 blocks hold none of our houses. The twelve blocks furthest below are
  listed under the table.
- Kinds: ours is compound_house 465, face_me_bungalow 110, walkup 60, shop_under_flats 22, shack 20, shed 6,
  kiosk_row 4. The real reading gives no sign of shacks as a fabric: 1,027 of 12,019 buildings are under 40 m² (9%),
  scattered, and no block meets the shack rule (median under 60 m² with 35% under 40 m²). Our 20 shacks are a *guess*
  away from the real; they could be kiosks or annexes.
- Big roofs: ours has 6 sheds against 43 real roofs of 1,000 m²+. The three industrial blocks (D14-03, D14-135,
  D14-222) are where sheds belong, on the expressway side.
- Ground floors: ours is home 588, flats 60, lock_ups 33, workshop 6. Eight blocks take the rule's "shops on the
  fronts" (8 or more Overture places a hectare, after the rules before it), the busiest D14-168 (Med-In Specialist Hospital, hotels, 10 named) at 12258, 6862,
  where Google has 60% cover and ours 20%.
