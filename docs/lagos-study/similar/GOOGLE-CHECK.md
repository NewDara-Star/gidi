# The close calls, checked on Google satellite (22 Oct 2026)

The similarity numbers (`SIMILAR.md`) flagged 10 merges as close or doubtful. Each pair was looked at side by side on Google satellite, about 1 km across, at each unit's seed point. Study only: the pictures stay outside the repo (`~/gidi-data/research/google/lagos-study/close-calls/`, sheet `close_calls.jpg`).

| Pair (similarity) | Verdict | What the pictures show |
|---|---|---|
| Ikoyi / Obalende-Onikan (69-71%) | **Split** | Ikoyi: big plots, gardens, trees, villas and blocks of flats. Obalende-Onikan: dense old roofs, tight streets, the flyover. Obalende-Onikan goes with the Island core instead |
| Festac / Okota-Ikotun (66%) | **Split** | Festac is one planned grid of avenues and closes with identical blocks. Okota is a grid of separate houses, each different |
| Magodo-Omole / Ketu-Ojota (67%) | **Split** | Magodo: a gated estate of detached houses, bounded by a canal. Ketu: dense dark roofs, the big market sheds, the expressway and the bus stop. Magodo joins Omole-Berger (an estates belt); Ketu-Mile 12 is a market and motor-park hub |
| Gbagada / Maryland (67%) | **Split** | Gbagada: quiet middle-class streets and a canal. Maryland: the junction, the flyover, old dense roofs west of it, church compounds and the mall east of it |
| Apapa / Trade Fair-Satellite Town (61%) | **Split** | Apapa: an old GRA (crescents, big trees, compounds) and the port. Trade Fair-Abule Ado: dense dark roofs. Satellite Town: a grid of numbered closes |
| Alaba / the Badagry road (62%) | **Split** | Alaba is a city of market sheds and warehouses. Ijanikin on the Badagry road: bush with thin new housing |
| Ilupeju / Ladipo (64%) | **Split** | Ilupeju: planned streets of houses with factories at the edge. Ladipo: the spare-parts market, warehouses with blue roofs, the rail line |
| Ajao Estate / the airport (63%) | **Split** | Ajao: estate closes and factories. Airport edge: grass, fuel depots, airport roads, one dense informal strip |
| Bariga-Akoka / Shomolu (61%) | **Merge** | The same dense rust roofs and tangled streets |
| Badore / Ajah, east Lekki (61%) | **Merge** | The same new estates, half-built plots and empty land |

## What it does to the count

Start from 29 (65%, tiny units folded). Then:
- Festac comes out of Okota-Ikotun: +1.
- Ketu splits from Magodo-Omole: +1.
- Ikoyi splits from Obalende-Onikan, which joins Isale Eko: 0.
- Gbagada splits from Maryland, which joins the Ikeja side: 0.
- Bariga joins Shomolu: -1.
- Badore joins east Lekki: -1.

Apapa, Alaba, Ladipo and Ajao are already apart at 29. **Net: about 29 districts, with different members.** Google backs the 29 and not the 23: in six of the merges the 23 makes, the pictures show plainly different places.

Not yet looked at: Airport with Ikeja Along, and how the units fold into the big outer belts (Ikorodu, Alimosho, Epe-Ibeju, Badagry). Those belts are squeezed hard anyway.
