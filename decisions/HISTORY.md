# Decisions that were replaced (history)

The owner's decisions that later ones replaced, or that held only for the old generated map (13-21 Oct). Kept so we know
what was decided before and why it changed; none of them guides new work. Moved out of `DECISIONS.md` on 24 Oct, word for
word, each with what replaced it. A decision that changes again comes here the same way.

- **Layout v2 at 1:4** (15 Oct). A fictional Lagos at one even scale, about 19.6 × 9.3 km. Each district sits where its inspiration really is; coasts and borders drawn fresh. See `docs/LAYOUT-V2.md`.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "The new map is 1:3" and "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" (22 Oct); The layout doc was deleted 25 Oct (git tag `before-cleanup-25oct`).
- **Regenerate, don't move the old frozen Food Gate block.** The new map leads. The pilot's value lives in the generator rules; the old file is kept as a record.
  - **Replaced:** old map only; the generator is frozen ("Hand-made, checked by programs", 22 Oct).
- **One shared generator, one profile per district, run in parallel.** Not 17 separate generators.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "The plan is hand-made; the models come from kits" (22 Oct): no generators.
- **The Reach moves to Jungle City, on Carnival Avenue** (19 Oct; it stood in Parade Square, Old Island, from 16 Oct). The Reach is the kit's sculpture after the owner's own artwork: a woman reaching out to an Eyo-style masquerade, about 14 m across. The owner drew it in 2020 for a FESTAC '77 event (never used), so it is FESTAC-coded and belongs in the district that takes from Festac: Jungle City's Carnival Avenue (after Festac's Seventh Avenue; `docs/hand/borrowed-lagos.md` row 23). Parade Square keeps its gate riders and needs a new centrepiece.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "The Reach goes with Festac into the new map" (22 Oct).
- **Know when to stop generating; then hand-make Gidi** (16 Oct). "It's important to know when to stop generating and start hand making Gidi, including checking out what makes Lagos unmistakably Lagos and working with them on the map... so we are not in generator hell."
  - **The stop line:** the 16 Oct build (all 17 districts from the deep studies, calibration pass 2, link roads) is the last generated base. Remaining check failures are fixed by hand, then every district is frozen. No more calibration passes or rebuilds.
  - **Then, district by district** (the Maze and the Hub first: Act 1 opens there):
    1. a list of what makes the place unmistakably Lagos, from the owner's photos, the deep study and `docs/LAGOS-ISMS.md`, checked by the owner
    2. placed by hand at exact spots through the hand files, labelled and noted
    3. the opening rebuilt and locked (the Maze and the Hub)
    4. freeze, show the owner, next district
  - **Replaced:** done: the old map stopped there and is history ("The new map is the truth", 24 Oct). Its district-by-district method went on into "The plan is hand-made".
- **Hand-place, not tune.** The generator gives the base; then exact hand placement and clean-up through hand files (`web/hand/<id>.json`).
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "The plan is hand-made; the models come from kits" (22 Oct): there is no generated base under the new map.
- **The Lagos pass** (19 Oct): a place is "Lagos" only when the owner has looked at it rendered and said so. One spot first, all the way (the Àdàrú motor park on its main road), to learn the technique; decisions made there become rules that help other places, but each place earns its own pass. Years of work; places improve in updates. Method and spot 1's shot list: `docs/hand/lagos-pass.md`.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "The owner's eye passes a kit" (22 Oct) and "The ultimate test" (23 Oct). The pass doc was deleted 25 Oct (git tag `before-cleanup-25oct`); its frame list is in `docs/OLD-MAP-LESSONS.md`.
- **Places are built like the fonts and the danfo** (19 Oct): study a real Lagos spot on Google Earth and Street View in close detail, rebuild it one-for-one first (its layout, buildings, stalls, wires, clutter), then change it step by step until it is Gidi's own (invented names, our shops and brands, our twists). Street View is the main reference: more detail than any photo search. The game may take a year or two; places get their pass one at a time. Rules learned in one place improve the kit city-wide (e.g. balconies always have a door or opening).
  - **Replaced:** replaced by the kit tests ("A kit is proven by rebuilding real streets from it", "A robust kit, not a matched photo") and by "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" (4): real houses are references, never copies.
- **District by district, matched to real life at plan level first** (19 Oct): the generator's guesses (road types, widths, building types) don't match Lagos, and fixing spots by hand doesn't stop the same mistakes elsewhere. So each district gets its real twin area studied from above on Google Earth, and its plan set from that (road network with the right types and widths, building types and heights, markets, motor parks, schools, churches, real counts) before details. Every street then starts red; details come street by street (Street View study, amber; one-for-one modelling and the owner's pass, green). Ìbùdó first. Lesson that prompted it: Market Road was entered as a "main", which the kit draws as a tarred highway with kerbs and lines; the type of a road decides how it looks.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" and "Roads first, districts by their borders" (22 Oct).
- **Every type has a real twin too** (19 Oct): not only streets: every type of building, plot, street item, vehicle, deck, monument, road, road surface, ground and house detail records the real thing it comes from, where it was seen, and pictures (kept in `~/gidi-data/research/`, never in the repo). The list is `docs/TWINS.md`, made by `scripts/twins.py` from the map and `web/twins.json`; new types appear there by themselves. Same colours as the streets: none (no twin yet), red (named, no picture), amber (pictures studied), green (modelled and passed by the owner). Owner: "so we know we have a real life twin". Getting the pictures is fine ("if you can get images it'll be fine").
  - **Replaced:** old-map types (`docs/TWINS.md`, `scripts/twins.py`); the idea lives on in "The ultimate test" (23 Oct): every thing modelled comes from a real picture.
- **A district is the child of its real inspirations, built only from real pieces** (owner, 19 Oct, night): "say these two people had a baby, and the baby was a functioning district". Ìbùdó is the child of Oshodi and Ojuelegba. Study both places in their entirety first (road structure from expressway to lane, markets, motor parks and how people park, kiosks and shop rows, schools, churches, mosques, civic centres, how the place functions socially), see what Ìbùdó's map can hold, then place every street and place from a real street or place in the two, mixed and matched ("you're not inventing any district from anything here"). Every Gidi street records its real source street, with pictures of its buildings, paths and ground (thousands of photos, outside the repo), so modelling works from them. The generated side streets go. Plan v2's big pieces (19 Oct) are not merged as such: only the rail through the Oshodi crossing stands; the rest is checked against the study.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" (22 Oct): a district is inspired only by the places actually there, never by somewhere across the city.
- **Seams between child districts** (owner, 20 Oct: "remember that"): shared lines (arterials, the rail, district-edge mains) keep their crossing points at district edges; inside a district they may follow the real roads. A neighbour's street that ends on the shared edge carries on into the district (checks 5-7 pass for every area). A real street reaching the edge is run on to the edge road, never left floating. Neighbours stay frozen; only a decided line changes a neighbour, by hand edits. At the seams the real places don't match their Gidi neighbours, so seams are joined by hand at edge roads and gateways; when a neighbour is rebuilt from its own real places, its crossings line up with the built district.
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "Roads first, districts by their borders" (22 Oct) and "Mùṣá's borders, edge roads..." (3), 23 Oct: an edge road belongs to one district and the neighbour joins onto it.
- **Gidi stays Gidi, not a clone of Lagos** (owner, 20 Oct): Lagos is too big and much of it repetitive, so the 17-district frame stays. What changes is that every street, road, house and civic place in a district has its reference in real Lagos, taken from that district's inspirations (its real LGAs and neighbourhoods), with its twin recorded. Roads, streets and links must connect properly with the neighbouring districts (see the seams rule).
  - **Replaced:** replaced by "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" (22 Oct).
- **Fixes reach everything already built** (owner, 21 Oct: "if you fix a bug in the future... let's figure out how to get it retroactive"; "I can ask you this question again tomorrow, and you still find gaps... we can't keep doing that"). The areas built from real places are not kept as hand edits: `scripts/rebuild_areas.py` builds every area again from its source (the child plan, the real data, the builder) into `web/hand/gen/<district>.json`, and the bake's own clean-up (`scripts/autoclean.py`) runs every bake. A fix to the builder or the clean-up reaches every area on the next rebuild. `web/hand/<district>.json` keeps only fixes made by hand; one that no longer lands on anything fails the checks (check 17) instead of vanishing. Every rule here has a row in `decisions/CHECKS.md` saying what checks it (`scripts/check_rules.py`); rules nothing checks yet are listed as GAP in every bake report, so a gap shows up without anyone asking. How close an area is to its real place is checked too (`scripts/check_real.py`: buildings, streets, twins both ways, kinds of place, the livable things the twin photos show, real names).
  - **Replaced:** its builder half replaced by "Hand-made, checked by programs; the base is frozen" (22 Oct); the checks half stands there.

## Ideas that were dropped
From `IDEAS.md`, word for word, with why they went (24 Oct).

- **Fabric types:** a district isn't one texture. The deep study finds 3–4 kinds of neighbourhood per district; the generator should lay them out in the real shares (busier along main roads, more open toward water and the city edge).
  - **Dropped:** a generator rule; the new map is hand-made ("The plan is hand-made; the models come from kits", 22 Oct). The real shares are still in `docs/studies/`.
- **Growing the map:** 1:2.5 (29 × 12 km) is the same layout rescaled, if Gidi ever needs more room.
  - **Dropped:** a rescale of the old 1:4 layout; the new map is 1:3 (22 Oct).

## Ìbùdó plan v2 (proposal, 19 Oct, awaiting the owner)
Ìbùdó as it is (`docs/hand/ibudo/district_as_is.png`) doesn't read as a district: the Oshodi things are packed into the north-east corner, the stadium sits alone in the north-west, and 4 km² of the same houses lie between them; the main roads only run round the edge. From a Google study of real Oshodi and Ojuelegba (screenshots in `~/gidi-data/research/google/ibudo/10-13`), proposal `docs/hand/ibudo/plan_v2_proposal.png`:
- two cores: Oshodi in the north-east (stays, beside the airport, as in life), Ojuelegba in the south-east (beside Saba, as in life): flyover junction, under-bridge market, stadium, a Shitta-type roundabout, a teaching hospital;
- three roads that cross the district: an expressway west from the Oshodi crossing (Airport Rd type), the Ojuelegba cross road (Itire Rd type, on to Saba), and a north-south link road (Ikorodu Rd type);
- five belts between them, each with its own grain: Mafoluku (densest), an Ajao-type estate (walled, bigger plots), Ilupeju-type industry, a Papa Ajao mix (houses and workshops, a spare-parts market), and a Surulere grid;
- several markets instead of one (an Arena-type set of sheds, a strip along the rail, spare parts, textiles, under the bridge), and walled institutions next to the Oshodi crossing (barracks, hospital, council).
Open question: real Oshodi has the railway running through the crossing; Gidi's rail only clips the corner. Moving it changes the whole city.

**Dropped:** a plan for the old Ìbùdó (the old map's Oshodi-Ojuelegba district); replaced by "Gidi is Lagos, miniaturised" (22 Oct): Oshodi and Surulere are on their real spots in the new map (32 Oshodi-Mafoluku, 13 Surulere). Its branch was removed on 24 Oct; the patch is in `~/gidi-data/to-delete/branches/`.
