# Words that mean the same thing here

`scripts/find.py` reads this list. A search for any word in a line also searches the line's other words, because the
same idea gets different names in different sessions. (Owner, 31 Oct: "I don't even know the words I can use"; the
wear "slider" they asked about turned out to be M_GidiPaint's Wear dial, built 19 Oct, and a search for "slider" missed it.)

When a search misses something that already existed, add the words that would have found it, in the same change.
Each line is a group, comma-separated. A word matches as a word start: `rust` also finds rusty and rusting.

- slider, dial, knob, parameter, setting, scalar
- wear, worn, rust, dirt, grime, dust, weather, fade, faded, aging, battered, wreck, damage, dent, scuff
- variation, variant, recipe, version, option, assortment
- vehicle, danfo, keke, okada, molue, korope, lorry, truck, bus, saloon, pickup, car
- paint, colour, color, livery, finish
- model, mesh, builder, build
- clean up, cleanup, retopology, retopo, remesh, decimate, simplify
- split, separate, detach, segment, modular, parts, pieces, kit
- meshy, tripo, rodin, hunyuan, AI model, generated model
- texture, material, shader
- light, lighting, sun, sky, grade, colour grade, tone
- street view, google maps, google earth, satellite, study shot
- check, test, verify, audit
- road, street, lane, carriageway, junction, roundabout, interchange
- building, house, footprint, roof, storey
- district, dNN, neighbourhood, area
- performance, fps, frame rate, budget, memory, 8 GB
