- District: 1.333 km² (Gidi), 1900 × 1250 m, the outline from the frame study (Shomolu-Bariga), named Ṣọmọlá (a

## How the lines were drawn

- As Agunda: each named street is its Overture segments merged per (name, kind), clipped to the border, pieces under
  8 m dropped; the same kinds, widths and values (Mùṣá's commonest for the kind, to read).
- **The expressways**, each one record on the middle of Overture's two carriageways, by hand:
  - **Ikorodu Road** (rd-7a9ab6b8, 661 m, 40 m): on from Mùṣá's rd-3079cryq end node at (13362.61, 5686.03), which
    lies 39 m inside Ṣọmọlá's border (Mùṣá's record already holds the road north of it), south to the border; 40 m
    and values as rd-3079cryq. Carriageways 3.9 m apart at 1:3.
  - **Third Mainland Bridge** (rd-e7a23019, 25 m) from the Gbagada interchange north-east to where it leaves the
    border, and **Third Axial Road** (rd-d6982e5f, 25 m) from the interchange south to the border; they meet on one
    point (14482.75, 5227.5), the middle of their two ends. Values as Ayégúnlẹ̀'s expressway rd-b2b8a5cc, widths to
    read.
  - **The Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway** (rd-f58919a3, 25 m) along the south border where it runs within 20 m of it
    (as Agunda's); about half its width lies over the land south of the border; that district takes it as held.
  - **The interchange's two ramps** (Overture, unnamed motorway) on their own lines as service roads, 433 and 546 m.
  - The local roads under them left out (more than 60% inside an expressway's carriageway): 29 pieces, among them
    the six slip roads Overture names after the expressway, two Ikorodu Road side pieces and an Alh Ariyo Street piece.
- **Held by Mùṣá**: a Majolate Street piece (16 m).
- **Pairs** (two records within 4 m along more than 70% of both) merged to their midline, the width the kind's plus
  the gap, to read: Finbarr Road 13.0, Jagun Molu Street 13.0, five unnamed (13.0, 10.0, 10.0, 9.5, 9.0).
- **Joins and the seam**: every road end the check found inside or within 1 m of another road was put on that road's
  line; Ṣọmọlá's points moved, never Mùṣá's: Mùṣá's Ikorodu Road record rd-3079cryq gained 14 vertices on its own line
  where Ṣọmọlá's streets meet it (16 -> 30 points, under 0.005 m off its line, its ends unchanged).
- **First points inside**: roads reversed or split at an inside vertex where both ends lay on or past the border (4
  splits).
- **By hand**: a lane (rd-2f2271f4) whose last 13 m ran on top of rd-fefd45a4, cut back to end on that road's point;
  two crossings given a shared point on both roads (Diya Street over an unnamed lane at the interchange, an unnamed
  main road over a lane).
- **Houses at the seam**: the road comes first: an unnamed 39 m main road (rd-5b312a6e) on the border runs where four
  of Mùṣá's houses stood; their fronts were cut back by its round outline + 0.1 m: b-14ca4599 399.6 -> 372.0 m²,
