## How the lines were drawn

- As Gbàgede: each named street is its Overture segments merged per (name, kind), clipped to the border, pieces
  under 8 m dropped; Mùṣá's commonest values for the kind, to read. Streets lying along a neighbour's road for more
  than 70% of their length left to the neighbour.
- **Pairs** (two records within 4 m along more than 70% of both) merged to their midline, the width the kind's plus
  the gap, to read: Central Avenue 9.0, Herbert Macaulay Way 13.05, Akoka Road 19.0, four unnamed (12.0, 11.0, 9.5,
  9.0).
- **Joins and the seam**: every road end the check found inside or within 1 m of another road was put on that road's
  line; Saba's points moved, never a neighbour's: three neighbour records gained vertices on their own lines where
  Saba's streets meet them (Sọ̀rọ̀lérè rd-955a0307 24 -> 26, Mùṣá rd-3079cryq 30 -> 34 and rd-i4klmw3w 5 -> 6
  points; none moved more than 0.004 m, their ends unchanged). One Saba lane's end (rd-1f54a510) put on Ṣọmọlá's node
  (13767.32, 6022.52).
- **First points inside**: roads reversed where their first point lay on or past the border, or split at an inside
  vertex where both ends did (2 splits).
- **By hand**:
  - **Murtala Muhammed Way** (rd-9e64ec60, 10.75 m): Overture's two carriageways had half-merged into four records
    that doubled back on themselves; drawn again as one record of 51 points from (13374.35, 6244.14) to
    (13562.81, 6679.07). It is a divided road: its width and median to read against the satellite.
  - **Trunk roads**: the draft's kinds have no trunk, so Overture's trunk roads came in as lanes; set by hand to
    main roads with Mùṣá's main-road values: Herbert Macaulay Way (rd-96ed58e3, 13.05 m as its pair) and Wright
    Street (rd-2f61115d, rd-3c2ed90d, 10.75 m).
  - **Ikorodu Road at the Mùṣá seam**: Saba's own Ikorodu Road record (rd-188b1a2a) ran 120 m about 6 m off the
    middle of Mùṣá's 40 m expressway, inside its carriageway: the road drawn twice. Cut back from 152 m to where it
    leaves the expressway (its first five points) and ended on the expressway's line; two side streets that ended on
    the cut part (rd-a4ed4412, rd-b036ed0f) now end on the expressway's line; a 6 m link (rd-cddbe2a1) between the
    two went.
  - Roads drawn twice cut back to where they leave the other, or gone where wholly on it.
- **Neighbours cut back for Saba's roads** (the board's test): Sọ̀rọ̀lérè's houses b-b2e4d25f (16.9 -> 11.9 m²) and
  b-18b20a3c (26.1 -> 25.7 m²); Ṣọmọlá's houses b-ce582c3e (25.2 -> 24.5 m²) and b-dc6ead10 (54.7 -> 45.1 m², clear of the round end of Saba's street rd-c586651e), its
  plot pl-sml02fa8 (484.7 -> 475.5 m²) and its wall wl-02fa8001 split around Saba's lane rd-ee2820ad (64.68 m ->
  13.2 m and wl-1bbc0baf 42.57 m).
