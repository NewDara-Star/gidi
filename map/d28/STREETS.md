# Gbàgede (district 28): the road plan and where each street comes from

Roads only, drawn on the new frame (1:3) at real widths. Real names live here only, never in the map. Houses and
places come in their own passes (NOW item 11).

## Count (Gbàgede hand pass 1, 26 Oct)

- District: 1.686 km² (Gidi), 1650 × 1933 m, the outline from the frame study (Gbagada), named Gbàgede (a
  sound-alike of Gbagada). Twelve specks of the frame grid (139-279 m², 1,809 m² in all) left out of the outline.
  One drawn neighbour: Ṣọmọlá (27), on the south, 2.5 km of shared border.
- Kept: **every street** inside the border, 1190 road records, 65.5 km: 3 motorways, 14 service roads (the
  interchanges' ramps and the expressways' slip roads), 22 main roads, 9 local mains, 203 home streets, 939 lanes
  (about 39 km/km²; Ṣọmọlá 50, Agunda 46; why it is sparser is not yet looked at).

## How the lines were drawn

- As Ṣọmọlá: each named street is its Overture segments merged per (name, kind), clipped to the border, pieces under
  8 m dropped; the same kinds, widths and values (Mùṣá's commonest for the kind, to read).
- **The expressways**, each one record on the middle of Overture's two carriageways, by hand, going on from
  Ṣọmọlá's ends:
  - **Ikorodu Road** (rd-9e02217d, 330 m, 40 m): from inside the west border south to Ṣọmọlá's rd-7a9ab6b8 end node
    (13349.61, 5025.21); it runs along the west border, about half its width over the land west of it, which the
    district drawn there takes as held. A shared point (13345.46, 4791.6) where the Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway
    leaves it. 40 m and values as rd-7a9ab6b8; carriageways 4.3 m apart at 1:3.
  - **The Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway** (rd-cbaebc6a, 658 m, 25 m): from that point on Ikorodu Road south-east to
    Ṣọmọlá's rd-f58919a3 start node (13862.27, 5175.12); values as rd-f58919a3.
  - **Third Axial Road** (rd-db0655fe, 1,549 m, 25 m): from the cloverleaf in the north-west notch south to
    Ṣọmọlá's rd-d6982e5f end node (14432.12, 4858.5); its first 136 m are the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway's piece inside
    the border, the one carriageway on through the cloverleaf. Values as rd-d6982e5f.
  - Ikorodu Road's north stretch crosses the notch's stepped border in five pieces of 7-33 m: left to the districts
    north and west of it.
  - **Ramps** (Overture, unnamed motorway) on their own lines as service roads: five of the cloverleaf (78-170 m;
    a sixth, 91 m, ran on the Third Axial Road's carriageway and went), two of the Ikorodu Road junction (46 and 48
    m) and one by Ṣọmọlá's expressway (46 m).
  - The local roads under them left out (more than 60% inside an expressway's carriageway): 37 pieces, among them
    four slip roads Overture names after the Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway. Overture's own "Ikorodu Road" main-road
    line (669 m) down the middle of the expressway's corridor went too: the expressway drawn a second time.
- **Pairs** (two records within 4 m along more than 70% of both) merged to their midline, the width the kind's plus
  the gap, to read: Ogudu Road 14.5 and 14.0 (two stretches), Diya Street 13.5, Agboyi Road 12.0, Salaudeen Akano
  Street 9.5, five unnamed (11.0, 11.0, 10.5, 10.5, 9.5).
- **Joins and the seam**: every road end the check found inside or within 1 m of another road was put on that road's
  line; Gbàgede's points moved, never Ṣọmọlá's: four Ṣọmọlá records gained vertices on their own lines where
  Gbàgede's streets meet them (rd-7a9ab6b8 16 -> 23, rd-f58919a3 21 -> 42, rd-399bb139 52 -> 53, rd-ccf86264
  40 -> 41 points; none moved more than 0.01 m, their ends unchanged).
- **First points inside**: roads reversed or split at an inside vertex where both ends lay on or past the border (6
  splits).
- **By hand**: roads drawn twice cut back to where they leave the other (an unnamed lane 67.6 -> 56.3 m); gone where
  wholly on another: an 8.5 m Ogudu Road stub, a 14.6 m lane, an 11.5 m expressway slip under rd-f58919a3, and a 37 m
  lane looping beside the expressway; ends put on the neighbour's own point where the check found them under 1 m
  apart (two of Ṣọmọlá's ends at (14149.0, 5132.96) and (14395.67, 5025.0), Ikorodu Road's (13354.09, 5240.9)), and
  the Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway's last point set back on rd-f58919a3's first.
