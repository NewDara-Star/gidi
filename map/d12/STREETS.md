# Agunda (district 12): the road plan and where each street comes from

Roads only, drawn on the new frame (1:3) at real widths. Real names live here only, never in the map. Houses and
places come in their own passes (NOW item 11).

## Count (Agunda hand pass 1, 26 Oct)

- District: 0.966 km² (Gidi), 1517 × 1483 m, the outline from the frame study (Aguda-Coker-Orile), named Agunda (a
  sound-alike of Aguda). The first district drawn against three drawn neighbours: Ìtìrí (14) on the north-west,
  Sọ̀rọ̀lérè (13) on the north-east, Ayégúnlẹ̀ (9) on the south.
- Kept: **every street** inside the border but what a neighbour already holds, 882 road records, 44.6 km: 1 motorway,
  11 service roads, 32 main roads, 22 local mains, 91 home streets, 725 lanes (about 46 km/km², as Ayégúnlẹ̀).
  Overture's raw piece count was not tallied this time.

## How the lines were drawn

- As Ayégúnlẹ̀: each named street is its Overture segments merged per (name, kind), clipped to the border, pieces
  under 8 m dropped; the same kinds, widths and values (Mùṣá's commonest for the kind, to read).
- **The Apapa-Oworonshoki Expressway** on the west border, rd-cd2a7637, 401 m, one 25 m motorway record with Ayégúnlẹ̀'s
  rd-b2b8a5cc's values: the midline of Overture's two carriageways (4.7 m apart at 1:3) where it runs within 20 m of
  the border; its first 70 m, outside the border, left to the western neighbour. About half its width lies over the
  frame land west of Agunda; that district takes it as held. The Lagos-Badagry Expressway pieces inside the south
  border are Ayégúnlẹ̀'s rd-b2b8a5cc (held, not drawn twice); 5 short Apapa Road pieces on the east border are left to
  Ijora-Iganmu-Costain (11).
- **Held by a neighbour** (more than 70% within 4 m of a neighbour's road): an Iganmu Road piece (49 m) and a College
  Road piece (61 m) left out.
- **Pairs** (two records within 4 m along more than 70% of both) merged to their midline, the width the kind's plus the
  gap, to read: Babs Animashaun Street 13.0, Iganmu Road 13.0 (two pieces), Eric Moore Road 12.5, Adelabu Street 15.5
  and 13.0, Ijesha Road 14.0, Alhaji Masha Road 13.5 (merged in two rounds; its width to read first), two unnamed lanes
  11.0 and 10.0.
- **Joins and the seam**: every road end the check found inside or within 1 m of another road was put on that road's
  line; Agunda's points moved, never a neighbour's: where a neighbour's road meets or crosses an Agunda road, the
  neighbour's road at most gained a vertex on its own line (d09 6 roads, d13 1, d14 2; none moved more than 0.006 m),
  and one Ìtìrí road (rd-594105d2) lost a point 0.25 m from its end where Agunda's road joins it.
- **First points inside**: 5 roads reversed; 14 seam roads with both ends on or past the border split in two at an
  inside vertex, both halves starting there, so neither join moved.
- **Drawn twice**: a 9 m service stub under the expressway, a border piece doubling Babs Animashaun Street (and its
  split half), and three lanes running alongside a main or service road within 3 m (rd-271aa1b3, rd-571688df,
  rd-b1a417ac) went; Adelabu Street's half rd-1e038c24 was cut back where it ran on Sọ̀rọ̀lérè's rd-88dbf4a8.
- **Houses at the seam**: the road comes first: four neighbour houses whose fronts stood in Agunda's streets were cut
  back from the carriageway: Ìtìrí's b-61409d21 (+0.05 m, 44.5 -> 38.8 m²) and b-717c0916 (+0.1 m, 169.3 -> 169.2
  m²); Sọ̀rọ̀lérè's b-79c140d5 (198.5 -> 177.7 m²) and Ìtìrí's b-e93643db (148.3 -> 129.7 m²), the last two by the
  road's round outline (+0.1 m), as the board draws it at ends and bends.
