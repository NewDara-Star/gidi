# Agunda (district 12): the buildings and their hand passes

## Hand pass 4 (26 Oct): the landmarks, walled with a gate each

Ayégúnlẹ̀'s hand pass 4 recipe on Overture's land use in Gidi metres: each ground less the carriageways along it is a
plot (the largest piece where a road cuts it), a wall 0.2 m inside its edge, open where houses close it, one 4 m gate
where it comes nearest a road (no road enters any). **3 plots**, 5 wall pieces, 3 gates: two factory compounds (882
and 965 m² after the carriageways, razor wire on the wall) and a school ground (588 m²) with its school place. No
house stands wholly inside one. Overture's land use names nothing else here (no named market, cemetery or
barracks), so Agunda's other grounds (its 44 schools' yards, the markets) are owed to the look pass, as the other
districts'.

The map picture (26 Oct, looked at): no land between districts left unclaimed (0 km²). Agunda reads lighter than its
neighbours: dense lanes, houses covering 15.2%, sparser in the north middle and the east lobe; filling up is the look
pass's (NOW), as the other districts'.

## Hand pass 3 (26 Oct): the real places

252 real places (Overture) have a kind the game uses (`working_bar.place_kind`, Mùṣá's hand pass 55 rules), by
Ayégúnlẹ̀'s hand pass 3 rules. **234 places**: church 135, school 44, clinic 20, buka 13, bar 6, fuel 4, garage 4,
mosque 3, bus_stop 2, police 2, bank 1.

- 121 on the house their point stands in or the nearest within 8 m; 18 real places shared a house and kind with
  another, so one place serves both. 113 houses carry a place link.
- 2 with no open ground within 15 m of their point, on their nearest house.
- 109 as 6 m squares at their point (the 2 bus stops, which stand on the street, and the rest in open ground with no
  house within 8 m), moved up to 13.5 m off roads and houses; their buildings are owed to the look pass, as Ìtìrí's.
- 2 by hand, where the rule left the place more than 10 m from its point: a church put on its house 9.3 m away
  (b-94181111; the rule's square stood 10.5 m off), and a buka whose point is in a main-road junction made a 5 m stall
  on the nearest open ground, 9.9 m off (the rule had put it on a house 35.2 m away).
- Only two bus stops are in Overture here and no motor park: Agunda's stops and parks are owed to a study of the map
  (NOW), as Ayégúnlẹ̀'s.

## Hand pass 2 (26 Oct): houses, storeys and kinds

**963 houses**, each with a kind and storeys; they cover 15.2% of the district (147,253 m²; Ayégúnlẹ̀ 16.0%, Sọ̀rọ̀lérè
15.0%, Ìtìrí 18.8%). The real cover was not measured here; filling up is owed to the look pass (NOW), as the other
districts'.

How, in Ayégúnlẹ̀'s order (no change to any builder; drafts on scratch copies of map/d12):

1. **Skeleton draft** on the 44 main roads, service roads and the expressway: 818 houses.
2. **The cut** (the road comes first): each house minus every carriageway of Agunda and its three drawn neighbours
   (Ayégúnlẹ̀, Sọ̀rọ̀lérè, Ìtìrí) + 0.05 m, and minus the neighbours' houses; 571 were cut (the note gives the m² before
   and after), 133 left under 12 m² or a third of themselves were not written: **685 kept** (12.7% of the district).
3. **Frontage** (`draft_buildings.py --frontage`, blocks still empty only): 307 drafted in 74 blocks (the table
   below), cut the same way: 9 cut, 28 not written, **279 kept**.
4. **The board's own test** (web/mapedit.js boardProblems, headless, Agunda with its three neighbours and Mùṣá): 91
   flagged (41 on a road, 50 overlaps), then 30, 4, 0: houses grazing a carriageway or a neighbour cut again to the
   carriageway + 0.1 m and a party wall 0.05 m off the larger neighbour or a neighbour district's house (82 houses,
   69.0 m² in all, the most 4.0 m²); the 29 where a lane ends at or bends by their wall cut to that road's
   round-ended carriageway + 0.1 m (33 cuts); one left too small by it (b-9b901685, by Ayégúnlẹ̀'s expressway) went.
5. **Storeys**: `draft_storeys.py` (its first Open Buildings tile has the pixels here, unlike Ayégúnlẹ̀) found
   sources for 519 and **read 456** (1: 78, 2: 306, 3: 56, 4: 16); 9 read 5 or more and were left;
   `draft_storeys_nearby.py` gave 325 more their neighbours' median (the 9 tall readings among them, now 2-3 storeys);
   the last 182 had no house with storeys within 30 m and were set by hand to their 5 nearest houses' median (Agunda's
   and its neighbours'), noted on each. Of the 9 tall readings, six are 29-53 m², too small to be 5 storeys; the three
   larger (103-194 m²) are owed a look on Street View (NOW). Storeys: 1: 173, 2: 650, 3: 120, 4: 20.
6. **Kinds** (`draft_kinds.py`): compound_house 587, face_me_bungalow 156, walkup 129, shop_under_flats 55, shack 28,
   shed 5, kiosk_row 3.
