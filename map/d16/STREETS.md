places come in their own passes (NOW item 11). The district's study note: docs/lagos-study/districts/d16.md.

## Count (Ìbùdó Mẹ́ta hand pass 1, 27 Oct)

- District: 0.475 km² (Gidi), the outline from the frame study (Ebute Metta, Oyingbo, Otto-Iddo), named Ìbùdó Mẹ́ta
  (its working name in docs/lagos-study/roads/NAMES.md, the owner to review). Two drawn neighbours: Saba (17) on the
  north-west, 531 m of shared border, and Makoro (19) on the north-east, 285 m; Costa (11) on the west, along the
  railway, is not drawn yet; the lagoon on the east.

  32 main roads, 14 local mains, 41 home streets, 213 lanes (about 41 km/km²; Makoro 28: the
  middle is the planned grid of 1867).
- **Not drawn here, owed by hand (NOW):** every motorway inside the border is a bridge or a ramp in Overture: Eko
  Bridge 689 m, Third Mainland Bridge 598 m and 1,231 m of unnamed ramps (both carriageways counted). They go in by
  hand as raised decks, as Mùṣá's Isolo Road bridge (rd-isodeck1), with Third Mainland Bridge drawn whole.
- **Drawn on the ground for now, raised in Overture:** Clinic Road (505 m, as Saba and Makoro drew their pieces),
  Carter Bridge (298 m), the Ijora Causeway (169 m), Federal Road (53 m), the Oyingbo Flyover Bridge (39 m) and about
  280 m of unnamed pieces. The same hand pass turns them into decks and ramps; houses must not stand under a ground
  road meanwhile, which the board's test holds.

## How the lines were drawn

- As Makoro: each named street is its Overture segments merged per (name, kind), clipped to the border, pieces under
  8 m dropped; Mùṣá's commonest values for the kind, to read.
- **Pairs**: Overture draws some streets twice, one line each way: one record on the midline (2 pairs).
- **Joins and the seam**: every road end the check found inside or within 1 m of another road was put on that road's
  line; Ìbùdó Mẹ́ta's points moved, never its neighbours': Saba's rd-8a424717 gained one vertex on its own line where
  rd-7b520257 meets it (5 -> 6 points, 0.0024 m off the old line, its ends unchanged); Makoro's file is unchanged.
- **First points inside**: roads reversed where their first point lay on or past the border, or split at an inside
  vertex where both ends did (rd-852b4cad, rd-db1964f1).
- **By hand, Clinic Road:** it is a dual carriageway along the north seam; Saba and Makoro each hold one carriageway
  as one main road. Ìbùdó Mẹ́ta keeps the named line that joins theirs and drops the other side where the two lines
  run 3-5 m apart (their 10.5-10.75 m surfaces overlap): rd-2161c858 and rd-1500d67d (an unnamed pair beside it),
  rd-fa588dfd and rd-51dc8c11 (beside rd-50124b2f and rd-7b520257), rd-4185f11b (beside rd-728902a8, and standing on
  Makoro's house b-e11c2478), and rd-ae7c6865 cut back to Makoro's rd-a30a6a75 where it ran beside it. rd-852b4cad
  ends on Makoro's road end at the three districts' corner. Saba's lane rd-9a781d14 ended on the dropped side: a 6 m
  lane, rd-6380f328, takes it to Clinic Road. Three vertices the steps had put on Saba's and Makoro's roads that no
  road met were taken back out.
- **By hand, small:** rd-6cef4690, a 14 m roundabout loop, went, and the three roads that started on it start at its
  centre (moved 1.6 m), as Makoro's rd-7d2d314f; rd-728902a8 overshot its junction and doubled back (one point
  dropped); rd-86a4cde8 (Carter Bridge) ran 2 m past its junction and back (two points dropped).
